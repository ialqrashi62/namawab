# 04 UX/UI Stitch — Pediatrics

## 1. Design Tokens
- **Palette**: child-friendly but professional, soft colors, clear alerts.
- **Typography**: same as Stitch clinical stations.
- **Layout**: 3-column station.

## 2. Components
- `PediatricVisitForm`: guardian info, chief complaint, vitals.
- `GrowthChart`: WHO percentile curves.
- `ImmunizationTimeline`: given and due vaccines.
- `WeightBasedDosePanel`: dose calculator.
- `RedFlagChecklist`: sepsis/dehydration/respiratory distress.

## 3. User Stories
- "As a pediatrician, I want growth percentiles plotted automatically against WHO charts."
- "As a pediatric nurse, I want immunization due alerts at every visit."

## 4. Wireframe Logic
- Left: pediatric patient list + guardian info.
- Center: visit/growth/immunization tabs.
- Right: alerts + dose calculator + next visit.

## 5. Accessibility
- RTL/LTR, high-contrast alerts, simple icons for caregivers.
