# 01_clinical_spec.md - NICU Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of NICU Ecosystem
Level III neonatal care for preterm and critically ill term neonates.

### Specialized Units:
- **Intensive Care:** Ventilated, very low birth weight, post-op.
- **High Dependency:** Growing preemies on CPAP/nasal ventilation.
- **Special Care:** Stable growing neonates preparing for discharge.
- **Mother-Baby Couplet:** Rooming-in and lactation support.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **NICU Flow:** Delivery → APGAR → Stabilization → NICU admit → Ventilation/TPN → Growth → Discharge.
- **KPIs:** NICU mortality, Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia (BPD) rate, Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) screening rate, breastfeeding at discharge, average length of stay.
- **Required Data:** APGAR scores, weight, head circumference, TPN composition, growth percentiles.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Newborn identification and parent-infant matching.
- **CBAHI:** Neonatal screening program (hearing, metabolic).
- **PDPL:** Encryption of neonatal imaging and family data.
