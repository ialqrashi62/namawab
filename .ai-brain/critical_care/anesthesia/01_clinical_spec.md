# 01_clinical_spec.md - Anesthesia Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Anesthesia Ecosystem
Perioperative anesthesia care from pre-op assessment to PACU handoff.

### Specialized Units:
- **Pre-Op Clinic:** ASA classification, airway assessment, optimization.
- **General Anesthesia:** Induction, maintenance, emergence.
- **Regional Anesthesia:** Spinal, epidural, peripheral nerve blocks.
- **Monitored Anesthesia Care (MAC):** Sedation for procedures.
- **Obstetric Anesthesia:** Labor analgesia and C-section anesthesia.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Perioperative Flow:** Pre-op ASA → Consent → Induction → Intra-op chart → Extubation → PACU.
- **KPIs:** Anesthesia-related mortality, PONV rate, unexpected ICU admission, awareness under anesthesia.
- **Required Data:** ASA class, Mallampati score, drug doses, vital trends every 5 min.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Surgical safety checklist and anesthesia documentation standards.
- **CBAHI:** Anesthesia consent and pre-op evaluation completeness.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of intra-op videos and anesthesia records.
