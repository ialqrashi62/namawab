# 01_clinical_spec.md - Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of ICU Ecosystem
Continuous monitoring and organ support for critically ill patients.

### Specialized Units:
- **Medical ICU:** Sepsis, respiratory failure, metabolic emergencies.
- **Surgical ICU:** Post-op major surgery, trauma, transplant.
- **Cardiac ICU:** Post-cardiac surgery, cardiogenic shock, mechanical circulatory support.
- **Neuro ICU:** Stroke, intracranial hemorrhage, status epilepticus.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Daily Flow:** Assessment → Devices/Vent → Labs → Sepsis Bundle → Daily Goals → Weaning.
- **KPIs:** ICU mortality, Ventilator-Associated Pneumonia (VAP) rate, Central Line-Associated Bloodstream Infection (CLABSI) rate, average length of stay, Sepsis bundle compliance.
- **Required Data:** MAP, lactate, SpO2, ventilator settings, sedation score, daily goals.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Daily goals, hand-hygiene compliance, VAP/CLABSI bundles.
- **CBAHI:** ICU staffing ratios and outcome reporting.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of continuous waveform data.
