# 01_clinical_spec.md - PACU (Post-Anesthesia Care Unit) Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of PACU Ecosystem
Recovery from anesthesia until discharge criteria are met.

### Specialized Units:
- **Phase 1 PACU:** Immediate post-op; intensive monitoring.
- **Phase 2 PACU:** Step-down; near discharge criteria.
- **Discharge Criteria:** Aldrete ≥ 9, pain controlled, no active bleeding.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Recovery Flow:** OR → PACU Admit → Aldrete q15min → Pain/Nausea Control → Discharge.
- **KPIs:** PACU length of stay, unplanned ICU admission, PONV rate, severe pain rate.
- **Required Data:** Aldrete score, vitals, pain score (NRS), nausea score.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Discharge criteria documentation and PACU staffing standards.
- **CBAHI:** PACU outcomes reporting.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of PACU records and any post-op imaging.
