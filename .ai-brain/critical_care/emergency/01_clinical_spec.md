# 01_clinical_spec.md - Emergency Department (ER) Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of ER Ecosystem
Acute care for life-threatening and urgent conditions across all ages.

### Specialized Units:
- **Triage Zone:** ESI 1-5 sorting with rapid registration.
- **Resuscitation Bay:** ESI 1 / trauma / cardiac arrest.
- **Acute Care Beds:** ESI 2-3 monitoring and treatment.
- **Fast-Track:** ESI 4-5 minor complaints.
- **Observation Unit:** Short-stay monitoring up to 24h.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Triage Flow:** Quick Reg → ESI → Bed → Workup → Treatment → Disposition.
- **KPIs:** Door-to-Provider time, Door-to-Disposition time, Left-Without-Being-Seen (LWBS) rate, Mortality in ER.
- **Required Data:** ESI level, chief complaint, vitals, allergies, time stamps.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** ATLS/ACLS compliance and time-to-treatment documentation.
- **CBAHI:** Emergency preparedness and mass casualty plans.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of triage notes and ER imaging.
