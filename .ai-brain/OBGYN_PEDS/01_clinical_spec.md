# 01 Clinical Spec — OB/GYN & Pediatrics Suite
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Maternal, neonatal, and child health across obstetrics, gynecology, general pediatrics, and pediatric subspecialties.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - Antenatal care and risk stratification.
  - Partogram and labor monitoring.
  - Delivery documentation and APGAR scoring.
  - Postpartum and newborn care.
  - Pediatric growth, immunization, and subspecialty referrals.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Pregnancy confirmation / child registration → 2. Antenatal/pediatric visits → 3. Labor/delivery or acute care → 4. Postpartum/newborn → 5. Pediatric follow-up → 6. Transition to adult care.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Gestational age and EDD calculation.
- Partogram alerts for prolonged labor.
- Growth percentile plotting.
- Immunization due alerts.
- High-risk pregnancy flags.

## 4. Integration Points
- CPOE, LIS, RIS, pharmacy.
- NICU and pediatric subspecialties.
- Billing and NPHIES eligibility.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Mother-baby identity match before discharge.
- Guardian consent for pediatric procedures.
- Blood availability for high-risk deliveries.
- Weight-based pediatric dosing.
