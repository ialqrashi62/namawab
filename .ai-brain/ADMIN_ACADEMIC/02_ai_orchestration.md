# 02_ai_orchestration.md - Administrative AI Orchestration
**Expert: Lead AI Engineer**

## 1. AI Architecture: The Governance Brain
AI-driven compliance monitoring and academic research synthesis.

### A. VectorMine Implementation
- **Vector Database:** PGVector storing embeddings of JCI/ISO standards and medical research papers.
- **Retrieval:** Semantic search to match current hospital la-practices with "Compliance Requirements".

## 2. LangChain Orchestration
- **Chain 1: Compliance Gap Analyzer:** `Input (Internal Audit Report) -> Vector Search (JCI Standards) -> LLM (Analysis) -> Output (Prioritized Fix Plan)`.
- **Chain 2: Research Synthesizer:** `Input (Clinical Trial Data) -> Vector Search (Similar Studies) -> LLM (Analysis) -> Output (Draft Research Paper/Summary)`.

## 3. Guardrails
- **Verification:** AI-suggested compliance fixes must be verified by the Quality Officer.
- **Observability:** Tracking "Audit Accuracy" vs "AI Suggestions".
