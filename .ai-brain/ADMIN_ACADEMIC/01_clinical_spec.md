# 01_clinical_spec.md - Administrative & Academic Specifications
**Expert: CEO & Chief Academic Officer**

## 1. Scope of Administrative & Academic Ecosystem
Governance, quality assurance, and medical education.

### Specialized Units:
- **Executive Administration:** CEO, CFO, COO, and Medical Staff Council.
- **Quality & Accreditation:** TQM, JCI/ISO/CAP/CBAHI compliance, and Risk Management.
- **Education & Research:** Residency programs, Clinical Trials (CRC), and IRB (Institutional Review Board).
- **HR & Admin:** Medical HR, Legal Affairs, and Public Relations.

## 2. Governance Protocols & KPIs
- **Quality Cycle:** Audit $\rightarrow$ Gap Analysis $\rightarrow$ Corrective Action (CAPA) $\rightarrow$ Re-audit.
- **Research Flow:** IRB Submission $\rightarrow$ Ethical Approval $\rightarrow$ Patient Enrollment $\rightarrow$ Data Collection.
- **KPIs:** Accreditation status, Research publication count, and Employee turnover rate.

## 3. Compliance
- **Saudi Law:** ZATCA Phase 2 compliance for all financial transactions.
- **PDPL:** Extreme encryption for research data and employee PII.
- **JCI:** Strict adherence to "Credentialing & Privileging" for all medical staff.
