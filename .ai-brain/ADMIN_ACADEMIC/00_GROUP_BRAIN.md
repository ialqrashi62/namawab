## 🎯 Group-Level Clinical Authority (CMO)

### Executive Administration

**Hospital governance structure:**
- Board of Directors / Trustees (ultimate authority)
- CEO (operational head)
- Executive team: CMO, CNO, CFO, COO
- Department heads
- Front-line staff

**Medical Staff Organization (MSO):**
- Medical Staff President
- Department chairs
- Section chiefs
- Medical Executive Committee (MEC)
- Credentials Committee
- Quality Committee
- Medical Ethics Committee

**Credentialing & Privileging (jointly Quality + Medical Staff):**
- Initial credentialing (medical school, residency, fellowship, board cert, license, DEA, references)
- Re-credentialing (typically q2y)
- Privileging (specific procedures, scope of practice)
- Proctoring (new procedures, peer review)
- OPPE (Ongoing Professional Practice Evaluation) — continuous
- FPPE (Focused Professional Practice Evaluation) — when concerns arise

**Ethics Committee:**
- End-of-life decisions
- Withdrawal of life support
- DNR / DNI / AND orders
- Brain death determination
- Conflict resolution
- Resource allocation
- Clinical research ethics
- Patient rights violations

### Quality & Accreditation

**Quality frameworks:**
- JCI (Joint Commission International) — 7th edition
- CBAHI (Saudi Central Board for Accreditation of Healthcare Institutions)
- ISO 9001:2015 (QMS)
- ISO 15189 (Medical Labs)
- ISO 13485 (Medical Devices)
- ISO 27001 (Information Security)
- CAP (College of American Pathologists)
- CARF (Rehab)
- ACI (Accreditation Canada)
- Magnet (Nursing Excellence)

**JCI 7th edition key chapters:**
- IPSG (International Patient Safety Goals)
- ACC (Access to Care & Continuity of Care)
- PFR (Patient & Family Rights)
- COP (Care of Patients)
- MMU (Medication Management & Use)
- QPS (Quality & Patient Safety)
- PCI (Prevention & Control of Infections)
- FMS (Facility Management & Safety)
- SQE (Staff Qualification & Education)
- MOI (Management of Information)
- MCI (Management of Communication)
- GLD (Governance, Leadership, Direction)
- SQE (continued)

**CBAHI (KSA National):**
- Required for all hospitals
- Aligned with JCI
- Has KSA-specific standards (e.g. MOH requirements)

**Quality improvement tools:**
- PDSA (Plan-Do-Study-Act) cycle
- Root cause analysis (RCA)
- Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA)
- Lean Six Sigma
- Statistical process control (SPC)
- Run charts, control charts
- Pareto analysis
- Fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram
- 5 Whys

**Quality metrics (clinical):**
- Door-to-balloon time
- Central line-associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI)
- Catheter-associated UTI (CAUTI)
- Ventilator-associated pneumonia (VAP)
- Surgical site infection (SSI)
- Hospital-acquired pressure injury
- Fall rate
- Readmission rate (30d)
- Mortality (observed/expected ratio)
- Length of stay
- Patient satisfaction (HCAHPS)

**Patient safety event classification:**
- Near miss
- No harm
- Mild harm
- Moderate harm
- Severe harm
- Death (Sentinel event)

**Sentinel events (Joint Commission list):**
- Unanticipated death
- Wrong-site, wrong-procedure, wrong-patient surgery
- Unintended retained foreign object
- Severe neonatal hyperbilirubinemia
- Suicide (inpatient)
- Unintended transfusion of incompatible blood
- Falls with serious injury
- Severe medication error

**Risk management:**
- Clinical risk assessment
- Insurance (medical liability)
- Incident management
- Claims management
- Disclosure and apology
- Root cause analysis
- Proactive risk assessment (FMEA)

### Education & Research

**Undergraduate medical education:**
- Medical school (5-6 years)

- Residency (3-7 years, depending on specialty)

Table credentials {
  id serial [pk]
  tenant_id integer [not null]
  user_id integer [not null]
  credential_type varchar(50)  // 'medical_license', 'board_cert', 'dea', 'specialty_cert', 'fellowship'
  credential_name varchar(255)
  issuing_authority varchar(255)
  credential_number varchar(100)
  issue_date date
  expiration_date date
  verified_by_user_id integer
  verified_at timestamptz
  document_url text  // encrypted
  status varchar(20)  // 'active', 'expired', 'pending_renewal', 'suspended'
  created_at timestamptz
  FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY
}

Table privileges {
  id serial [pk]
  tenant_id integer [not null]
  user_id integer [not null]
  privilege_name varchar(255)  // 'cardiology_consult', 'echocardiography', 'PCI', 'TAVR', 'cardiac_surgery'
