# 01_clinical_spec.md - Radiation Oncology & Therapeutics Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Oncology Therapeutics
Advanced delivery of radiation and chemotherapy to treat malignant tumors.

### Specialized Units:
- **Radiation Oncology:** IMRT, SRS, Gamma Knife, CyberKnife, and Proton Therapy.
- **Brachytherapy:** Internal radiation (Interstitial/Intracavitary).
- **Clinical Pharmacy:** Chemotherapy compounding, TDM (Therapeutic Drug Monitoring).

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Radiation Flow:** Simulation $\rightarrow$ Planning (Dosimetry) $\rightarrow$ Delivery $\rightarrow$ Weekly Review.
- **KPIs:** Target volume coverage, OAR (Organs at Risk) sparing, and Toxicity grade.
- **Required Data:** Gray (Gy) dose, Fractionation schedule, and Isotope activity.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict radiation safety/shielding and isotope disposal protocols.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of radiation dose maps and treatment plans.
