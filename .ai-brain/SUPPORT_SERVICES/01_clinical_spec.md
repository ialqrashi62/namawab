# 01_clinical_spec.md - Support & Nursing Services Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO) & Chief Nursing Officer (CNO)**

## 1. Scope of Support Services Ecosystem
The operational backbone of the hospital, ensuring patient safety, nutrition, and psychosocial support.

### Specialized Units:
- **Nursing Administration:** Ward management, nursing ratios, and specialized nursing (ICU, ER, OBGYN).
- **Clinical Nutrition:** TPN/Enteral nutrition, therapeutic diets, and central kitchen management.
- **Psychosocial Services:** Medical social work, patient advocacy, and employee assistance.
- **Logistics & Technical:** Biomedical engineering (Calibration), Health IT (EMR/PACS), and Medical Translation.
- **Safety & Security:** Hospital security, Occupational Health, and Disaster Management.

## 2. Operational Protocols & KPIs
- **Nursing Flow:** Shift Handover $\rightarrow$ Medication Administration (MAR) $\rightarrow$ Vital Monitoring $\rightarrow$ Discharge Planning.
- **Nutrition Flow:** Diet Order $\rightarrow$ Meal Prep $\rightarrow$ Delivery $\rightarrow$ Intake Monitoring.
- **KPIs:** Nursing hours per patient day (NHPPD), Medication error rate, and Equipment uptime (Biomed).

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict adherence to "Patient Identification" and "Medication Safety" goals.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of social work case notes and employee health records.
