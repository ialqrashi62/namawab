# 05 Compliance & Security — Support Services & Operations

## 1. Compliance
- CBAHI operations and facility management standards.
- WHO medical waste guidelines / MWAN Saudi.
- SFDA medical device regulations.
- Saudi PDPL for operational data.

## 2. Security
- Service-specific roles (`cssd_tech`, `dietary_officer`, `porter`, `biomedical_engineer`, `waste_officer`), `requireTenantScope`.
- Restricted access to mortuary and social service notes.
- PHI vault for device manuals and waste certificates.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for CSSD release, device maintenance, waste disposal, and transport handoffs.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Sterile set release only after cycle completion.
- Life-support device return-to-service after valid calibration.
- Waste disposal only with matched manifest and certificate.

## 5. Data Retention
- Operational records retained per policy; audit logs 7+ years.
