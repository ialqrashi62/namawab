## 🎯 Group-Level Clinical Authority (CMO)

### Nursing Universal Workflow

**Nursing Process (ADPIE):**
1. **Assessment** — data collection (subjective + objective)
2. **Diagnosis** — NANDA nursing diagnosis
3. **Planning** — goals, outcomes
4. **Implementation** — interventions
5. **Evaluation** — outcome reassessment

**Documentation formats:**
- SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
- SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) for handover
- PIE (Problem, Intervention, Evaluation)
- DAR (Data, Action, Response)

**Vital signs (per shift or more frequent):**
- T, HR, RR, BP, SpO2
- Pain score (0-10)
- I/O
- Weight (daily in ICU, weekly otherwise)
- GCS (if altered)
- Glucose (PRN or scheduled)

**Medication administration (5 Rights + 3):**
- Right patient
- Right drug
- Right dose
- Right route
- Right time
- Right documentation
- Right reason
- Right response
- Right education
- Right to refuse

**Patient safety goals (Joint Commission International Patient Safety Goals):**
1. Identify patients correctly (2 identifiers)
2. Improve effective communication
3. Improve the safety of high-alert medications
4. Ensure safe surgery (time-out, site marking)
5. Reduce the risk of patient falls
6. Reduce the risk of healthcare-associated infections
7. Reduce the risk of pressure injuries
8. Reduce the risk of suicide

**Braden Scale (pressure injury risk):**

| Subscale | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sensory perception | Limited | Very limited | Slightly limited | No impairment |
| Moisture | Constantly moist | Often moist | Occasionally moist | Rarely moist |
| Activity | Bedfast | Chairfast | Walks occasionally | Walks frequently |
| Mobility | Completely immobile | Very limited | Slightly limited | No limitation |
| Nutrition | Very poor | Probably inadequate | Adequate | Excellent |
| Friction/Shear | Problem | Potential problem | No apparent problem | — |

Total: 6-23. < 14 = at risk; < 10 = very high risk.

**Morse Fall Scale:**

| Item | Score |
|---|---|
| History of falling (yes/no) | 25 / 0 |
| Secondary diagnosis (yes/no) | 15 / 0 |
| Ambulatory aid (none/bedrest/nurse/crutches/walker/furniture) | 0/0/15/15/15/15/30 (furniture) |
| IV/Heparin lock (yes/no) | 20 / 0 |
| Gait (normal/bedrest/wheelchair/weak/impaired) | 0/0/0/10/20 |
| Mental status (oriented/forgets limitations) | 0/15 |

Total: 0-125. ≥ 45 = high fall risk.

### Clinical Nutrition

**Nutrition assessment:**
- Anthropometric: weight, height, BMI, weight history
- Biochemical: albumin, prealbumin, transferrin, glucose, electrolytes
- Clinical: physical exam, GI symptoms
- Dietary: 24h recall, food frequency
- Functional: hand grip strength, swallow eval

**BMI classification (WHO):**
- < 18.5: underweight
- 18.5-24.9: normal
- 25-29.9: overweight
- 30-34.9: obese class I
- 35-39.9: obese class II
- ≥ 40: obese class III (morbid)

**Caloric needs (Harris-Benedict):**
- Men: BMR = 66 + (13.75 × weight_kg) + (5 × height_cm) - (6.75 × age)
- Women: BMR = 655 + (9.56 × weight_kg) + (1.85 × height_cm) - (4.68 × age)
- Total = BMR × activity factor (1.2-1.9)

**Protein needs:**
- Healthy adult: 0.8 g/kg/day
- Critically ill: 1.2-2.0 g/kg/day
- Burns: 1.5-2.5 g/kg/day
- HD: 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day
- PD: 1.0-1.5 g/kg/day
- Elderly at risk: 1.2-1.5 g/kg/day

**TPN (Total Parenteral Nutrition):**
- Indications: non-functioning GI > 7-10d, severe malabsorption, high-output fistula, severe pancreatitis
- Components: dextrose (3.4 kcal/g), amino acids (4 kcal/g), lipids (10 kcal/g, 20% emulsion), electrolytes, trace elements, vitamins
- Access: central (osm > 900 mOsm) vs peripheral
- Monitoring: glucose q6h, electrolytes daily, LFTs weekly, triglycerides weekly

**Enteral nutrition:**
- NG (nasogastric) — < 4 weeks
- PEG/PEJ (percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy/jejunostomy) — > 4 weeks
- Nasojejunal (post-pyloric) — high aspiration risk
- Continuous vs bolus
- Starting rate: 20-30 mL/h, advance q4-6h
- Goal: 60-80 mL/h (or meets 60% of caloric needs within 48-72h)

**Disease-specific diets:**

| Disease | Diet |
|---|---|
| Diabetes | Carb-controlled, low glycemic, fiber 25-30g |
| Renal (predialysis) | 0.6-0.8 g/kg protein, low K, low PO4, low Na |
| Renal (dialysis) | 1.0-1.2 g/kg protein, K restriction (HD), fluid restriction |
| Heart failure | 2g Na, fluid restriction 1.5-2L |
| HTN | DASH, low Na (< 2.3g), high K, high Ca |
| Celiac | Gluten-free |
| Lactose intolerance | Lactose-free or lactase enzyme |
| PKU | Low phenylalanine, special formula |
| MSUD | Low BCAA, special formula |
| Galactosemia | Lactose-free |
| Crohn's | Low-residue during flare, high protein |
| Dysphagia | IDDSI levels (per assessment) |
