# 01_clinical_spec.md - Rehabilitation & Physical Therapy Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Rehabilitation Ecosystem
Holistic recovery and functional restoration of patients after acute illness or injury.

### Specialized Units:
- **Physical Therapy:** Electrotherapy, Hydrotherapy, and Manual Therapy.
- **Occupational Therapy:** ADL (Activities of Daily Living) training and Sensory Integration.
- **Speech & Swallowing Therapy:** Dysphagia management and Speech disorders.
- **Specialized Rehab:** Spinal Cord Injury, Pediatric Rehab, and Prosthetics/Orthotics.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Rehab Cycle:** Initial Assessment $\rightarrow$ Goal Setting $\rightarrow$ Intervention $\rightarrow$ Re-evaluation.
- **KPIs:** ROM (Range of Motion) improvement, Barthel Index (ADL), and Patient Quality of Life (QoL).
- **Required Data:** Muscle strength (MMT), Joint angles, and Swallowing safety levels.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict adherence to patient safety during hydrotherapy and electrotherapy.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of rehabilitation progress videos and photos.
