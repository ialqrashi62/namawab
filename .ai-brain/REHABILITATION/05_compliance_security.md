# 05 Compliance & Security — Rehabilitation & Physical Medicine

## 1. Compliance
- CBAHI rehabilitation service standards.
- Saudi PDPL for patient functional data and photos.
- JCI continuity of care and discharge planning.

## 2. Security
- `requireRole('physiatrist')` / `requireRole('therapist')`, `requireTenantScope`.
- Therapy photos/videos in PHI vault.
- Restricted access to sensitive functional and psychosocial notes.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for assessments, sessions, goals, and discharge plans.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Physician order required before therapy initiation.
- Contraindication check (fracture stability, DVT, vital signs).
- Goal review every 2 weeks.

## 5. Data Retention
- Therapy records retained per policy; audit logs 7+ years.
