## B. Workflow — Office Visit Flow

```
Patient arrives → Check-in (PDPL consent + intake)
   ↓
Triage by chief complaint
   ↓
Room assignment
   ↓
Provider visit: HPI + ROS + Exam + Decision (meds, scope, labs)
   ↓
AI Co-pilot pane: shows DDx + red flags + drug alerts (citation-first)
   ↓
Provider accepts/edits/rejects
   ↓
Orders placed (Endoscopy, labs, imaging, consults)
   ↓
Procedure scheduled
   ↓
Pre-procedure prep (cleansing, NPO, anticoag hold)
   ↓
Procedure (endoscopy or otherwise)
   ↓
Recovery (PACU or chair)
   ↓
Procedure note (auto-drafted via scribe mode)
   ↓
Pathology if biopsy → result in 3-7 days
   ↓
Follow-up visit (in-person or telehealth)
   ↓
Disposition: continue meds / step-up therapy / surgical referral
```

## C. Workflow — Acute GI Bleed (Emergency)

```
ER triage → SIRS/sepsis screen
   ↓
STAT labs (CBC, coags, type & cross, BUN, lactate)
   ↓
IV access (2 large bore) + IV fluids
   ↓
Hold antiplatelets / anticoagulants
   ↓
Prokinetic + IV PPI
   ↓
STAT EGD (within 12h for upper, 24h for lower)
   ↓
Hemostasis (clip / band / thermal / injection)
   ↓
ICU/ward disposition based on severity
   ↓
HP eradication if ulcer + H.pylori+
   ↓
Discharge with secondary prevention
```

## D. Workflow — IBD Treat-to-Target

```
DX → classify (Montreal: age, location, behavior)
   ↓
Stratify risk (age<40, extensive, severe endoscopic, perianal)
   ↓
Induction therapy (steroids / biologic depending)
   ↓
Week 12 assessment (clinical + CRP + calprotectin)
   ↓
If non-response → step-up (different biologic, JAK)
   ↓
Maintenance + monitoring q3-6mo
   ↓
Annual screening (DRE, colonoscopy 8-10y after DX)
   ↓
Vaccination update (live vaccines if on biologic)
```
