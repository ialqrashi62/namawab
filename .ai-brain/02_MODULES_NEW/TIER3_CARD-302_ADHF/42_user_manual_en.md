# User Manual — Advanced Heart Failure (TIER3_CARD-302_ADHF)

> **Audience**: HF cardiologists, nurses, pharmacists
> **Version**: 1.0.0

---

## 1. Introduction

The AHF Center provides comprehensive care for all stages of heart failure: HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF, with focus on the 4-pillar GDMT and advanced therapies (LVAD, transplant, palliative).

## 2. GDMT 4 Pillars (Class I, AHA/ACC 2022)

### 1. ARNI (Sacubitril/Valsartan)
- **Start**: 24/26 mg BID
- **Target**: 97/103 mg BID
- **Washout**: 36 hours from ACEi

### 2. Beta-Blocker
- **Bisoprolol** 10 mg
- **Carvedilol** 25 mg BID
- **Metoprolol succinate** 200 mg

### 3. MRA
- **Spironolactone** 25 mg daily
- **Eplerenone** 50 mg

### 4. SGLT2i
- **Dapagliflozin** 10 mg
- **Empagliflozin** 10 mg

## 3. Workflows

### 3.1 GDMT Optimization
1. **Eligibility check**: 4 pillars with contraindications
2. **Initiation**: focus on 1 pillar every 2 weeks
3. **Follow-up**: monitor BP, K+, GFR

### 3.2 Acute Decompensation
1. Navigate to **Menu → AHF Center**
2. Click **🚨 Admit**
3. Enter weight, IV diuretic, urine output

### 3.3 LVAD
1. Navigate to **LVAD**
2. Enter device parameters (Speed, Power, Flow)
3. Check for complications

### 3.4 Transplant
1. Navigate to **Transplant**
2. Enter Status (1A/1B/2)
3. Track Biopsies

## 6. FAQ

### Q: When to start ARNI?
**A**: After 36-hour washout from ACEi, with SBP ≥ 100, K+ < 5.0, GFR ≥ 30.

### Q: When to consider LVAD?
**A**: Stage D HF with persistent symptoms on optimal GDMT, INTERMACS 1-3.

### Q: When to refer for transplant?
**A**: After full evaluation, SCOT Listing Status 1A/1B/2.
