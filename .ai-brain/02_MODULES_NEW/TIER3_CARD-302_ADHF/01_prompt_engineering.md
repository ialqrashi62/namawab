# CARD-302_ADHF — Advanced Heart Failure

## Module ID
`TIER3_CARD-302_ADHF` — Advanced Heart Failure (AHF) Center

## Specialty
Cardiovascular — Advanced Heart Failure, Cardiomyopathy, Mechanical Circulatory Support, Heart Transplantation

## Clinical Scope
- **HFrEF** — Heart Failure with reduced EF (≤40%)
- **HFmrEF** — Mid-range EF (41-49%)
- **HFpEF** — Heart Failure with preserved EF (≥50%)
- **Acute Decompensated HF (ADHF)** — Hospitalization
- **Cardiogenic shock** — Temporary MCS (IABP, Impella, ECMO, LVAD)
- **Heart Transplantation** — Evaluation + listing + post-transplant care
- **LVAD** — Destination therapy + bridge to transplant
- **Cardiac Rehab** — Phase I-III
- **Palliative Care** — Advanced HF

## Scoring Systems
- **NYHA Class** — I-IV functional capacity
- **ACC/AHA Stage** — A, B, C, D
- **LVEF** — 0-100%
- **NT-proBNP** — 0-35000 pg/mL
- **MAGGIC Score** — 1-year mortality risk
- **Seattle Heart Failure Model** — 1, 2, 5-year survival
- **INTERMACS** — 1-7 profile for MCS
- **HeartMate II/III Risk Score** — LVAD mortality
- **ISHLT Registry Risk** — Transplant outcome

## Key Workflows
1. **Initial HF Assessment** — Echo, BNP, ECG, MRI
2. **GDMT Optimization** — 4-pillar therapy (ARNI, BB, MRA, SGLT2i)
3. **Acute Decompensation** — IV diuresis, vasoactives
4. **Cardiogenic Shock** — Multidisciplinary shock team, MCS
5. **LVAD Implantation** — Pre-op, surgery, post-op
6. **Heart Transplant** — Listing, donor matching, post-op
7. **End-of-Life Care** — Palliative, hospice

## Compliance
- **AHA/ACC/HFSA 2022** — HF Guidelines
- **ISHLT 2023** — Transplant listing
- **CBAHI** — Advanced HF center
- **NPHIES** — HF bundle
- **MoH** — Saudi cardiac center designation
- **SCOT** — Saudi Center for Organ Transplantation

## Approval Gate
- **Advanced HF Cardiologist** — LVAD/transplant decision
- **Cardiac Surgeon** — LVAD/Transplant
- **Cardiothoracic Anesthesia** — Peri-op
- **Infectious Disease** — Pre/post-transplant
- **Nephrology** — Cardio-renal syndrome
- **Pharmacy** — Immunosuppression dosing
- **Palliative Care** — Advanced directives
