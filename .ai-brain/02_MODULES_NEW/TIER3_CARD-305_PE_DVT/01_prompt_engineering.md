# TIER3_CARD-305_PE_DVT — PE/DVT Response Team

## Module ID
`TIER3_CARD-305_PE_DVT` — Pulmonary Embolism & Deep Vein Thrombosis Response

## Specialty
Acute PE/DVT response + CTEPH management

## Clinical Scope
- **Acute PE** — Massive / Submassive / Low-risk stratification
- **DVT** — Provoked / Unprovoked / Recurrent
- **PERT activation** — Pulmonary Embolism Response Team
- **Systemic thrombolysis** — Alteplase, Tenecteplase
- **Catheter-directed thrombolysis** — EKOS
- **Mechanical thrombectomy** — FlowTriever, Indigo
- **IVC filter** — Optional / Retrievable
- **Anticoagulation** — DOAC, Warfarin, LMWH
- **CTEPH** — Chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension
- **PEA surgery** — Pulmonary endarterectomy

## Scoring
- **PE Severity Index (PESI)** — Mortality
- **Simplified PESI (sPESI)** — Risk strat
- **RV/LV ratio** — Echo/CT
- **Troponin / BNP** — Risk
- **Wells DVT** — Pre-test probability
- **CHA2DS2-VASc** — AF recurrence
- **HAS-BLED** — Bleeding
- **Modified Borg** — Dyspnea
