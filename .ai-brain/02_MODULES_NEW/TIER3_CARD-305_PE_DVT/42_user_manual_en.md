# User Manual — PE/DVT Response Team (TIER3_CARD-305_PE_DVT)

> **Audience**: ER physicians, pulmonologists, cardiologists
> **Version**: 1.0.0

---

## 1. Introduction

The PE/DVT Response Team (PERT) is a multidisciplinary team for rapid management of acute pulmonary embolism and deep vein thrombosis.

## 2. PERT Activation

Activate PERT for:
- **Massive PE** (SBP <90 + RV dysfunction)
- **Submassive PE with deterioration**
- **Cardiac arrest from suspected PE**

## 3. Severity Classification

| Severity | Criteria | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Massive** | SBP <90 + RV failure | Thrombolysis + PERT |
| **Intermediate-High** | RV dysfunction + biomarker + | PERT + close monitoring |
| **Intermediate-Low** | RV dysfunction OR biomarker | Anticoagulation |
| **Low Risk** | No RV dysfunction, normal biomarker | Early discharge on DOAC |

## 4. Thrombolysis

- **Alteplase 100mg IV over 2 hours**
- **Tenecteplase single bolus (preferred)**
- Contraindications: Recent surgery/stroke, active bleeding, ICH history

## 5. Anticoagulation

- **DOAC first-line** (Apixaban 5mg BID)
- **LMWH** for cancer (gastric/pancreatic) or pregnancy
- **Warfarin** if DOAC contraindicated
- **Duration**: 3-6 months minimum

## 6. IVC Filter

- **Acute contraindication to anticoagulation**
- **Recurrent PE on anticoagulation**
- **Retrievable** within 30 days

## 7. CDT / Thrombectomy

- **Catheter-Directed Therapy (EKOS)**: Submassive with systemic contraindication
- **Mechanical Thrombectomy (FlowTriever)**: Massive with systemic contraindication

## 8. CTEPH Workup

- Persistent dyspnea >3 months after PE
- V/Q scan, right heart cath
- PEA evaluation if eligible

## 9. FAQ

### Q: When to thrombolyse?
**A**: Massive PE (SBP <90 + RV dysfunction). Avoid in recent surgery/stroke.

### Q: When DOAC vs LMWH?
**A**: DOAC first-line. LMWH for cancer (gastric/pancreatic), pregnancy.

### Q: When IVC filter?
**A**: Contraindication to anticoagulation or recurrent PE on anticoagulation.
