## Document control

- All P&P in DOC-CONTROL system
- Version + approval workflow
- Read acknowledgement
- Annual review

## Records

- 7+ year retention
- Indexed by patient + procedure + date
- Hash-chained audit log
- Encrypted at rest

## Internal audit (quarterly)

- Process audit: cath lab, echo, stress, device clinic, HF clinic
- Product audit: cath reports, device interrogation, GDMT optimization
- System audit: full QMS review

## Customer satisfaction

- Outpatient: post-visit survey
- Inpatient: discharge survey
- 30-day follow-up call
- Quarterly report

## Corrective action

- Nonconformity logged
- Root cause (5 Whys)
- Corrective action + owner + due date
- Verification of effectiveness
- Close-out sign-off
