# User Manual — Robotic CV Surgery (TIER3_CARD-304_ROBOTIC)

> **Audience**: Cardiac surgeons, interventional cardiologists, cardiac anesthesiologists
> **Version**: 1.0.0

---

## 1. Introduction

The Robotic CV Surgery Center provides minimally invasive procedures using DaVinci, TAVI/TAVR, MitraClip, and WATCHMAN, with comprehensive Heart Team management.

## 2. Heart Team

All major cardiac procedures MUST be reviewed by Heart Team (Cardiologist + Cardiac Surgeon + Cardiac Anesthesia).

## 3. Procedure Types

### Robotic (DaVinci)
- **Mitral valve repair/replacement**
- **CABG (TECAB / MIDCAB)**
- **ASD/VSD closure**
- **Cardiac myxoma resection**
- **Tricuspid valve repair/replacement**
- **Cox-Maze IV for AF**

### Transcatheter
- **TAVI/TAVR** — For elderly patients
- **MitraClip** — For high-risk MR
- **WATCHMAN** — For AF without anticoagulation

## 4. Risk Assessment

### STS Risk Score
- **Low** < 4%
- **Moderate** 4-8%
- **High** 8-12%
- **Very High** > 12%

### EuroSCORE II
- **Low** < 4%
- **Moderate** 4-8%
- **High** 8-15%
- **Very High** > 15%

## 5. Pre-Op Checklist (10 items)

1. Echo within 30 days
2. Coronary anatomy known
3. Pulmonary function tests (PFTs)
4. Renal function
5. Frailty assessment
6. Coagulation profile
7. Blood typing + crossmatch
8. PDPL consent
9. Anesthesia consult
10. Perfusionist consult

## 6. Workflows

### 3.1 Robotic Open Heart
1. Heart Team → Approve
2. Pre-op checklist → Complete
3. DaVinci Docking
4. CPB + Procedure
5. ICU → Step-Down → Discharge

### 3.2 TAVI
1. Heart Team → TAVI
2. CT for sizing
3. Femoral access
4. Valve deployment
5. DAPT 1-6 months

### 3.3 MitraClip
1. MR Grade ≥3+
2. COAPT criteria met
3. Transseptal puncture
4. Clip deployment

## 7. Quality Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Procedure success | ≥ 95% |
| 30-day mortality | < 2% |
| Stroke | < 1% |
| Conversion to open | < 5% |
| LOS | 5-7 days |

## 8. FAQ

### Q: When TAVI vs Open Surgery?
**A**: STS ≥ 4% or age ≥ 80, with severe AS.

### Q: When MitraClip?
**A**: MR Grade ≥3+, STS ≥8%, EF 30-60%.

### Q: When WATCHMAN?
**A**: AF + CHA2DS2-VASc ≥2 + contraindication to anticoagulation.
