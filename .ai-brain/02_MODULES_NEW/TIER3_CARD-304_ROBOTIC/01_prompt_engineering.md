# CARD-304_ROBOTIC — Robotic CV Surgery

## Module ID
`TIER3_CARD-304_ROBOTIC` — Robotic Cardiovascular Surgery

## Specialty
Minimally invasive robotic cardiac surgery + interventional procedures

## Clinical Scope
- **Robotic mitral valve repair/replacement** — DaVinci platform
- **Robotic CABG** — MIDCAB, TECAB
- **Robotic ASD/VSD closure** — Atrial septal defect repair
- **Robotic atrial myxoma** — Tumor resection
- **Robotic tricuspid valve** — Repair/replacement
- **Robotic atrial fibrillation** — Cox-Maze IV
- **TAVI/TAVR** — Transcatheter aortic valve
- **MitraClip** — Transcatheter mitral repair
- **WATCHMAN** — Left atrial appendage closure
- **Endovascular aneurysm** — TEVAR, EVAR
- **Transcatheter tricuspid** — Cardioband, PASCAL

## Scoring Systems
- **STS Score** — Risk prediction for cardiac surgery
- **EuroSCORE II** — European risk score
- **MitraScore** — MR severity
- **Aortic valve area** — TAVI sizing
- **CHA2DS2-VASc** — AF stroke risk
- **HAS-BLED** — Bleeding risk
- **NYHA / ACC Stage** — Functional
- **EF %** — LV function
