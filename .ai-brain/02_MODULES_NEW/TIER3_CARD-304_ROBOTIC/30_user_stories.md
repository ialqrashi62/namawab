# CARD-304_ROBOTIC — User Stories

## US-1: Heart Team MDT
**As** a cardiac surgeon
**I want** to convene Heart Team MDT
**So that** we make optimal surgical decisions

### AC
- [ ] Cardiologist + Surgeon + Anesthesia review
- [ ] Document MDT decision
- [ ] Patient consent (PDPL)
- [ ] STS/EuroSCORE II calculated
- [ ] Final approach: Robotic / Open / Catheter

## US-2: Pre-Op Checklist
**As** a nurse coordinator
**I want** to ensure all 10 pre-op items complete
**So that** patient is surgery-ready

### AC
- [ ] Echo within 30 days
- [ ] Coronary anatomy known
- [ ] PFTs done
- [ ] Renal clearance documented
- [ ] Frailty assessed
- [ ] Coagulation profile
- [ ] Blood typing + crossmatch
- [ ] PDPL consent signed
- [ ] Anesthesia consult
- [ ] Perfusionist consult

## US-3: TAVI Eligibility
**As** a cardiologist
**I want** to assess TAVI eligibility
**So that** I can refer appropriate patients

### AC
- [ ] Age ≥65 with STS ≥4% or age ≥80
- [ ] Annulus 18-30 mm
- [ ] EF ≥20%
- [ ] Life expectancy >1 year
- [ ] Device choice (Sapien 3 / Evolut)

## US-4: MitraClip
**As** an interventional cardiologist
**I want** to identify MitraClip candidates
**So that** high-risk MR patients get transcatheter repair

### AC
- [ ] MR Grade ≥3+
- [ ] EF 30-60%
- [ ] STS ≥8%
- [ ] NYHA II-IV
- [ ] Symptoms on GDMT

## US-5: WATCHMAN
**As** a cardiologist
**I want** to identify WATCHMAN candidates
**So that** AF patients avoid anticoagulation

### AC
- [ ] CHA2DS2-VASc ≥2
- [ ] HAS-BLED ≥3
- [ ] Contraindication to anticoagulation
- [ ] LAA ostium 17-31 mm

## US-6: Robotic Surgery Eligibility
**As** a robotic cardiac surgeon
**I want** to identify robotic candidates
**So that** I can offer minimally invasive surgery

### AC
- [ ] Age <80
- [ ] EF ≥30%
- [ ] No prior cardiac surgery
- [ ] BMI <40
- [ ] Adequate pulmonary function

## US-7: Conversion to Open Risk
**As** a robotic surgeon
**I want** to assess conversion risk
**So that** I can prepare for sternotomy

### AC
- [ ] BMI >35 = increased risk
- [ ] Prior cardiac surgery = increased risk
- [ ] Complex anatomy = increased risk
- [ ] Sternotomy set ready

## US-8: Discharge Planning
**As** a cardiac nurse
**I want** to confirm discharge readiness
**So that** patient can safely go home

### AC
- [ ] Afebrile 24 hours
- [ ] Ambulating independently
- [ ] Pain controlled
- [ ] No inotropes
- [ ] Eating normally
- [ ] Echo shows improvement
