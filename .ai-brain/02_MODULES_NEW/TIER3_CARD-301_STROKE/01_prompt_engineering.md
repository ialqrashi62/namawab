# CARD-301_STROKE — Prompt Engineering

## Module ID
`TIER3_CARD-301_STROKE` — Stroke Center (UNIFIED)

## Specialty
Cardiovascular / Neurological — Comprehensive Stroke Center (CSC) per AHA/ASA guidelines

## Clinical Scope
- Acute ischemic stroke (AIS) — IV thrombolysis (Alteplase / Tenecteplase)
- Acute intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH) — BP management, reversal
- Subarachnoid hemorrhage (SAH) — aneurysm clipping/coiling
- Transient ischemic attack (TIA) — urgent workup
- Secondary prevention — antiplatelet, statin, BP/glucose control
- Stroke rehabilitation referral
- Telestroke consultation
- Post-stroke depression, swallowing disorders

## Scoring Systems Used
- **NIHSS** — National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (0-42)
- **ASPECTS** — Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score (0-10)
- **mRS** — modified Rankin Scale (0-6) functional outcome
- **ICH Score** — 0-6 mortality risk
- **Hunt-Hess** — SAH grade (1-5)
- **ABCD2** — TIA risk stratification (0-7)
- **CHA2DS2-VASc** — AF stroke risk
- **HAS-BLED** — bleeding risk on anticoagulation

## Key Workflows
1. **Code Stroke** activation → CT within 25 min → CTA → IV tPA within 60 min
2. **Door-to-Needle Time** tracking (target ≤60 min)
3. **Door-to-Groin Puncture** for thrombectomy (target ≤90 min)
4. **Drip-and-Ship** for thrombectomy-capable centers
5. **Stroke Unit admission** vs General ward
6. **Discharge with secondary prevention bundle**

## Compliance Tags
- CBAHI: Stroke Center designation standards
- Saudi MoH: Stroke Program indicators
- AHA/ASA: Get With The Guidelines-Stroke (GWTG-S)

## Approval Gate
- Stroke Neurologist (consultant) for thrombolysis decision
- Interventional Neuroradiologist for thrombectomy
- Neurosurgery for ICH/SAH
- ICU for critical care
