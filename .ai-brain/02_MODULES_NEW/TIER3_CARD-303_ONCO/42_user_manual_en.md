# User Manual — Cardio-Oncology (TIER3_CARD-303_ONCO)

> **Audience**: Cardio-Onc specialists, oncologists, pharmacists
> **Version**: 1.0.0

---

## 1. Introduction

Cardio-Onc Center manages cardiac complications of cancer therapy (anthracyclines, trastuzumab, ICIs, VEGF), cancer-associated VTE, cardiac amyloidosis, and survivorship.

## 2. HFA-ICOS Risk Stratification

| Risk | Factors | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Low | No risk factors | Standard surveillance |
| Moderate | Age >65, HTN, DM | Echo every 3-6 months |
| High | Doxorubicin >250, prior RT | Echo every 3 months + cardioprotection |
| Very High | EF <50%, prior cardiotox, AL | MUST Cardio-Onc consult |

## 3. Anthracycline Monitoring

- **Echo + GLS** every 3 months
- **Troponin** monthly
- **GLS drop >15%** → Hold chemo
- **EF drop >10% to <50%** → Hold + ACEi + BB

## 4. ICI Myocarditis Monitoring

- **ECG + Troponin** before each cycle
- **Troponin ↑** → Pause ICI + Corticosteroids
- **Severe (Troponin >3x ULN)** → Methylprednisolone 1g/day
- **Fulminant** → ICU + mechanical support

## 5. Cancer VTE Treatment

- **Breast/Lung** → Apixaban 5mg BID (DOAC)
- **Gastric/Pancreatic** → LMWH (GI bleed risk)
- **Duration**: 6 months minimum

## 6. Cardiac Amyloid

- **Pyrophosphate Scan** → ATTR
- **Light Chains** → AL
- **Cardiac MRI LGE** → confirm

## 7. Cardioprotection

- **ACEi** (Enalapril 5-10 mg)
- **Beta-blocker** (Carvedilol 6.25-25 mg BID)
- **Statin** (Atorvastatin 20-40 mg)
- **Dexrazoxane** (10:1 ratio with Doxorubicin)

## 8. Quality Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Cardioprotection in high-risk | ≥ 75% |
| EF preservation | ≥ 90% |
| ICI myocarditis survival | ≥ 50% |
| VTE recurrence | < 5% |

## 9. FAQ

### Q: When to start cardioprotection?
**A**: Very High risk (EF <50%, Doxorubicin >400 mg/m²).

### Q: When to hold chemo?
**A**: GLS drop >15% relative OR EF drop >10% to <50%.

### Q: ICI Myocarditis treatment?
**A**: Methylprednisolone 1g/day IV × 3-5 days IMMEDIATELY.
