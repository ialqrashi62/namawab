# CARD-303_ONCO — Cardio-Oncology

## Module ID
`TIER3_CARD-303_ONCO` — Cardio-Oncology Center

## Specialty
Cardiovascular + Oncology — Managing cardiac complications of cancer therapy

## Clinical Scope
- **Chemotherapy-induced cardiotoxicity** — Anthracyclines, Trastuzumab, TKIs, ICIs
- **Radiation-induced heart disease** — Valvular, Pericardial, CAD, Conduction
- **Cardiac tumors** — Myxoma, Fibroelastoma, Sarcoma, Metastases
- **Cardiac amyloidosis** — AL, ATTR, TTR
- **Pericardial disease** — Effusion, Tamponade, Constrictive
- **Pulmonary hypertension** — Group 1 (PAH), Group 4 (CTEPH)
- **VTE in cancer** — DOAC vs Warfarin
- **Cardio-Onc clinical trials** — Cardioprotection, β-blockers, ACEi, Statins
- **Pre-chemotherapy CV risk assessment**
- **Survivorship** — long-term monitoring

## Scoring Systems
- **HFA-ICOS** — Heart Failure Association Cardio-Onc 4-step
- **CTCAE v5.0** — Cardiac toxicity grading
- **LVEF** — Surveillance during chemo
- **GLS** — Global Longitudinal Strain (early detection)
- **CARDIOTOX-Score** — Risk prediction
- **CHA2DS2-VASc** — AF in cancer
- **HAS-BLED** — Bleeding on anticoagulation
- **HASHTAR** — Stratified HF risk in cancer

## Key Workflows
1. **Pre-Treatment CV Risk Assessment** — baseline echo, GLS, biomarkers
2. **Anthracycline Monitoring** — Echo every 3 months
3. **Trastuzumab Monitoring** — Echo every 3 months
4. **HTN Management** — VEGF inhibitors (Sunitinib, Pazopanib)
5. **QTc Monitoring** — Multiple agents prolong QT
6. **VTE Management** — DOAC vs LMWH
7. **Cardiac Amyloid Workup** — Pyrophosphate scan, biopsy
8. **Survivorship Monitoring** — Long-term CV complications
