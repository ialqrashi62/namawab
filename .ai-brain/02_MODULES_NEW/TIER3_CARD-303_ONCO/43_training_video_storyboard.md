## Video 2: Anthracycline Monitoring (5 min)

### Scene 1 — Anthracycline Risk
- Doxorubicin 250, 400, 550 mg/m² thresholds
- Spoken: "Dox >400 mg/m² خطير"

### Scene 2 — Echo + GLS
- GLS >-18% = normal
- GLS drop >15% = hold chemo
- Spoken: "GLS أكثر حساسية من EF"

### Scene 3 — Treatment
- EF drop >10% to <50% → hold + ACEi + BB
- Spoken: "Hold + Optimize + Reassess 4 weeks"

### Scene 4 — Dexrazoxane
- For high-dose anthracycline
- Spoken: "10:1 ratio protection"

## Video 3: ICI Myocarditis (5 min)

### Scene 1 — Recognition
- ICI drugs: Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Atezolizumab
- Symptoms: dyspnea, fatigue, chest pain
- Spoken: "1-2% incidence, 50% mortality"

### Scene 2 — Diagnosis
- Troponin elevation
- ECG: QRS widening
- Cardiac MRI LGE
- Spoken: "ECG + Troponin every cycle"

### Scene 3 — Treatment
- Mild: hold ICI
- Moderate: methylprednisolone 1-2 mg/kg
- Severe: methylprednisolone 500-1000 mg/day IV
- Fulminant: ICU + mechanical support
- Spoken: "ابدأ الكورتيزون فوراً"

## Video 4: Cancer VTE (4 min)

### Scene 1 — Recognition
- Cancer VTE 4-7x risk
- Spoken: "Cancer + VTE = Mortality"

### Scene 2 — Treatment
- Non-Gastric: DOAC (Apixaban 5mg BID)
- Gastric: LMWH
- Spoken: "DOAC for non-GI, LMWH for Gastric"

### Scene 3 — Duration
- 3-6 months minimum
- Indefinite while on chemo
- Spoken: "6 أشهر أساسي"
