# 05 Compliance & Security — Obstetrics & Gynecology

## 1. Compliance
- CBAHI maternal and newborn safety standards.
- Saudi PDPL for maternal and neonatal data.
- JCI newborn identification and mother-baby matching.

## 2. Security
- `requireRole('obstetrician')` / `requireRole('midwife')`, `requireTenantScope`.
- Ultrasounds and delivery videos in PHI vault.
- Restricted access to sensitive gynecology notes.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for Partogram edits, delivery summary, APGAR, and newborn matching.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Mother-baby identity match before discharge.
- Blood availability check for high-risk deliveries.
- Consent before operative delivery or procedures.

## 5. Data Retention
- Maternal records retained per institutional policy; audit logs 7+ years.
