# 05 Compliance & Security — Rehabilitation Support Services

## 1. Compliance
- CBAHI discharge planning and continuity of care.
- Saudi PDPL for patient home and caregiver data.

## 2. Security
- `requireRole('rehab_therapist')` / `requireRole('social_worker')`, `requireTenantScope`.
- Restricted access to home assessment details.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for equipment loans, home assessments, and caregiver training.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Therapist approval for equipment issue.
- Home safety checklist completion before discharge.
- Caregiver acknowledgment of training.

## 5. Data Retention
- Support records retained per policy; audit logs 7+ years.
