## 🎯 Group-Level Clinical Authority (CMO)

### Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation

**Rehabilitation phases:**
1. **Acute (inpatient)** — early mobilization, prevention of complications
2. **Sub-acute (IRF)** — intensive multidisciplinary
3. **Skilled nursing (SNF)** — less intensive
4. **Home health** — intermittent visits
5. **Outpatient** — ongoing therapy

**Rehab team:**
- Physiatrist (PM&R physician)
- Physical therapist (PT)
- Occupational therapist (OT)
- Speech-language pathologist (SLP)
- Rehabilitation nurse
- Social worker
- Psychologist
- Recreational therapist
- Prosthetist/Orthotist

**PT modalities:**
- Manual therapy (joint mobilization, soft tissue)
- Therapeutic exercise (strength, endurance, ROM)
- Electrotherapy (TENS, NMES, ultrasound)
- Hydrotherapy (aquatic)
- Gait training
- Balance/proprioception
- Modalities: heat, cold, ultrasound, laser

**OT domains:**
- ADL (activities of daily living) training
- IADL (instrumental ADL)
- Fine motor skills
- Visual-perceptual
- Cognitive rehab
- Sensory integration (pediatric)
- Wheelchair prescription
- Splinting

**Speech therapy:**
- Aphasia (receptive, expressive)
- Dysarthria
- Apraxia of speech
- Dysphagia (swallowing)
- Voice disorders
- Cognitive communication
- AAC (augmentative and alternative communication)

**Swallowing assessment:**
- Bedside swallow eval
- VFSS (videofluoroscopic swallow study)
- FEES (fiberoptic endoscopic evaluation of swallowing)
- Modified barium swallow

**Dysphagia diet levels (IDDSI):**
- Level 0: Thin liquid
- Level 1: Slightly thick
- Level 2: Mildly thick
- Level 3: Moderately thick (liquidized)
- Level 4: Extremely thick (puree)
- Level 5: Minced and moist
- Level 6: Soft and bite-sized
- Level 7: Regular

**Wheelchair prescription (key measurements):**
- Seat width
- Seat depth
- Seat height
- Back height
- Armrest height
- Footrest length
- Cushion type (foam, gel, air, custom)

**Prosthetic levels (lower limb):**
- Toe
- Partial foot
- Symes (ankle disarticulation)
- Below knee (BK / transtibial)
- Knee disarticulation
- Above knee (AK / transfemoral)
- Hip disarticulation
- Hemipelvectomy

**Spinal Cord Injury (SCI) levels:**
| Level | Function preserved |
|---|---|
| C1-C3 | Ventilator-dependent |
| C4 | Diaphragm (vent-free) |
| C5 | Deltoid, biceps |
| C6 | Wrist extensors |
| C7 | Triceps |
| T1 | Hand intrinsic |
| T6 | Upper abdominal |
| T10 | Lower abdominal |
| L2 | Hip flexion |
| L4 | Knee extension |
| L5 | Ankle dorsiflexion |
| S1 | Plantarflexion |

**ASIA Impairment Scale (AIS):**
- A: Complete (no motor/sensory below level)
- B: Sensory incomplete
- C: Motor incomplete, more than half muscles < 3
- D: Motor incomplete, more than half muscles ≥ 3
- E: Normal

### Radiation Oncology

**Radiation types:**
- External beam (EBRT)
  - 3D-CRT
  - IMRT (Intensity Modulated RT)
  - VMAT (Volumetric Modulated Arc Therapy)
  - SRS (Stereotactic Radiosurgery): single fraction
  - SBRT (Stereotactic Body RT): few fractions
  - IGRT (Image-Guided RT)
  - Proton therapy
- Brachytherapy
  - HDR (High Dose Rate)
  - LDR (Low Dose Rate)
  - Seeds (permanent)
  - Eye plaques

**Total dose & fractionation:**
- Conventional: 1.8-2 Gy/fraction, 5 fractions/week
- Hypofractionated: 3-8 Gy/fraction, fewer fractions
- SRS: 12-24 Gy single fraction (small targets)
- SBRT: 30-60 Gy in 3-5 fractions

**Common regimens:**
| Cancer | Dose | Fractions |
|---|---|---|
| Breast (adjuvant whole breast) | 50 Gy | 25 |
| Breast (boost) | 10-16 Gy | 5-8 |
| Prostate (localized) | 78 Gy | 39 |
| Prostate (post-prostatectomy) | 66-70 Gy | 33-35 |
| Lung (SBRT) | 48-60 Gy | 4-5 |
| Head & Neck (IMRT) | 70 Gy | 35 |
| Brain (glioblastoma) | 60 Gy | 30 |
| Palliative bone | 8 Gy | 1 |

**Toxicity grading (CTCAE v5):**
- G1: Mild, asymptomatic
- G2: Moderate, local intervention
- G3: Severe, hospitalization
- G4: Life-threatening
- G5: Death

**Common toxicities:**
| Site | Acute | Late |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | Erythema, desquamation | Fibrosis, telangiectasia |
| GI (abdominal) | Nausea, diarrhea | Stricture, fistula |
| GU (pelvic) | Cystitis, dysuria | Hemorrhagic cystitis |
| Pulmonary | Pneumonitis | Fibrosis |
| CNS | Edema | Necrosis, cognitive decline |
| Bone marrow | Cytopenia | MDS, leukemia |

**Radiation safety:**
- Time, distance, shielding
- ALARA principle (As Low As Reasonably Achievable)
- Personal dosimetry
- Pregnancy screening
- Area monitoring

### Clinical Pharmacy

**Pharmacy workflow:**
1. Order received
2. Pharmacist verification (dose, route, frequency, indication, allergy, interaction, renal dose)
3. Preparation (USP <797> sterile, USP <795> non-sterile)
4. Double check (chemo, high-alert)
5. Delivery to floor
6. Nurse administration (right patient, right drug, right dose, right route, right time, right documentation, right reason, right response, right education, right to refuse)

**High-alert medications (ISMP list):**
- Insulin
- Opioids
- Anticoagulants (heparin, warfarin, DOACs)
- Chemotherapy
- Concentrated electrolytes (KCl, NaCl)
- Digoxin
- Neuromuscular blockers
- IV inotropes
- Magnesium sulfate
- Anti-epileptics (loading doses)

**IV admixture (USP <797> classifications):**
- Category 1: BUD ≤ 12 days, ISO 5
- Category 2: BUD ≤ 9 days, ISO 5
- Category 3: BUD ≤ 45 days, ISO 5 (sterility tested)

**Chemo preparation (USP <800>):**
- Negative pressure room
- Biological safety cabinet (BSC)
- Closed-system transfer device (CSTD)
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Double-glove, chemotherapy gown
- Eye/face protection
- Designated chemo refrigerator

**TDM (Therapeutic Drug Monitoring):**

| Drug | Therapeutic range | Sample timing |
|---|---|---|
| Vancomycin | Trough 10-20 μg/mL | Trough (just before next dose) |
| Aminoglycosides (Gent) | Peak 5-10, Trough < 2 | Peak 30min after, Trough |
| Phenytoin | Total 10-20, Free 1-2 | Trough |
| Valproic acid | 50-100 | Trough |
| Carbamazepine | 4-12 | Trough |
| Theophylline | 10-20 | Trough |
| Digoxin | 0.5-0.9 (HF), 0.8-2 (AF) | Trough (>6h post-dose) |
| Lithium | 0.6-1.2 (acute), 0.6-0.8 (chronic) | Trough (12h post-dose) |
| Tacrolimus | 5-20 (depends on organ/time) | Trough |
| Cyclosporine | Variable | Trough or C2 |
| Methotrexate | Variable (high-dose needs leucovorin rescue) | Per protocol |

// rehabilitation_engine.js

### USP <797> sterile compounding
- ISO Class 5 environment for sterile
- Gowning, gloving, hand hygiene
- BUD (beyond-use date) tracking
- Sterility testing
- Environmental monitoring

### USP <800> hazardous drug handling
- Negative pressure room
- Closed-system transfer device
- Eye/face protection
- Double glove
- Chemo-tested gown
- Spill kit
- Designated equipment
- Waste handling as hazardous

- Area monitoring
- ALARA
- Pregnancy screening
