# 01 Clinical Spec — Rehabilitation Support Services

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Support functions for rehabilitation including equipment loans, home modifications, caregiver training, and community resource coordination.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - Equipment loan tracking.
  - Home assessment and modification requests.
  - Caregiver education documentation.
  - Community resource referrals.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Rehabilitation discharge plan → 2. Support needs assessment → 3. Equipment/home modifications → 4. Caregiver training → 5. Community handoff → 6. Follow-up.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Equipment compatibility checks.
- Home safety checklist.
- Caregiver competency verification.

## 4. Integration Points
- Rehabilitation module.
- ADT/discharge planning.
- Inventory for equipment.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Therapist approval for equipment loan.
- Home safety verification before discharge.
- Caregiver acknowledgment of training.
