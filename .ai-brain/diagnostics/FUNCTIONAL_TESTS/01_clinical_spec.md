# 01_clinical_spec.md - Functional Tests Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Functional Testing Ecosystem
Physiological assessments of organ systems.

### Specialized Units:
- **Cardiac Function:** ECG, Stress Testing, Holter, and Echo.
- **Neurological Function:** EMG, Nerve Conduction, and EEG.
- **Respiratory Function:** PFT, Diffusion capacity, and Sleep studies.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Test Flow:** Preparation $\rightarrow$ Acquisition $\rightarrow$ Interpretation $\rightarrow$ Final Report.
- **KPIs:** Test accuracy, Patient tolerance, and Interpretation TAT.
- **Required Data:** Baseline vitals, Test parameters, and Waveform data.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Safety protocols for stress tests and EEG/EMG/Spirometry.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of physiological waveforms and recordings.
