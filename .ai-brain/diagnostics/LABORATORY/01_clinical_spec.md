# 01_clinical_spec.md - Central Laboratory Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Laboratory Ecosystem
High-throughput clinical pathology and molecular diagnostics.

### Specialized Units:
- **Clinical Pathology:** Histopathology, Cytopathology, and Frozen Sections.
- **Microbiology:** Bacteriology, Virology, and Antibiotic Sensitivity.
- **Clinical Chemistry:** Routine labs, Hormones, and Tumor Markers.
- **Immunology & Serology:** Autoimmune markers and Allergy testing.
- **Medical Genetics:** Cytogenetics and Molecular DNA analysis.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Lab Workflow:** Order $\rightarrow$ Specimen Collection $\rightarrow$ Processing $\rightarrow$ Analysis $\rightarrow$ Verification $\rightarrow$ Result.
- **KPIs:** Turnaround Time (TAT), Error rate (Pre-analytical/Analytical), and QC variance.
- **Required Data:** Specimen ID, Collection time, Reagent lot, and Control values.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI/ISO 15189:** Strict adherence to quality control and specimen traceability.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of genetic data and sensitive pathology reports.
