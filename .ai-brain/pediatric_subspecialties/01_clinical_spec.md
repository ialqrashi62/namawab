# 01 Clinical Spec — Pediatric Subspecialties

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Pediatric cardiology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, nephrology, endocrinology, neurology, hematology/oncology, rheumatology, dermatology, and developmental/behavioral pediatrics.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - Subspecialty referral triage.
  - Condition-specific assessment and monitoring.
  - Growth and developmental context for all decisions.
  - Transition planning to adult services.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Referral from general pediatrics → 2. Subspecialty assessment → 3. Diagnostic workup → 4. Treatment plan → 5. Follow-up → 6. Transition to adult care.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Subspecialty-specific scoring (e.g., asthma control, seizure frequency, HbA1c).
- Growth-adjusted thresholds.
- Transition readiness checklist at age 14+.

## 4. Integration Points
- General pediatrics growth/immunization.
- CPOE, LIS/RIS, pharmacy.
- Pediatric surgery and ICU.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Guardian consent for subspecialty procedures.
- Weight/age-based dosing.
- Allergy and contraindication checks.
