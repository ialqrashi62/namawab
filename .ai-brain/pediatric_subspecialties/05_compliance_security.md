# 05 Compliance & Security — Pediatric Subspecialties

## 1. Compliance
- AAP and Saudi MOH pediatric subspecialty standards.
- CBAHI pediatric safety.
- Saudi PDPL for pediatric and guardian data.

## 2. Security
- `requireRole('pediatric_subspecialist')`, `requireTenantScope`.
- Guardian-linked portal access.
- Restricted sensitive notes.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for referrals, assessments, scores, and transition plans.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Guardian consent for procedures.
- Weight/age-based dosing verification.
- Allergy and contraindication hard stop.

## 5. Data Retention
- Subspecialty records retained per policy; audit logs 7+ years.
