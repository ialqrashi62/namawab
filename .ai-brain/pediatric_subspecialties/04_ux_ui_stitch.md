# 04 UX/UI Stitch — Pediatric Subspecialties

## 1. Design Tokens
- **Palette**: same as pediatrics with subspecialty accent colors.
- **Typography**: Stitch clinical stations.
- **Layout**: 3-column station.

## 2. Components
- `SubspecialtyReferralForm`: reason, priority, attachments.
- `SubspecialtyAssessmentPanel`: diagnosis, plan, scores.
- `GrowthContextBadge`: growth percentile visible in all views.
- `TransitionReadinessChecklist`: age 14+ readiness items.

## 3. User Stories
- "As a pediatric cardiologist, I want growth-adjusted cardiac thresholds and referral context."
- "As a general pediatrician, I want to track referral status and subspecialty recommendations."

## 4. Wireframe Logic
- Left: referral queue + patient list.
- Center: assessment and score tabs.
- Right: growth context + transition checklist + alerts.

## 5. Accessibility
- RTL/LTR, high-contrast alerts, guardian-friendly summaries.
