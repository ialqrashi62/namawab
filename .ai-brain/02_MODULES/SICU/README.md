# SICU — Surgical Intensive Care Unit

## Mission
Post-op care for high-risk surgical patients: cardiac surgery, major abdominal, vascular, neuro, transplant.

## Top Conditions
- Post-cardiac surgery
- Major abdominal surgery (pancreatectomy, esophagectomy, Whipple)
- Major vascular surgery (AAA repair, carotid)
- Post-neurosurgery
- Solid organ transplant

## Red Flags
- Post-op hemorrhage
- Anastomotic leak
- Sepsis
- Hemodynamic instability
- Compartment syndrome

## AI Decision Support
- Post-op complication risk
- Hemodynamic monitoring
- Sepsis screening
