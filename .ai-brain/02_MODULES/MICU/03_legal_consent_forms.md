# MICU — Legal Consent Forms (10 types)

## 1. General Admission Consent
- Patient: "I consent to admission to MICU and treatment by the medical team"
- Includes: routine care, monitoring, lab tests, imaging
- Excludes: specific procedures (need separate consent)
- Signature: patient or guardian
- Date, time, witness

## 2. ICU-Specific Consent
- Mechanical ventilation
- Central venous catheter
- Arterial line
- Foley catheter
- NG tube
- Blood transfusion
- Signature: patient or legal representative

## 3. Central Line Consent
- Indications: medications, monitoring, dialysis
- Risks: pneumothorax, arterial puncture, infection, thrombosis
- Alternatives: peripheral IV, PICC
- Site marked, timeout performed
- Signature: patient, MD, witness

## 4. Arterial Line Consent
- Indications: continuous BP monitoring, ABG
- Risks: bleeding, ischemia, infection
- Site: radial (preferred), femoral
- Allen test performed
- Signature: patient, MD

## 5. Mechanical Ventilation Consent
- Indications: respiratory failure, airway protection
- Modes: explain in simple terms
- Risks: VILI, barotrauma, VAP, ventilator dependence
- Weaning plan
- Signature: patient/family

## 6. Blood Transfusion Consent
- Indications: Hb <7, active bleed, symptomatic
- Type: PRBC, FFP, platelets, cryo
- Risks: TRALI, TACO, infection, hemolytic reaction
- Alternatives: cell saver, EPO
- Signature: patient/family

## 7. DNR / Code Status Form
- Patient capacity assessment
- Patient wishes (if capacity)
- Surrogate decision-maker (if no capacity)
- Specific: Full, DNR, DNI, AND, Comfort
- Family meeting documented
- Signature: patient/family, MD, witness

## 8. AI-Assisted Decision Consent
- "I understand that AI may be used to assist in clinical decision-making"
- AI provides recommendations only
- Final decision is by physician
- Audit log of AI use
- Signature: patient

## 9. Research Consent (if applicable)
- Study title, sponsor
- Procedures, risks, benefits
- Confidentiality
- Right to withdraw
- Compensation (if any)
- Signature: patient, investigator

## 10. Telemedicine Consent (if applicable)
- Provider at remote site
- Real-time audio/video
- PHI transmitted
- Recording (if any)
- Signature: patient

## Bilingual Format
- EN + AR side-by-side
- Patient chooses language
- Witness for illiterate patients
- Cultural/religious accommodation

## Storage
- Encrypted at rest
- 7-year retention
- Audit log of access
- Available to patient on request (PDPL right to access)
