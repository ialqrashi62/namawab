# MICU — Medical ICU Clinical Workflows

## Top 10 Conditions
1. **Septic shock** (mortality 30-50%)
2. **Acute respiratory failure** (ARDS)
3. **Cardiogenic shock**
4. **Acute MI (post-PCI)**
5. **Acute stroke** (post-tPA)
6. **DKA** (diabetic ketoacidosis)
7. **GI bleed** (massive)
8. **Liver failure** (acute, decompensated)
9. **Pancreatitis (severe)**
10. **Drug overdose** (intentional, accidental)

## Scoring Systems
| System | Use | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **APACHE II** | Severity, mortality | 0-71 |
| **SOFA** | Organ failure | 0-24 |
| **qSOFA** | Sepsis screening | 0-3 (≥2) |
| **GCS** | Consciousness | 3-15 |
| **RASS** | Sedation | -5 to +4 |
| **CAM-ICU** | Delirium | +ve / -ve |
| **Braden** | Pressure ulcer | 6-23 |
| **NRS-2002** | Nutrition risk | 0-7 |

## Sepsis Bundle (Hour-1)
1. Lactate (repeat if >2)
2. Blood cultures before ABX
3. Broad-spectrum ABX within 1h
4. 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥4
5. Vasopressors (norepinephrine) for MAP <65

## ARDS Management (ARDSNet)
- Tidal volume: 6 mL/kg PBW
- Plateau pressure ≤30 cmH2O
- PEEP titration (FiO2/PEEP table)
- Prone positioning (P/F <150, 16h/day)
- Conservative fluid strategy

## Vasoactive Drips (ICU)
| Drug | Dose | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| Norepinephrine | 0.05-1 mcg/kg/min | Septic shock (1st line) |
| Epinephrine | 0.05-0.5 mcg/kg/min | Cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis |
| Vasopressin | 0.03-0.04 U/min | Septic shock (2nd line) |
| Dopamine | 5-20 mcg/kg/min | Bradycardia, hypotension |
| Dobutamine | 2-20 mcg/kg/min | Cardiogenic shock |
| Phenylephrine | 0.1-0.5 mcg/kg/min | Hypotension (pure α) |

## Ventilator Liberation (Weaning)
1. **Screen daily:** RSBI (f/Vt) <105
2. **Spontaneous breathing trial (SBT):** 30-120 min T-piece or PS 5-8
3. **Extubation if:** SBT passed, mental status, airway patent
4. **NIV post-extubation** if high risk (CHF, COPD)

## Red Flags
- New onset arrhythmia
- Hypotension (MAP <65)
- Hypoxia (SpO2 <90)
- Increased vasopressor need
- Decreased urine output <0.5 mL/kg/h
- Mental status change
- GI bleed (coffee-ground, hematochezia)
- DVT/PE signs

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: hemodynamics, airway
- Drug safety: high-alert (insulin drip, heparin, vasopressors)
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: Intensivist + ICU nurse
- Compliance: JCI, SCCM, CBAHI
- Tests: sepsis bundle, ARDSNet, vent weaning
