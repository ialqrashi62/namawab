# MICU — JCI 7th Edition Checklist

## International Patient Safety Goals (IPSG)
- [ ] IPSG.1 — Identify patients correctly (2 identifiers: name + MRN)
- [ ] IPSG.2 — Improve effective communication (read-back for critical results)
- [ ] IPSG.3 — Improve safety of high-alert medications (insulin, anticoag, vasopressors)
- [ ] IPSG.4 — Ensure correct site, correct procedure, correct patient (WHO checklist)
- [ ] IPSG.5 — Reduce risk of healthcare-associated infections (CLABSI, CAUTI, VAP)
- [ ] IPSG.6 — Reduce risk of patient falls (Braden + Morse)

## Access to Care & Continuity of Care (ACC)
- [ ] MICU admission criteria documented
- [ ] Bed allocation policy
- [ ] Hand-off (SBAR) at shift change
- [ ] Transfer to/from ICU standardized
- [ ] Discharge planning starts at admission

## Patient-Centered Care (PCC)
- [ ] Daily family communication
- [ ] Visiting hours policy
- [ ] Cultural / religious considerations
- [ ] End-of-life care planning
- [ ] Pain management (NRS documented q4h)

## Assessment of Patients (AOP)
- [ ] Initial nursing assessment within 30 min
- [ ] MD assessment within 1h
- [ ] Vital signs q1h (ICU minimum)
- [ ] Pain assessment q4h
- [ ] Risk assessment: falls, pressure ulcer, DVT, nutrition, suicide (if applicable)

## Care of Patients (COP)
- [ ] Order sets for top 10 conditions (sepsis, ARDS, MI, stroke, etc.)
- [ ] VTE prophylaxis
- [ ] Stress ulcer prophylaxis (if ventilated)
- [ ] Daily sedation vacation
- [ ] Daily SBT
- [ ] Head of bed ≥30° (if ventilated)
- [ ] Oral care q4h (VAP prevention)
- [ ] Central line bundle (CHG bath, daily review, line necessity)

## Medication Management & Use (MMU)
- [ ] High-alert medication policy (insulin, anticoag, vasopressors, opioid, paralytic, K+)
- [ ] Double-check policy (verbal + visual)
- [ ] Smart pump library (vasoactive drips)
- [ ] Pharmacist on rounds
- [ ] Renal dose adjustment
- [ ] Antibiotic stewardship

## Quality Improvement & Patient Safety (QPS)
- [ ] Bundle compliance tracking (sepsis, VAP, CLABSI, CAUTI)
- [ ] ICU mortality rate (APACHE-adjusted)
- [ ] ICU readmission rate (within 48h)
- [ ] Code blue rate
- [ ] Unplanned extubation
- [ ] Pressure ulcer rate
- [ ] CLABSI rate
- [ ] CAUTI rate
- [ ] VAP rate
- [ ] Patient/family satisfaction

## Staffing (SQE)
- [ ] 1:1 or 1:2 nurse:patient ratio (based on acuity)
- [ ] Intensivist on call 24/7
- [ ] RT coverage 24/7
- [ ] Pharmacist coverage
- [ ] Annual competencies (ACLS, ventilator, vasoactive, etc.)

## Facility Management & Safety (FMS)
- [ ] Code blue button (every bed)
- [ ] Crash cart on unit
- [ ] Backup power (UPS + generator)
- [ ] Medical gas (O2, air, suction) at every bed
- [ ] Negative pressure isolation rooms
