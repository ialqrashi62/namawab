# MICU 7-Expert Synthesis (L1-L4)

## Mission
Critical care for adult medical patients: respiratory failure, sepsis, shock, multi-organ failure. 24/7 intensivist coverage. Highest mortality reduction opportunity.

## Top 10 Conditions (CMO)
1. Septic shock (mortality 30-50%)
2. ARDS (mortality 30-40%)
3. Cardiogenic shock (mortality 50-80%)
4. Acute respiratory failure
5. Status epilepticus
6. Massive GI bleed
7. DKA severe
8. Severe electrolyte derangement
9. Multi-organ dysfunction
10. Acute liver failure

## ICU Scoring
- **APACHE II** — admission + daily
- **SOFA** — daily
- **qSOFA** — quick screen
- **GCS** — neuro assessment
- **RASS** — sedation
- **CAM-ICU** — delirium

## Workflow
1. **Admission** (from ED, ward, OR, transfer)
2. **Initial resuscitation** (ABCs, fluids, vasopressors)
3. **Stabilization** (vent, lines, monitoring)
4. **Daily rounds** (intensivist + team, 2x/day)
5. **Family communication** (daily minimum)
6. **Disposition** (ward, step-down, transfer, palliative)

## Red Flags (Top 10)
1. Cardiac arrest
2. Respiratory failure requiring intubation
3. MAP <65, lactate >4
4. Severe arrhythmias
5. Massive bleeding
6. SpO2 <88 on 100% O2
7. Status epilepticus
8. Acute mental status change
9. Multi-organ failure
10. Withdrawal of life support (ethical consult)

## Sepsis Bundle (1h)
- Lactate + repeat
- Blood cultures BEFORE antibiotics
- Broad-spectrum antibiotics <1h
- IV crystalloid 30 mL/kg if hypotensive
- Vasopressors if MAP<65 after fluids

## Mechanical Ventilation
- Lung-protective: TV 6-8 mL/kg PBW
- PEEP titration
- ARDSNet FiO2/PEEP table
- Daily SAT + SBT

## AI Decision Support
- NEWS / NEWS2 (early warning)
- Sepsis screening (qSOFA, SOFA)
- Ventilator weaning readiness
- Sedation level (RASS)
- Delirium (CAM-ICU)
- Drug dosing (renal adjustment)

## KPIs
- ICU mortality (SMR < 1.0)
- Ventilator days
- CLABSI, CAUTI, VAP, pressure ulcer (all target 0)
- Unplanned extubation (<1%)
- ICU readmission within 48h
- LOS

## Compliance
- **JCI:** COP.4, MMU.1-7, PCI, QPS
- **CBAHI:** ICU standards
- **SCCM/ESICM:** Surviving Sepsis 2021
- **PDPL:** all PHI

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: 10+ identified
- Drug safety: renal dose, weight-based for peds, double-check
- PHI: encrypted (vitals, notes, images)
- Auth: Intensivist + ICU-trained RN
- Compliance: JCI, CBAHI, SCCM
