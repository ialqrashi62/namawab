# PEDS-002 — JCI + Compliance

## JCI 7th Edition

### Patient Safety
- [ ] IPSG.1 — Two identifiers
- [ ] IPSG.3 — High-alert meds (insulin, vasopressors, prostaglandin)
- [ ] IPSG.4 — Site marking for invasive procedures
- [ ] IPSG.5 — HAI prevention (CLABSI, VAP)

### Care of Patients
- [ ] Daily MD rounds
- [ ] Multidisciplinary rounds (RN, MD, RT, Pharm, Lactation, Social)
- [ ] Family-centered rounds
- [ ] Thermoregulation (incubator/warmer)
- [ ] Pain assessment (NIPS for neonates)
- [ ] Skin care
- [ ] Developmental care

### Quality
- [ ] CLABSI rate
- [ ] VAP rate
- [ ] NEC rate
- [ ] IVH rate (grade 3-4)
- [ ] Severe ROP rate
- [ ] BPD rate
- [ ] Survival rate by GA
- [ ] Breastfeeding at discharge
- [ ] Parent satisfaction

### Staffing
- [ ] 1:1 or 1:2 nurse:patient ratio
- [ ] Neonatologist on call 24/7
- [ ] RT coverage 24/7

## PDPL
- Special category (neonate)
- Parental consent (mother + father if married)
- Field-level encryption
- Audit log
- 7-year retention

## CBAHI
- NICU-specific standards
- Outcome reporting
- Sentinel event reporting
- Family-centered care

## MOH
- Birth notification (within 24h)
- Death notification
- Disease notification

## Sentinel Events
- Unexpected death
- Wrong patient procedure
- Severe IVH
- Severe ROP
- Severe NEC requiring surgery
