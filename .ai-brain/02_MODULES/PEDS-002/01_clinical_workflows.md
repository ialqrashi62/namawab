# PEDS-002 — Clinical Workflows (NICU)

## Top 10 Conditions
1. **Prematurity** (premature birth)
2. **Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS)** (surfactant deficiency)
3. **Neonatal sepsis** (early/late onset)
4. **Hyperbilirubinemia** (jaundice)
5. **Necrotizing Enterocolitis (NEC)**
6. **Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia (BPD)**
7. **Intraventricular Hemorrhage (IVH)**
8. **Patent Ductus Arteriosus (PDA)**
9. **Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)**
10. **Hypoxic Ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE)**

## NICU Levels
- **Level II:** Special care, ≥32 weeks
- **Level III:** NICU, <32 weeks, ventilation
- **Level IV:** Surgical, ECMO, complex

## Daily Care Bundle
- Vital signs (q1-4h depending on stability)
- Weight (daily)
- Feeding (volume + tolerance)
- Respiratory support (weaning as tolerated)
- Thermal regulation (incubator/warmer)
- Family-centered rounds

## Respiratory Management
- **Surfactant:** for RDS (≤28 weeks, on CPAP or vent)
- **CPAP:** first-line for preterm respiratory distress
- **Conventional ventilation:** for failure of CPAP
- **HFOV:** for severe RDS, air leak
- **iNO:** for persistent pulmonary HTN
- **ECMO:** for refractory respiratory failure

## Nutrition
- **TPN:** first 24-48h if <32 weeks
- **Enteral feeds:** trophic feeds by day 2-3
- **Breast milk:** first choice
- **Fortification:** if growth faltering
- **Goal growth:** 15-20 g/kg/day

## Red Flags
- Bradycardia (<100)
- Apnea (>20s)
- Desaturation (<85%)
- Hypothermia (<36.5°C)
- Hypoglycemia (<40 mg/dL)
- Tachycardia (>200)
- High O2 requirement

## Family-Centered Care
- 24/7 parental presence
- Kangaroo care
- Breastfeeding support
- Parent education
- Bereavement support (if applicable)
