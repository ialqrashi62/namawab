# PEDS-002 — Sub-Department Catalog

## Sub-Units
1. **NICU-II** — Special care (≥32 wks)
2. **NICU-III** — Intensive care (<32 wks)
3. **NICU-IV** — Surgical + ECMO
4. **NICU-HIE** — Therapeutic hypothermia
5. **NICU-ROP** — Retinopathy screening
6. **NICU-FU** — Follow-up clinic (high-risk)
7. **NICU-TRANS** — Transport team

## Per-Subunit
- **NICU-II:** Late preterm, term, stable
- **NICU-III:** Ventilator, vasopressors
- **NICU-IV:** Surgical (NEC, PDA ligation, ROP laser)
- **NICU-HIE:** 72h cooling protocol
- **NICU-ROP:** Eye exams (wks 32-44 corrected)
- **NICU-FU:** 6mo, 12mo, 24mo assessments
- **NICU-TRANS:** Inter-hospital transport

## Cross-Cutting
- Family-centered rounds
- Breastfeeding support
- Discharge planning (begins at admission)
- Bereavement support
