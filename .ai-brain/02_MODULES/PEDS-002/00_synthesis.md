# PEDS-002 7-Expert Synthesis (L1-L4)

## Mission
Critical care for premature and sick newborns. Level III/IV NICU. Highest level of neonatal intensive care.

## Top 10 Red Flags (CMO veto)
1. **Apnea** (>20 sec with bradycardia/desaturation)
2. **Bradycardia** (HR <100)
3. **Hypoxia** (SpO2 <85)
4. **Hypothermia** (<36.5°C)
5. **Hypoglycemia** (glucose <40 mg/dL)
6. **Severe anemia** (Hgb <10 in first week)
7. **Hemorrhage** (especially IVH signs)
8. **NEC** (bloody stool, abdominal distension)
9. **Seizures**
10. **Sudden deterioration** (sepsis, pneumothorax)

## NICU Levels

### Level III
- <32 weeks, <1500g
- Sustained life support
- Mechanical ventilation
- 24/7 neonatologist coverage

### Level IV (Regional)
- All Level III + surgical repair
- ECMO
- Complex congenital surgery
- Multi-subspecialty

## Workflow
1. **Admission** from L&D or transfer
2. **Stabilization** (thermoregulation, respiratory, fluids)
3. **Monitoring** (continuous + labs + imaging)
4. **Care** (respiratory, nutrition, meds)
5. **Family-centered care** (kangaroo, parental presence)
6. **Discharge** (when stable, near term-equivalent)

## Drug Safety (Pediatric — Critical)
- **ALL medications weight-based** (mg/kg or mcg/kg)
- **High-alert double-check**: insulin, heparin, vasopressors, digoxin
- **TPN compounding in pharmacy** (sterile)
- **Off-label use documented** with evidence
- **Look-alike/sound-alike**: TALL-man lettering
- **Dosing weight re-checked daily** (gains/losses)

## Common Meds (mg/kg)
- Surfactant: 200 mg/kg endotracheal
- Caffeine: 20 mg/kg loading
- Ampicillin: 50 mg/kg q8h
- Gentamicin: 4 mg/kg q24h
- Vancomycin: 15 mg/kg q8-12h
- Furosemide: 0.5-1 mg/kg q6-12h
