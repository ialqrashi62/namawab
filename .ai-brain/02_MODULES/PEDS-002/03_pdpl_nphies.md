# PEDS-002 — PDPL + NPHIES + CBAHI

## PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law)
- Neonate = special category (highest protection)
- Parental consent (mother + father if married)
- Field-level encryption (PHI)
- Audit log of all access
- 7+ year retention
- Cross-border: restricted (KSA-based)
- Patient rights: access (by parents), correction, portability

## NPHIES
- Eligibility check
- Pre-authorization (surgery, NICU admission)
- Bundle codes
- Medical necessity documentation
- Appeal process

## CBAHI
- NICU-specific standards
- Outcome reporting
- Sentinel event reporting
- Near-miss reporting
- Patient safety goals
- Quality indicators

## SDAIA
- Data classification
- AI model registration (if used clinically)
- Algorithm transparency

## MOH
- Birth notification (within 24h)
- Death notification (within 24h)
- Disease notification (HBV, HIV, syphilis, GBS)
- Perinatal mortality review (all cases)
- Sentinel events reporting

## Sentinel Events (must report)
- Unexpected neonatal death
- Severe IVH
- Severe NEC (requiring surgery)
- Severe ROP (stage 3+)
- Severe BPD
- Wrong patient procedure
- Medication error resulting in harm
- Equipment failure resulting in harm

## Quality Indicators
- Survival rate by GA
- CLABSI rate (per 1000 line-days)
- VAP rate (per 1000 vent-days)
- NEC rate (medical + surgical)
- IVH rate (grade 3-4)
- Severe ROP rate
- Breastfeeding at discharge (>80% target)
- Length of stay
- Readmission within 30 days
