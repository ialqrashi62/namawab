# PEDS-002 — Legal Consent

## 1. NICU Admission Consent
- Reasons for admission
- Treatments (vent, surfactant, etc.)
- Risks
- Parental authority

## 2. Surfactant Consent
- Indication
- Risks: transient hypoxia, bradycardia, ETT blockage
- Alternative: supportive care
- Witness required

## 3. Procedural Consent
- LP, central line, UVC, UAC, ET intubation
- Indication
- Risks
- Alternative
- Witness

## 4. Surgical Consent
- PDA ligation, NEC laparotomy, ROP laser
- Indication
- Risks
- Anesthesia
- Witness

## 5. Blood Transfusion
- Indication
- Risks
- Alternative (if applicable)

## 6. Vaccination
- Hepatitis B (mother HBsAg- or unknown)
- RSV prophylaxis (palivizumab)
- Influenza (after 6 months)
- Catch-up schedule

## 7. Research
- Study title, sponsor
- Risks, benefits
- Voluntary
- Right to withdraw

## 8. Photography/Video
- For medical record
- For teaching
- For publication
- Parental consent (specific)

## 9. HIE Therapeutic Hypothermia
- Eligibility criteria
- Risks: bradycardia, thrombosis, subcutaneous fat necrosis
- Benefits: reduced neuro-disability
- 72h protocol

## 10. Discharge
- Parent understanding
- Education topics
- Follow-up plan
- Caregiver capacity

## Special Considerations
- **Mother + father consent** (if married)
- **Single mother:** Mother only
- **Adoption:** Legal guardian
- **Foster:** Foster care authority
- **Cultural/religious:** Accommodation
- **Bilingual:** EN + AR

## Storage
- Encrypted at rest
- 7+ year retention
- Audit log
