# PEDS-002 — Clinical Red Flags

## Respiratory
- Apnea (>20s pause)
- Bradypnea (<30)
- Tachypnea (>60)
- Desaturation (<85% persistent)
- Severe retractions
- Grunting
- Stridor
- Cyanosis

## Cardiovascular
- Bradycardia (<100)
- Tachycardia (>200)
- Hypotension (BP < GA weeks)
- Poor perfusion (capillary refill >3s)
- Murmur (new)
- Heart failure signs

## Neurologic
- Lethargy
- Seizure
- Bulging fontanelle
- Hypotonia
- Hypertonia
- High-pitched cry
- Loss of consciousness

## GI / Feeding
- Bloody stools
- Bilious vomiting
- Abdominal distension
- Feeding intolerance
- NEC signs

## Infectious
- Fever (>38.0 in neonate = sepsis workup)
- Hypothermia (<36.0)
- Apnea (sepsis presentation)
- Lethargy

## Metabolic
- Hypoglycemia (<40 mg/dL)
- Hyperglycemia (>180)
- Hyperkalemia
- Hyponatremia
- Metabolic acidosis

## Hematologic
- Severe anemia (Hct <30)
- Bleeding (DIC)
- Jaundice (rapid rise)
- Thrombocytopenia

## Temperature
- Hypothermia (<36.0)
- Hyperthermia (>38.0)
