# PEDS-001 — General Pediatrics

## Mission
Comprehensive pediatric care: well-child visits, acute illness, chronic disease management, immunizations, developmental screening.

## Workflow
- Well-child visits (growth, development, vaccines)
- Acute visits (URI, fever, GI, etc.)
- Chronic disease management (asthma, diabetes, etc.)
- School + sports physicals
- Behavioral health screening

## Top Conditions
- URI (common cold)
- Acute otitis media
- Asthma (chronic)
- Allergic rhinitis
- Viral gastroenteritis
- Fever without source
- Bronchiolitis
- Croup
- Strep pharyngitis
- Hand-foot-mouth disease

## Red Flags
- Fever in infant <3 months (meningitis/sepsis risk)
- Febrile seizure (first, complex)
- Severe dehydration
- Anaphylaxis
- Respiratory distress
- Altered mental status
- Toxic appearance

## Drug Safety (Peds Critical)
- **ALL weight-based** (mg/kg)
- Double-check for high-alert (insulin, heparin, opioids)
- Avoid aspirin (Reye syndrome)
- Avoid tetracyclines <8 years
- Avoid fluoroquinolones <18 years

## AI Decision Support
- Age-specific dosing
- Vaccine schedule
- Growth chart tracking
- Developmental screening
