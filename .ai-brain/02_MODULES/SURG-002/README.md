# SURG-002 — Surgical Oncology

## Mission
Cancer surgery: breast, GI, hepatobiliary, pancreatic, melanoma, sarcoma. Often combined with medical + radiation oncology (MDT).

## Top Conditions
- Breast cancer (lumpectomy, mastectomy, SLNB)
- Colorectal cancer (colectomy, APR)
- Pancreatic cancer (Whipple)
- Hepatobiliary (liver resection, Whipple)
- Melanoma (WLE, SLNB, node dissection)
- Sarcoma (limb-sparing)
- Gastric cancer

## MDT (Multidisciplinary Team)
- Surgical oncologist
- Medical oncologist
- Radiation oncologist
- Pathologist
- Radiologist
- Nurse navigator
- Genetic counselor

## Red Flags
- Incomplete resection (positive margin)
- Lymph node involvement
- Metastatic disease
- Anastomotic leak (post-op)
- Recurrence on surveillance

## AI Decision Support

- SLNB identification
- Survival estimates
- Surveillance protocol
