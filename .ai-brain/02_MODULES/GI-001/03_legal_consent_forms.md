# GI-001 — Legal Consent

## 1. EGD Consent
- Risks: bleeding, perforation, aspiration, reaction to sedation

## 2. Colonoscopy Consent
- Bowel prep
- Polypectomy if needed
- Bleeding, perforation
- Sedation

## 3. ERCP Consent
- Indication
- Risks: pancreatitis (3-5%), bleeding, perforation, infection
- Sphincterotomy
- Stent placement
- Stone removal

## 4. EUS Consent
- Indication
- FNA
- Risks: bleeding, infection, perforation (rare)

## 5. Liver Biopsy Consent
- Indication
- Risks: bleeding, bile leak, infection

## 6. Paracentesis Consent
- Large-volume paracentesis
- Albumin replacement
- Risks: bleeding, infection, hypotension

## 7. TIPS Consent
- Indication
- Risks: hepatic encephalopathy, shunt dysfunction, bleeding

## 8. Endoscopic Mucosal Resection (EMR/ESD)
- Indication
- Risks: bleeding, perforation, stricture
- Histology

## 9. HCV Treatment Consent
- DAAs
- Duration
- SVR expectations
- Drug interactions
- Side effects

## 10. Biologic Consent
- Drug name
- Indication
- Infections risk
- TB screening
- Long-term

## Special
- Jehovah's Witness
- Pregnancy
- Coagulopathy (correct before)
- Sedation risk assessment

## Storage
- Encrypted
- 7+ years
- Audit log
