# GI-001 — Clinical Workflows

## Top 10
1. GERD
2. PUD
3. GI bleeding
4. IBD
5. IBS

## Workflow
1. Symptom assessment
2. Endoscopy (EGD, colonoscopy)
3. Imaging (US, CT, MRI)
4. Biopsy
5. Treatment

## GI Bleed Management
- Resuscitation (2 large-bore IVs, type & cross)
- Risk stratify (GBS, AIMS65)
- IV PPI
- EGD within 12-24h
- Variceal band ligation / sclerotherapy
- Hemoclips / thermal coagulation
- Antibiotic prophylaxis (cirrhosis)

## IBD
- 5-ASA (mild)
- Steroids (moderate-severe)
- Immunomodulator (AZA, 6-MP, MTX)
- Biologics (anti-TNF, anti-integrin, anti-IL12/23)
- JAK inhibitors (UC)
- Surgery (UC: colectomy; Crohn: resection)

## Cirrhosis
- **Child-Pugh:** bilirubin, albumin, INR, ascites, encephalopathy
- **MELD-Na:** score, transplant priority
- **Surveillance:** HCC (US q6m), varices (EGD)

## Pancreatitis
- **Severity:** BISAP, Ranson
- **Treatment:** IV fluids, NPO, pain control
- **Severe:** ICU, antibiotics if necrotic

## Red Flags
- Massive GI bleed
- Perforation
- Acute cholangitis
- Acute liver failure
- Severe pancreatitis
- Bowel ischemia
- Toxic megacolon
