# GI-001 — Red Flags

## GI Bleed
- Massive (>2L in 24h)
- Hemodynamic instability
- Re-bleed
- Variceal
- Aorto-enteric fistula

## Hepatic
- Hepatic encephalopathy (any grade)
- Acute liver failure (INR >1.5 + encephalopathy)
- Spontaneous bacterial peritonitis (SBP)
- Hepatorenal syndrome
- Variceal bleed
- Hepatopulmonary syndrome
- Acute-on-chronic liver failure

## Pancreas
- Severe acute (BISAP ≥3)
- Hemorrhagic pancreatitis
- Necrotizing
- Pseudocyst
- Chronic with complications

## IBD
- Toxic megacolon
- Perforation
- Massive bleed
- Refractory flare

## Biliary
- Charcot triad (cholangitis)
- Reynolds pentad (severe)
- Bouveret syndrome

## Other
- Mesenteric ischemia
- Bowel perforation
- Esophageal perforation (Boerhaave)
- Peritonitis
