# GI-001 — Middleware

## Roles
- `gastroenterologist`
- `hepatologist`
- `gi_nurse`
- `endoscopy_tech`
- `transplant_coordinator`
- `pharmacist` (biologics)

## High-Alert Meds
- Biologics (anti-TNF, etc.)
- TIPS procedure
- ERCP with sphincterotomy
- Sedation

## Audit
- All endoscopies
- Liver biopsies
- MELD scores
- TIPS procedures
- Biologic administrations
