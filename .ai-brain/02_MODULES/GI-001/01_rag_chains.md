# GI-001 — RAG Chains (Detailed)

## Chain 1: Massive GI Bleed
- HR >100, SBP <90, syncope
- Resuscitation (IV, blood)
- IV PPI
- Octreotide (if variceal suspected)
- Antibiotic (if cirrhotic)
- EGD within 12h (or emergency)

## Chain 2: Hepatic Encephalopathy
- West Haven grade
- Trigger: infection, GI bleed, electrolyte, sedatives
- Lactulose
- Rifaximin
- Treat trigger

## Chain 3: SBP (Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis)
- Ascitic fluid analysis
- PMN ≥250
- IV ceftriaxone
- Albumin (1.5 g/kg day 1, 1 g/kg day 3)

## Chain 4: Acute Cholangitis
- Charcot triad (fever, jaundice, RUQ pain)
- Reynolds pentad (add shock, encephalopathy)
- ERCP within 24-48h
- Antibiotics

## Chain 5: IBD Flare
- UC: partial Mayo
- Crohn: CDAI, CRP, calprotectin
- Steroid
- Biologic escalation
- Surgery if refractory
