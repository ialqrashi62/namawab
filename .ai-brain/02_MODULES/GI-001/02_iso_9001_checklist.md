# GI-001 — ISO 9001

## QMS
- SOPs: bleed, IBD, cirrhosis, hepatitis, pancreatitis, ERCP
- KPIs: mortality, readmission, HCV SVR, ERCP success, ADR
- Audits: quarterly
- Improvement: CAPA

## Quality Indicators
- GI bleed mortality
- Cirrhosis 30-day readmission
- IBD remission rate
- HCV SVR12 (>95% target)
- HBV DNA suppression
- ERCP success (>90%)
- Colonoscopy ADR (>25%)
- HCC surveillance compliance (>80%)
- TIPS success rate
- 30-day readmission
- ERCP post-ERCP pancreatitis (<5%)
- Endoscopy complications

## Audits
- Quarterly internal
- Annual external

## Improvement
- CAPA
- M&M conferences
- Lessons learned
- Continual improvement
