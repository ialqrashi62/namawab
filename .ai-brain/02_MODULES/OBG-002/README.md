# OBG-002 — Maternal-Fetal Medicine (High-Risk Pregnancy)

## Mission
High-risk pregnancy care: pre-existing maternal disease, fetal anomalies, multiple gestation, recurrent pregnancy loss, prenatal diagnosis.

## Top Conditions
- Pre-gestational diabetes
- Chronic hypertension
- Preeclampsia (recurrent)
- Multiple gestation (twins, triplets)
- Fetal anomalies (chromosomal, structural)
- Recurrent pregnancy loss
- Preterm labor history
- Antiphospholipid syndrome
- Maternal cardiac disease

## Workflow
- Detailed US (anomaly scan, growth, Doppler)
- Genetic counseling + testing (NIPT, CVS, amnio)
- Serial monitoring (growth, Doppler, NST, BPP)
- Delivery planning (timing, mode, location)

## Red Flags
- Fetal growth restriction
- Pre-eclampsia (early onset)
- PPROM (preterm premature rupture of membranes)
- Fetal distress
- Twin-to-twin transfusion syndrome
- Placenta accreta spectrum

## AI Decision Support
- Preeclampsia risk (ASPRE)
- Growth chart tracking
- Doppler interpretation
- Genetic risk calculation

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: FGR, pre-eclampsia, TTTS
- Drug safety: teratogens, anticoagulants
- PHI: encrypted (genetic data)
- Auth: MFM specialist
- Compliance: JCI, SMFM, CBAHI
- Tests: growth tracking, Doppler
