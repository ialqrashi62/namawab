# HH-001 — Home Healthcare

## Mission
Medical care at home: skilled nursing, physical therapy, home health aide, hospice, telemonitoring.

## Top Services
- Wound care
- IV therapy (ABX, hydration, TPN)
- Tube feeding
- Tracheostomy care
- Ventilator support (home vent)
- Post-op care
- Hospice
- Telemonitoring (chronic disease)

## Eligibility
- Homebound
- Skilled need
- MD order

## Red Flags
- Patient safety (fall, abuse, neglect)
- Deterioration (need higher level of care)
- Medication error
- Caregiver burnout
- Equipment failure (vent, O2)

- Red flags: deterioration, abuse
- Drug safety: home med safety
- PHI: encrypted (home visit notes)
- Auth: Home health nurse
- Compliance: JCI, CBAHI, MoH
