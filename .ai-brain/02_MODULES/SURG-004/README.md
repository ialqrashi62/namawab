# SURG-004 — Trauma Surgery

## Mission
Operative management of trauma: damage control laparotomy, thoracotomy, vascular control, fasciotomy.

## Indications
- Hemodynamic instability + positive FAST
- Peritonitis
- Penetrating abdominal trauma
- Pneumothorax / hemothorax requiring thoracotomy
- Vascular injury
- Compartment syndrome (fasciotomy)

## Damage Control Surgery
1. Initial operation (stop bleeding, control contamination)
2. ICU resuscitation (rewarming, correction of coagulopathy)
3. Re-operation (definitive repair)

- Red flags: ongoing hemorrhage, missed injury
- Drug safety: MTP, antibiotics
