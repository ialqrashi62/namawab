# SLEEP-001 — Sleep Medicine

## Mission
Sleep disorders: OSA, insomnia, parasomnia, narcolepsy, circadian rhythm disorders, RLS, sleep apnea in children.

## Top Conditions
- OSA (obstructive sleep apnea)
- Insomnia
- Restless leg syndrome (RLS)
- Narcolepsy
- Parasomnia (REM behavior disorder)
- Shift work disorder
- Pediatric sleep apnea

## Workflow
- Sleep history
- Sleep diary
- Questionnaires (Epworth, STOP-BANG, PSQI)
- Polysomnography (PSG) — in-lab
- Home sleep test (HST) — for uncomplicated OSA
- Treatment (CPAP, oral appliance, surgery, behavioral)

## Red Flags
- Severe OSA (AHI >30)
- Obesity hypoventilation
- Nocturnal seizures
- Narcolepsy with cataplexy
- REM behavior disorder (PD prodrome)
- Sudden infant death risk (peds)

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: severe OSA, RBD
- Drug safety: hypnotics, modafinil
- PHI: encrypted (sleep study)
- Auth: Sleep medicine physician
- Compliance: JCI, AASM
- Tests: PSG interpretation, CPAP titration
