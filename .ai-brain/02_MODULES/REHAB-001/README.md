# REHAB-001 — Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation

## Mission
Rehabilitation: physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, prosthetics, orthotics, post-stroke, post-amputation, post-TBI, spinal cord injury.

## Top Conditions
- Stroke (post-acute)
- Spinal cord injury
- Traumatic brain injury
- Amputation
- Joint replacement
- Sports injury
- Back pain
- Pediatric (CP, developmental)

## Workflow
- Referral (post-acute or chronic)
- Multidisciplinary assessment
- Goal setting (SMART)
- Therapy plan (PT, OT, ST)
- Progress monitoring (FIM, Barthel)
- Discharge planning
