# GEN-001 — Medical Genetics

## Mission
Genetic diagnosis and counseling: prenatal, pediatric, adult, cancer genetics, pharmacogenomics.

## Top Services
- **Prenatal:** NIPT, CVS, amniocentesis, carrier screening
- **Pediatric:** dysmorphic, intellectual disability, metabolic
- **Cancer:** hereditary cancer (BRCA, Lynch, Li-Fraumeni)
- **Adult:** Huntington, familial hyperlipidemia, hemochromatosis
- **Pharmacogenomics:** CYP450, TPMT, DPYD

## Workflow
- Referral
- Genetic counseling
- Testing (panel, exome, genome)
- Result interpretation
- Risk assessment
- Recommendations
- Family cascade testing

## Red Flags
- Positive BRCA1/2
- Lynch syndrome
- Trisomy 18/13
- Spinal muscular atrophy (Type 1)
- Trisomy X fragile
- Hemoglobinopathy
- Unexpected paternity
- VUS (variant of uncertain significance) counseling

## AI Decision Support
- Variant interpretation (ACMG)
- Drug-gene interaction
- Risk model
