# SURG-006 — Hepatobiliary & Pancreatic Surgery

## Mission
HPB surgery: liver resection, Whipple (pancreaticoduodenectomy), distal pancreatectomy, bile duct surgery, transplant.

## Top Conditions
- Liver tumors (HCC, mets, adenoma)
- Pancreatic cancer
- Bile duct cancer (cholangiocarcinoma)
- Pancreatitis complications (pseudocyst, necrosis)
- Gallbladder cancer
- Liver cysts

## Whipple Procedure
- Pancreaticoduodenectomy for pancreatic head mass
- High-risk (mortality 2-5% in expert centers)
- Major complications: pancreatic fistula, delayed gastric emptying, bleeding

## Red Flags
- Post-op pancreatic fistula (ISGPS)
- Hemorrhage
- Bile leak
- Liver failure (post-resection)
- Delayed gastric emptying

- Red flags: fistula, hemorrhage
- Drug safety: pancreatic enzyme, anticoag
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: HPB surgeon
- Compliance: JCI, AHPBA
- Tests: fistula grading, complications
