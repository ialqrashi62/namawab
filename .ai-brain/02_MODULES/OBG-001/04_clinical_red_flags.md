# OBG-001 — Clinical Red Flags

## Pregnancy — Obstetric Emergencies
- **Eclampsia** (seizure in pregnancy)
- **Placental abruption** (painful bleeding, tense uterus, fetal distress)
- **Placenta previa** (painless bleeding)
- **Uterine rupture** (sudden severe pain, fetal distress, loss of station)
- **Cord prolapse** (visible/palpable cord, fetal bradycardia)
- **Shoulder dystocia** (turtle sign)
- **Postpartum hemorrhage** (EBL >500 mL SVD, >1000 mL C-section)
- **Amniotic fluid embolism** (sudden dyspnea, hypotension, DIC)
- **HELLP syndrome** (platelets <100k, AST >70, hemolysis)

## Pregnancy — Hypertension
- BP ≥160/110 (severe)
- BP ≥140/90 + proteinuria (preeclampsia)
- Headache, visual disturbance, epigastric pain (severe features)
- Platelets <100k
- AST/ALT >2x normal
- Creatinine >1.1

## Pregnancy — GDM
- Glucose ≥200 (GDM by 50g screen)
- Persistent fasting >95, 2h >120 (poor control)
- Fetal macrosomia (>4 kg)
- Polyhydramnios

## Fetal Distress
- FHR <110 or >160 (tachycardia or bradycardia)
- Late decelerations
- Variable decelerations (recurrent)
- Decreased fetal movement
- Category II/III FHR tracing

## Postpartum
- PPH (EBL >500 mL)
- Endometritis (fever, uterine tenderness, foul lochia)
- Wound infection
- DVT/PE
- Postpartum depression (suicidal ideation)
- Mastitis

## Gyn — Oncologic
- Postmenopausal bleeding
- Adnexal mass with concerning features
- Cervical mass (visible)
- Vulvar lesion (non-healing)

## Gyn — Acute
- Ovarian torsion (sudden severe pain, mass)
- Ectopic pregnancy (severe pain, βhCG)
- Ruptured ovarian cyst
- Severe PID (Tubo-ovarian abscess)

## Neonatal (delivery room)
- Apgar 5-min <7
- Meconium staining
- Shoulder dystocia
- Cord prolapse
- Newborn not breathing
