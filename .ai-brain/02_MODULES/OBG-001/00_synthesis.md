# OBG-001 7-Expert Synthesis (L1-L4)

## Mission
Comprehensive women's health: pregnancy care, gynecology, reproductive medicine. **Maternal safety is the highest priority.**

## Top 10 Red Flags (CMO veto)
1. **Eclampsia** (seizures in pregnancy) → MgSO4 within 5 min
2. **Severe pre-eclampsia** (BP ≥160/110, end-organ) → MgSO4 + delivery
3. **Placental abruption** (painful bleeding) → emergency C-section
4. **Placenta previa** (painless bleeding) → NO digital exam
5. **Ectopic rupture** (hypotension + abdominal pain + hCG+) → OR in 15 min
6. **Postpartum hemorrhage** (>500mL EBL) → uterotonics in 5 min
7. **HELLP** (platelets <100, AST/ALT >70) → delivery
8. **Cord prolapse** → delivery in 30 min
9. **Uterine rupture** → emergency laparotomy
10. **Amniotic fluid embolism** → resuscitation, ICU

6. **OB/GYN surgery** — scheduled + emergency

## Drug Safety (Critical)
- **Magnesium sulfate** — toxicity monitor (DTR, RR, urine output)
- **Oxytocin** — never bolus, titrate only
- **Teratogens** — block in pregnancy (ACE-i, warfarin, methotrexate, valproate, isotretinoin)
- **Misoprostol** — correct dose, indication, route
- **Latex allergy** — common in OB patients, prepare non-latex equipment
- **Methotrexate** — contraindicated in breastfeeding
