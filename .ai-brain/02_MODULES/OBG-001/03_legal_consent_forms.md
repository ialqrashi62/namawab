# OBG-001 — Legal Consent Forms

## 1. General OBG Care Consent
- Routine prenatal care
- Lab tests
- Ultrasound
- Education

## 2. Delivery Consent (SVD)
- Spontaneous vaginal delivery
- Episiotomy if needed
- Vacuum/forceps if needed
- Blood transfusion if needed

## 3. Cesarean Section Consent
- Indication
- Risks: bleeding, infection, injury to surrounding organs, future pregnancy risks
- Anesthesia
- Blood transfusion
- Hysterectomy if life-threatening
- Partner presence

## 4. Epidural Consent
- Anesthesia type
- Risks: hypotension, dural puncture, failure
- Benefits: pain relief

## 5. Vacuum / Forceps Delivery Consent
- Indication
- Risks: scalp injury, cephalohematoma, facial nerve palsy, fetal injury
- Alternative: C-section
- Trainee involvement

## 6. Blood Transfusion Consent (OB)
- Indication
- Risks: TRALI, TACO, infection
- Alternatives: cell saver (in C-section), EPO, withholding

## 7. Sterilization Consent (Tubal Ligation)
- Permanent
- Waiting period (30 days)
- Risks: failure (rare), ectopic
- Spousal consent (KSA: required)

## 8. Termination Consent (if legally applicable)
- KSA: only for maternal life or fetal lethal anomaly
- Religious/cultural counseling
- Two-doctor consensus
- Committee approval
- Documentation

## 9. IVF Consent
- Procedure, risks (OHSS, multiple gestation)
- Success rates (age-dependent)
- Cryopreservation of embryos
- Disposition of stored gametes/embryos
- Genetic testing (if applicable)
- Donor gametes (if applicable)
- Cost

## 10. Newborn Procedures Consent
- Vitamin K
- Hepatitis B vaccine
- Eye prophylaxis (erythromycin)
- Newborn screening (heel prick)
- Hearing screening
- Circumcision (if requested, religious)

## 11. PPH Interventions Consent (verbal + documented)
- Bimanual massage
- Uterotonics
- Bakri balloon
- Surgical (B-Lynch, artery ligation, hysterectomy)

## Special Considerations
- **Adolescent pregnancy:** Guardian consent + minor assent
- **Mental capacity assessment** before consent
- **Witness** for illiterate patients
- **Translation** if needed
- **Cultural/religious accommodation**
- **Bilingual** (EN + AR)

## Storage
- Encrypted at rest
- 7-year retention (or longer for maternal/child)
- Audit log of access
