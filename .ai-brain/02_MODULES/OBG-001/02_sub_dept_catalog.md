# OBG-001 — Sub-Department Catalog

## Sub-Units
1. **OBG-001-ANC** — Antenatal Care
2. **OBG-001-LD** — Labor & Delivery (L&D)
3. **OBG-001-PNC** — Postnatal Care
4. **OBG-001-MFM** — Maternal-Fetal Medicine
5. **OBG-001-GYN** — Gynecology
6. **OBG-001-ONC** — Gyn-Oncology
7. **OBG-001-REI** — Reproductive Endocrinology & IVF
8. **OBG-001-UROGYN** — Urogynecology
9. **OBG-001-MIN** — Minimally Invasive (Laparoscopy, Hysteroscopy)

## Per-Subunit Top Issues
- **ANC:** Routine prenatal, screening
- **L&D:** Active labor, vaginal delivery, C-section
- **PNC:** Postpartum follow-up, breastfeeding, contraception
- **MFM:** High-risk pregnancy, fetal anomalies
- **GYN:** Menstrual disorders, contraception, menopause
- **ONC:** Cervical, ovarian, uterine cancer
- **REI:** Infertility, IVF
- **UROGYN:** Pelvic floor, prolapse, incontinence
- **MIN:** Laparoscopy, hysteroscopy

## Cross-Cutting
- Family planning
- STI screening
- Cervical cancer screening (Pap, HPV)
- Breast cancer screening
- Domestic violence screening
