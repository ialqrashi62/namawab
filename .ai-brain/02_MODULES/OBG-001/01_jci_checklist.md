# OBG-001 — JCI + ISO 9001 + Compliance

## JCI 7th Edition — OBG-Specific

### International Patient Safety Goals
- [ ] IPSG.1 — Patient identification (mother + newborn)
- [ ] IPSG.2 — Read-back for critical results
- [ ] IPSG.3 — High-alert medications (MgSO4, oxytocin, insulin, anticoag)
- [ ] IPSG.4 — WHO surgical checklist (C-section)
- [ ] IPSG.5 — SSI prevention (C-section)
- [ ] IPSG.6 — Fall risk in pregnancy

### Care of Patients (COP)
- [ ] MgSO4 for preeclampsia (4g load, 1-2g/h maintenance)
- [ ] Oxytocin for postpartum (10U IV, then 20U/L drip)
- [ ] Antibiotic prophylaxis for C-section (cefazolin)
- [ ] Skin-to-skin + breastfeeding within 1h
- [ ] Vitamin K to newborn
- [ ] Hepatitis B vaccine to newborn

### Quality Improvement
- [ ] C-section rate (target <30%)
- [ ] VBAC success rate
- [ ] PPH rate
- [ ] Eclampsia rate
- [ ] Perinatal mortality rate
- [ ] Apgar <7 at 5 min
- [ ] Exclusive breastfeeding at discharge

## ISO 9001 — QMS
- SOPs for each procedure
- Risk register
- Internal audits
- CAPA process
- KPI monitoring

## PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law)
- **Pregnancy data:** Special category (highest protection)
- **Field-level encryption:** HIV, hepatitis, syphilis
- **Access control:** Restricted to OB team
- **Audit log:** All access logged
- **Cross-border:** Restricted (KSA-based)
- **Patient rights:** Access, correction, portability

## NPHIES (Insurance)
- Eligibility verification
- Pre-authorization (C-section, infertility)
- Bundle codes (DRG)
- Medical necessity documentation
- Appeal process

## CBAHI (Saudi)
- OB/GYN department standards
- Maternal + perinatal mortality reporting
- Quality indicators
- Sentinel event reporting
- Near-miss reporting

## MOH (Ministry of Health)
- License for OB/GYN department
- License for L&D
- License for IVF (if applicable)
- Maternal death notification (within 24h)
- Perinatal death notification
- Disease notification (HIV, hepatitis, syphilis)

## Sentinel Events (must report)
- Maternal death
- Perinatal death
- Severe PPH requiring hysterectomy
- Eclampsia
- Uterine rupture
- Amniotic fluid embolism
- Neonatal death
- Birth asphyxia (Apgar <4 at 5 min)
