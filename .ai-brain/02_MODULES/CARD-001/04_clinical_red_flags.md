# CARD-001 — Clinical Red Flags

## Critical (Time-Sensitive)
- STEMI (ST elevation in 2+ contiguous leads)
- Cardiogenic shock
- Cardiac tamponade
- Aortic dissection
- Massive pulmonary embolism
- Sustained VT
- Complete heart block
- Anaphylaxis (cardiac)
- Hypertensive emergency
- Acute aortic syndrome

## Urgent
- NSTEMI
- Unstable angina
- Symptomatic bradycardia
- New AF with rapid response
- Acute heart failure
- Symptomatic severe AS
- Endocarditis with embolic phenomena
- Myocarditis with hemodynamic compromise
- Pericarditis with effusion

## Concerning (Watch)
- Elevated troponin (small)
- Chest pain with intermediate HEART score
- AF with controlled rate
- Stable HF
- Stable angina

## ECG Patterns to Never Miss
- STEMI (anterior, inferior, lateral, posterior, RV)
- Wellens syndrome (critical LAD)
- De Winter T (anterior STEMI equivalent)
- Hyperacute T waves
- New LBBB
- Brugada pattern
- Long QT
- AF with WPW
- Tricyclic overdose pattern
- Hyperkalemia progression
