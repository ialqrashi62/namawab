# CARD-001 — Sub-Department Catalog

## Sub-Units
1. **CARD-001-CCU** — Coronary Care Unit
2. **CARD-001-INVASIVE** — Cardiac catheterization lab
3. **CARD-001-NONINVASIVE** — Echo, stress, EKG
4. **CARD-001-EP** — Electrophysiology
5. **CARD-001-DEVICES** — Pacemaker/ICD clinic
6. **CARD-001-AMB** — Outpatient clinic
7. **CARD-001-CHEST_PAIN** — Chest pain unit
8. **CARD-001-STRUCTURAL** — TAVR, MitraClip, ASD closure
9. **CARD-001-PREHOSP** — Pre-hospital ECG (EMS)

## Per-Subunit
- **CCU:** Acute MI, cardiogenic shock, post-PCI
- **INVASIVE:** Diagnostic cath, PCI, structural
- **NONINVASIVE:** Echo, TEE, stress, nuclear
- **EP:** EP study, ablation, devices
- **DEVICES:** Pacemaker/ICD follow-up
- **AMB:** Outpatient
- **CHEST_PAIN:** Triage
- **STRUCTURAL:** TAVR, MitraClip
- **PREHOSP:** Pre-hospital ECG transmission
