# CARD-001 — ISO 9001 (Detailed)

## QMS Scope
- All cardiac care: inpatient, outpatient
- Diagnostic (ECG, echo, stress, cath)
- Therapeutic (meds, PCI, devices, surgery)
- Preventive (cardiac rehab, education)

## Quality Objectives
- Door-to-balloon median <60 min
- D2B >90 min <5%
- STEMI mortality <5%
- HF readmission (30 days) <20%
- AF anticoag compliance >90%
- Bleeding (major) <3%
- PCI success >95%
- Patient satisfaction >4.5/5

## Process Maps
- Chest pain → ECG → cath lab → CCU → discharge
- HF → admit → stabilization → GDMT → discharge → clinic
- AF → confirm → anticoag → rate/rhythm → follow-up
- Syncope → workup → diagnosis → treatment

## Risk Register
- STEMI miss
- Bleeding from anticoag
- Stent thrombosis
- Device failure
- Contrast nephropathy
- Vascular access complication
- Wrong procedure

## Internal Audits
- Monthly: D2B
- Quarterly: HF readmission, AF anticoag
- Semi-annual: PCI outcomes
- Annual: full

## Management Review
- Annual
- KPIs
- Risk register
- CAPA
- Improvement actions

## Improvement
- CAPA
- Lessons learned
- M&M conferences
- Continual improvement
