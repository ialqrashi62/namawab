# CARD-001 — Cardiology Clinical Workflows

## Top 10 Conditions
1. **Acute coronary syndrome (STEMI, NSTEMI, UA)**
2. **Heart failure** (HFrEF, HFpEF)
3. **Atrial fibrillation**
4. **Hypertension**
5. **Valvular heart disease** (AS, MR, AR, MS)
6. **Cardiomyopathy** (DCM, HCM, RCM)
7. **Pulmonary embolism**
8. **Pericarditis**
9. **Endocarditis**
10. **Syncope**

## Workflow
1. Clinical assessment
2. ECG (within 10 min for chest pain)
3. Troponin (0h, 3h)
4. Echo
5. Stress test
6. Cardiac catheterization
7. Treatment (medication, PCI, CABG, devices)

## Risk Stratification
- **TIMI score** (NSTEMI)
- **GRACE score** (ACS)
- **HEART score** (chest pain)
- **CHA2DS2-VASc** (AF stroke risk)
- **HAS-BLED** (AF bleeding risk)
- **Killip class** (HF in MI)

## Acute MI Management
- **STEMI:**
  - Primary PCI (door-to-balloon <90 min)
  - If PCI not available: fibrinolysis (door-to-needle <30 min)
  - Aspirin 325 mg
  - P2Y12 (clopidogrel, ticagrelor, prasugrel)
  - Anticoag (heparin, bivalirudin)
  - Statin
  - Beta-blocker (within 24h)
  - ACE-I/ARB (within 24h)
- **NSTEMI:**
  - Risk stratify (TIMI/GRACE)
  - Aspirin + P2Y12
  - Anticoag
  - Statin
  - Invasive vs conservative
  - Cath within 24-72h (high risk)

## Heart Failure Management
- **Acute decompensated:**
  - IV diuretics
  - Vasodilators (nitroglycerin)
  - Inotropes (dobutamine, milrinone)
  - Ultrafiltration (refractory)
- **Chronic HFrEF:**
  - ACE-I or ARB or ARNI (sacubitril/valsartan)
  - Beta-blocker (carvedilol, metoprolol, bisoprolol)
  - MRA (spironolactone, eplerenone)
  - SGLT2i (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin)
  - Diuretic as needed

## AF Management
- **Acute:** rate control (BB, CCB, digoxin) vs rhythm control (amiodarone, cardioversion)
- **Chronic:** rate control + anticoagulation
- **Anticoag:** CHA2DS2-VASc ≥2 (men) or ≥3 (women) → anticoag
- **DOAC preferred:** apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, edoxaban
- **Warfarin:** if mechanical valve, severe MS, CrCl <30

## Red Flags
- STEMI
- Cardiogenic shock
- Cardiac tamponade
- Aortic dissection
- Pulmonary embolism (massive)
- Complete heart block
- VF / sustained VT
- Anaphylaxis (cardiac)
- Hypertensive emergency

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: STEMI, shock, dissection
- Drug safety: high-alert (anticoag, inotrope)
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: Cardiologist
- Compliance: JCI, ACC/AHA
- Tests: HEART, CHA2DS2-VASc, ECG
