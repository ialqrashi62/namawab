# CARD-001 — JCI + ISO 9001

## JCI 7th Edition

### Patient Safety
- [ ] IPSG.1 — Patient ID
- [ ] IPSG.3 — High-alert meds (anticoag, inotrope, IV antiarrhythmic)
- [ ] IPSG.4 — Site marking (vascular access)
- [ ] IPSG.5 — HAI prevention
- [ ] Falls risk (HF, syncope)

### Care of Patients
- [ ] Door-to-ECG <10 min
- [ ] Door-to-balloon <90 min
- [ ] Heparin loading before PCI
- [ ] Aspirin + P2Y12
- [ ] Statin at discharge
- [ ] Smoking cessation counseling
- [ ] Cardiac rehab referral
- [ ] HF education (weight, diet, meds)

### Quality
- [ ] Door-to-balloon time
- [ ] PCI volume / operator
- [ ] In-hospital mortality (AMI, HF)
- [ ] 30-day readmission (HF)
- [ ] Bleeding (anticoag)
- [ ] Stent thrombosis
- [ ] Major adverse cardiac events (MACE)
- [ ] D2B >90 min rate

## ISO 9001

### SOPs
- Chest pain triage
- STEMI activation
- PCI procedure
- HF management
- AF management
- Device implant
- Device follow-up
- Anticoagulation clinic
- Cardiac rehab
- Echo lab
- Cath lab

### KPIs
- Door-to-ECG <10 min (>95%)
- Door-to-balloon <90 min (>90%)
- STEMI mortality
- HF readmission (30 days)
- Bleeding (major)
- Device complication rate
- Echo quality (completeness)
- Stress test quality

### Audits
- Monthly: D2B time
- Quarterly: HF readmission
- Annual: full

### Improvement
- CAPA
- Mortality + morbidity conferences
- Lessons learned
- Continual improvement
