# CARD-001 — Legal Consent

## 1. General Cardiology Consent
- EKG, echo, stress test
- Routine care
- Lab tests
- Education

## 2. PCI Consent
- Indication
- Procedure: access site (radial/femoral), cath, angioplasty, stent
- Risks: bleeding, vascular injury, MI, stroke, death, contrast reaction, restenosis, stent thrombosis
- Alternatives: medical, CABG
- Sedation

## 3. CABG Consent
- Indication
- Procedure: sternotomy, grafts (LIMA, RIMA, radial, saphenous), CPB
- Risks: bleeding, infection, MI, stroke, AF, cognitive decline, death
- On-pump vs off-pump
- ICU post-op

## 4. TEE Consent
- Probe insertion
- Risks: esophageal injury, bleeding, aspiration, arrhythmia
- Sedation

## 5. Cardioversion Consent
- Indication
- Procedure: pads, sedation, shock
- Risks: arrhythmia, embolic stroke (if AF >48h), skin burn

## 6. Pacemaker Implant Consent
- Indication
- Procedure: pocket, leads
- Risks: pneumothorax, lead dislodgement, infection, hematoma, perforation
- Battery life (5-15 years)
- Restrictions (MRI conditional, etc.)

## 7. ICD Implant Consent
- Indication (primary vs secondary)
- Procedure
- Risks: inappropriate shock, lead fracture, infection
- Driving restrictions (varies)
- Deactivation (if terminal)

## 8. EP Study + Ablation Consent
- Indication
- Procedure: catheters, mapping
- Risks: vascular injury, perforation, tamponade, AV block, stroke, death
- Success rates
- Recurrence possible

## 9. TAVR Consent
- Indication (severe AS, high surgical risk)
- Procedure: transfemoral, transapical, etc.
- Risks: stroke, vascular injury, paravalvular leak, pacemaker, death
- Valve-in-valve (future)

## 10. Anticoagulation Consent
- Drug name
- Indication
- Duration
- Risks: bleeding
- Monitoring (warfarin, INR)
- Reversal agent
- Missed dose
- Diet (vitamin K)

## Special Considerations
- Emergency (STEMI): consent can be deferred if patient unable
- Jehovah's Witness: blood transfusion (specific consent)
- Pregnancy: contrast (caution)
- Renal failure: contrast risk
- Allergies: contrast, anticoag

## Storage
- Encrypted
- 7+ year retention
- Audit log
