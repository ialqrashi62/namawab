# CARD-001 — PDPL + NPHIES

## PDPL
- Cardiac imaging: encrypted
- ECG stored as PDF + structured
- 7-year audit
- Patient access (request)
- Cross-border: restricted

## NPHIES
- Eligibility check
- Pre-authorization (PCI, CABG, TAVR, devices)
- Bundle codes (DRG)
- CPT codes
- Medical necessity
- Pre + post procedure documentation

## CBAHI
- Cardiology standards
- Door-to-balloon compliance
- Mortality reporting
- Sentinel event reporting

## MOH
- Cardiac surgery license
- Cath lab license
- Device implant license
- Cardiologist credentials
- Outcome reporting

## Sentinel Events
- Wrong-site PCI
- Wrong patient PCI
- Intra-op death (PCI, CABG)
- Stroke during procedure
- Massive bleeding
- Tamponade
- Aortic dissection (cath complication)
- Anaphylaxis to contrast
- Stent thrombosis (acute)
- Pacemaker lead perforation

## Quality Indicators
- D2B time (median)
- D2B >90 min (rate)
- STEMI mortality (in-hospital, 30-day)
- PCI success rate
- Stent thrombosis rate
- Bleeding (BARC 3+)
- HF readmission (30-day)
- AF anticoag compliance
- MACE (12-month)
- Device infection rate
