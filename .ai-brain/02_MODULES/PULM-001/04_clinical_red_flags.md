# PULM-001 — Red Flags

## Respiratory Failure
- SpO2 <88% on RA or escalating O2
- RR >35 or <8
- Accessory muscle use, paradoxical breathing
- Cyanosis
- Altered mental status

## Tension Pneumothorax
- Hypotension + absent breath sounds + distended neck veins
- **Emergency:** needle decompression (2nd ICS, midclavicular) + chest tube

## Massive PE
- Hypotension
- Hypoxia
- Right heart strain (echo)
- **Thrombolysis indicated** if no contraindication

## Status Asthmaticus
- Silent chest (worst sign)
- Normal/high PaCO2 (respiratory failure)
- Exhaustion
- Altered mental status

## ARDS
- Acute onset
- Bilateral infiltrates
- P/F <300 (Berlin)
- Not explained by cardiac failure

## Massive Hemoptysis
- >200 mL in 24h
- Airway protection priority
- Bronchoscopy
- IR embolization
