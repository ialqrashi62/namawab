# PULM-001 — Clinical Workflows

## Top 10 Conditions
1. **COPD** (chronic)
2. **Asthma**
3. **Pulmonary embolism**
4. **Pneumonia**
5. **Lung cancer**
6. **ILD (interstitial lung disease)**
7. **Pleural effusion**
8. **Pneumothorax**
9. **OSA**
10. **Pulmonary hypertension**

## Workflow
1. Clinical assessment
2. PFT (spirometry, DLCO, lung volumes)
3. Imaging (CXR, CT, PET)
4. Bronchoscopy
5. Biopsy
6. Staging + treatment

## PFT Interpretation
- **Obstructive:** FEV1/FVC <0.7 (COPD, asthma)
- **Restrictive:** FVC <80% (ILD, neuromuscular)
- **Mixed**
- **Severity:** based on FEV1% predicted

## COPD (GOLD)
- **Stage 1 (mild):** FEV1 ≥80% — SABA PRN
- **Stage 2 (moderate):** 50-79% — LABA or LAMA
- **Stage 3 (severe):** 30-49% — LABA + LAMA
- **Stage 4 (very severe):** <30% — LABA + LAMA + ICS (if eosinophilic)

## Asthma (GINA)
- **Step 1:** SABA PRN (or low-dose ICS-formoterol)
- **Step 2:** Low-dose ICS
- **Step 3:** Low-dose ICS-LABA
- **Step 4:** Medium-dose ICS-LABA
- **Step 5:** High-dose ICS-LABA + LAMA + biologic

## PE Workflow
1. Wells score
2. D-dimer (if low)
3. CTPA
4. Anticoagulation
5. Thrombolysis (massive)

## Red Flags
- Tension pneumothorax
- Massive PE
- Status asthmaticus
- Acute respiratory failure (any cause)
- Massive hemoptysis (>200 mL in 24h)
- ARDS
- Anaphylaxis (respiratory)
