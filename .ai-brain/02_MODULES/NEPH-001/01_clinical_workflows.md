## KDIGO CKD Staging
- G1: ≥90
- G2: 60-89
- G3a: 45-59
- G3b: 30-44
- G4: 15-29
- G5: <15 (dialysis)

## AKI (KDIGO)
- Stage 1: Cr ↑ ≥0.3 or 1.5-1.9× baseline
- Stage 2: Cr 2-2.9× baseline
- Stage 3: Cr ≥3× baseline or ≥4 mg/dL or RRT

## RRT Indications (AEIOU)
- **A**cidosis (severe)
- **E**lectrolyte (hyperkalemia)
- **I**ngestion (toxic)
- **O**verload (fluid)
- **U**remia

## Red Flags
- Hyperkalemia (>6.5)
- Severe metabolic acidosis
- Uremic complications
- Pulmonary edema
- Pericarditis
