# NEPH-001 — Red Flags

## AKI
- Oliguria <0.5 mL/kg/h × 2h
- Anuria
- Rapid Cr rise
- Volume overload
- Uremia

## Electrolyte
- Hyperkalemia >6.5
- Severe acidosis (pH <7.1)
- Hyperphosphatemia
- Severe hypo/hypernatremia
- Hypocalcemia (tetany)

## Dialysis
- Access dysfunction
- Hypotension
- Air embolism
- Hemolysis
- Disequilibrium syndrome
- Dialyzer reaction

## Transplant
- Acute rejection
- Infection (BK, CMV)
- PTLD
- Drug toxicity (calcineurin)
- Wound dehiscence
- Vascular thrombosis
