# ORTHO-001 — Orthopedic Surgery

## Top 10
1. Fracture (hip, femur, tibia, wrist, ankle)
2. Osteoarthritis (knee, hip)
3. Disc herniation
4. Spinal stenosis
5. ACL/meniscus
6. Rotator cuff
7. Carpal tunnel
8. Scoliosis
9. Sports injury
10. Trauma (MVC, fall)

## Workflow
1. ER/trauma → assess (X-ray, CT, MRI)
2. Reduction + splinting
3. OR scheduling
4. Surgery (open, MIS, arthroscopy)
5. Post-op (pain, mobility, VTE prophylaxis)
6. PT/OT
7. Follow-up

## Trauma
- ATLS
- Ortho: splint, definitive fixation
- Open fracture: ABX + debridement within 6h
- Compartment syndrome (5 P's): emergency fasciotomy

## Joint Replacement
- THA, TKA
- Pre-op optimization
- Surgical site marking
- VTE prophylaxis (LMWH, aspirin, DOAC)
- Antibiotic prophylaxis (cefazolin)
- DVT screening
- Post-op mobility

## Spine
- Disc herniation (conservative vs discectomy)
- Stenosis (decompression)
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (spinal cord injury)

## Red Flags
- Compartment syndrome
- Open fracture
- Septic arthritis
- Necrotizing fasciitis
- Cauda equina
- DVT/PE
- Fat embolism
- Vascular injury (knee dislocation)
- Compartment syndrome (cast)

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: compartment syndrome, open fracture
- Drug safety: opioid, anticoag
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: orthopedic surgeon
- Compliance: JCI, AAOS, CBAHI
- Tests: 12+
