# ORTHO-001 — Orthopedic Surgery

## Mission
Musculoskeletal disorders: fractures, joint replacement, sports medicine, spine, hand, foot/ankle, tumor, pediatrics.

## Top 10 Conditions
1. Hip fracture (femoral neck, intertrochanteric)
2. Knee osteoarthritis (TKA)
3. Rotator cuff tear
4. ACL tear
5. Carpal tunnel syndrome
6. Lumbar disc herniation
7. Ankle fracture
8. Distal radius fracture
9. Osteoporosis
10. Bone tumor

## Workflow
1. **Clinic** — assessment, imaging
2. **OR** — fracture fixation, joint replacement, arthroscopy
3. **Cast/splint room** — closed reduction
4. **Inpatient** — post-op, rehab
5. **Outpatient follow-up** — recovery, PT

## Red Flags
- Compartment syndrome (pain out of proportion, tense)
- Open fracture (immediate OR)
- Vascular injury (pulselessness, pallor)
- Cauda equina (saddle anesthesia, bladder dysfunction)
- Septic arthritis (fever + hot swollen joint)
- Necrotizing fasciitis
