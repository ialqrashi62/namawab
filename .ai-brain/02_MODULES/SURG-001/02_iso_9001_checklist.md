# SURG-001 — ISO 9001 (Detailed)

## QMS Scope
- All surgical procedures (elective, urgent, emergency)
- Pre-op, intra-op, post-op
- Wound care
- Discharge

## Interested Parties
- Patients
- Families
- Surgical team
- Anesthesia team
- Nursing
- MOH
- JCI / CBAHI
- Insurance (NPHIES)
- Vendors (implants, instruments)

## Quality Objectives
- SSI rate <2% (clean)
- Time-out compliance >99%
- Antibiotic timing compliance >95%
- VTE prophylaxis compliance >95%
- Unplanned return to OR <2%
- 30-day mortality <2% (per risk-adjusted)
- Patient satisfaction >4.5/5
- LOS within expected range (per procedure)

## Risk Register
- Wrong-site surgery
- Retained foreign object
- Anesthesia complication
- Massive hemorrhage
- SSI outbreak
- Equipment failure
- Staff shortage
- Implant recall

## SOPs
1. OR scheduling
2. Pre-op clinic
3. Pre-op checklist
4. Anesthesia protocols
5. Surgical procedures (per type, per surgeon preference card)
6. Post-op care
7. Wound care
8. Discharge
9. Complication management
10. Counts procedure
11. Specimen handling
12. Implant tracking

## Internal Audits
- Quarterly: pre-op + post-op
- Semi-annual: intra-op, wound care
- Annual: full

## Management Review
- Annual
- KPIs review
- Risk register
- CAPA
- Improvement actions

## Improvement
- CAPA process
- Lessons learned
- Mortality + morbidity conferences
- Patient feedback
- Continual improvement
