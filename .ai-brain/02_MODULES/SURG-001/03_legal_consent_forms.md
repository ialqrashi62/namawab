# SURG-001 — Legal Consent Forms

## 1. General Surgery Consent
- Procedure name
- Indication
- Risks: bleeding, infection, injury, anesthesia, death
- Benefits
- Alternatives
- Right to refuse
- Translator if needed
- Witness

## 2. Specific Procedure Consent
- Procedure details
- Specific risks
- Specific benefits
- Possible complications
- Recovery time
- Specific alternatives

## 3. Anesthesia Consent
- Type (general, regional, local, MAC)
- Anesthesia risks
- Specific risks (malignant hyperthermia, allergic reaction)
- NPO
- Blood transfusion if needed

## 4. Blood Transfusion Consent
- Indication
- Type of product
- Risks: TRALI, TACO, hemolytic, infection
- Alternatives
- Right to refuse (Jehovah's Witness)

## 5. Procedure + Blood Consent (Combined)
- Single consent for procedure + potential blood
- Saves time, comprehensive

## 6. Organ Removal Consent
- Indications
- Pathology review
- Disposition
- Research consent (optional)

## 7. Research Consent
- Study title, sponsor
- Procedures
- Risks, benefits
- Confidentiality
- Voluntary, withdraw
- Compensation

## 8. Telemedicine Consent
- For pre-op consult via video
- Real-time
- PHI transmitted
- Recording (if any)

## 9. Photography/Video
- For medical record
- For teaching
- For publication
- Specific consent (not blanket)

## 10. Surgical Implant Consent
- Type, brand, manufacturer
- Lifetime
- Monitoring needed
- Recall process
- Removal (if needed)

## Special Considerations
- **Jehovah's Witness:** No blood products (specific consent)
- **Minors:** Guardian + minor assent
- **Mental capacity:** Assessment
- **Translator:** Bilingual
- **Witness:** For illiterate

## Storage
- Encrypted
- 7+ year retention
- Audit log
