# SURG-001 — Clinical Red Flags

## Post-Op Complications
- Hemorrhage (sudden drop in Hb, distension, drain output)
- Sepsis (fever, tachycardia, hypotension, elevated lactate)
- Anastomotic leak (fever, abdominal pain, leukocytosis)
- Wound dehiscence
- DVT/PE (chest pain, hypoxia, leg swelling)
- Ileus (obstipation, distension, nausea)
- Pneumonia (fever, cough, infiltrate)
- MI (chest pain, ECG changes, troponin)
- Stroke (focal deficit, altered mental status)
- Renal failure (oliguria, elevated Cr)

## Pre-Op
- Cardiac risk (recent MI, unstable angina)
- Pulmonary (severe COPD, recent PE)
- Coagulopathy
- Uncontrolled DM
- Active infection
- Poor nutrition (albumin <3)

## Intra-Op
- Unexpected bleeding
- Anesthesia complication
- Hypothermia (<36°C)
- Massive blood loss
- Unintended organ injury
- Anaphylaxis
- Malignant hyperthermia
