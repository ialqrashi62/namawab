# SURG-001 — Clinical Workflows

## Top 10 Conditions
1. **Acute appendicitis**
2. **Acute cholecystitis / biliary colic**
3. **Inguinal hernia**
4. **Bowel obstruction**
5. **Acute pancreatitis**
6. **GI bleed (upper)**
7. **Diverticulitis**
8. **Perforated ulcer**
9. **Soft tissue infection / abscess**
10. **Trauma (abdominal, thoracic)**

## Workflow
1. Clinical assessment
2. Imaging (US, CT, AXR)
3. Resuscitation (NPO, IV, antibiotics)
4. Decision: OR vs conservative
5. Pre-op optimization
6. Consent
7. Anesthesia consult
8. OR booking
9. Pre-op checklist
10. Procedure
11. Post-op care
12. Discharge

## WHO Surgical Safety Checklist

### Sign-In (Before Anesthesia)
- Patient identity
- Procedure + site
- Consent signed
- Site marked
- Anesthesia plan
- Pulse ox on patient
- Allergy check
- Airway risk

### Time-Out (Before Incision)
- Team introduces
- Patient, procedure, site (verify)
- Antibiotic given within 60 min
- Imaging displayed
- Anticipated critical events
- Sterility confirmation

### Sign-Out (Before Leaving OR)
- Procedure performed (confirmed)
- Counts (sponges, instruments, sharps)
- Specimens labeled
- Equipment issues
- Plan for recovery

## Pre-Op Antibiotic Prophylaxis
- **Clean:** cefazolin 2g IV (3g if >120 kg)
- **Clean-contaminated (GI):** cefazolin + metronidazole
- **Biliary:** cefazolin
- **Cardiac/orthopedic implant:** cefazolin
- **Vascular graft:** cefazolin
- **Within 60 min of incision** (or 120 min if vancomycin)

## VTE Prophylaxis
- **Caprini score** for risk
- Low: early ambulation
- Moderate: LMWH enoxaparin 40 mg daily
- High: LMWH + mechanical (SCDs)
- Contraindications: active bleed, platelets <50, recent ICH

## ERAS Protocol
- Pre-op: carb drink, no prolonged NPO
- Intra-op: short-acting anesthesia, normothermia
- Post-op: early feeding, early mobilization, multimodal analgesia
- Reduces LOS by 30-50%

## Complication Tracking (Clavien-Dindo)
- **Grade I:** Any deviation, no therapy
- **Grade II:** Pharmacologic
- **Grade III:** Surgical/endoscopic/radiologic intervention
- **Grade IV:** Life-threatening (ICU)
- **Grade V:** Death

## Red Flags (Post-Op)
- Fever POD 1-2: atelectasis
- Fever POD 3-5: SSI
- Fever POD 5+: deep infection, leak
- Tachycardia, hypotension: sepsis
- Drop in Hb: bleed
- Distension + nausea: ileus
- Chest pain + hypoxia: PE
- Wound dehiscence

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: hemorrhage, sepsis, leak
- Drug safety: high-alert (anticoag, opioid)
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: Surgeon
- Compliance: WHO, SCIP, ACS, CBAHI
- Tests: WHO checklist, antibiotic timing
