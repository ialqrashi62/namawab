# SURG-001 7-Expert Synthesis (L1-L4)

## Mission
Elective and emergency general surgery: abdominal, hernia, gallbladder, appendix, colon, breast, endocrine, soft tissue. WHO Surgical Safety Checklist mandatory.

## Top 10 Conditions (CMO)
1. Acute appendicitis (emergency)
2. Cholecystitis (both)
3. Inguinal hernia (elective)
4. Bowel obstruction (emergency)
5. Colorectal cancer (elective)
6. Breast cancer (elective)
7. Perforated viscus (emergency)
8. GI bleed (emergency)
9. Hemorrhoids (elective)
10. Thyroid nodule (elective)

## WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (Mandatory)

### Sign-In (Before Anesthesia)
- Patient ID, site, procedure, consent
- Site marked
- Anesthesia safety check
- Pulse oximeter
- Allergies
- Difficult airway / aspiration risk
- Blood loss risk

### Time-Out (Before Skin Incision)
- Team introductions (name + role)
- Patient, procedure, site confirmed
- Antibiotic prophylaxis (<60 min)
- Imaging displayed
- Critical events (surgeon, anesthesia, nursing)

### Sign-Out (Before Leaving OR)
- Procedure performed
- Instrument + sponge + needle count
- Specimen labeled (patient + site)
- Equipment issues
- Recovery priorities

## Workflow
1. Clinic (assessment, workup, planning)
2. Pre-op (H&P, labs, anesthesia, consent)
3. OR (time-out, surgery, count, sign-out)
4. PACU (recovery, pain, antiemetic)
5. Ward (daily rounds, drain, ambulation)
6. Discharge (instructions, follow-up)
7. Follow-up (clinic, pathology, surveillance)

## Antibiotic Prophylaxis

| Surgery | Antibiotic | Dose | Timing |
|---------|-----------|------|--------|
| Clean (hernia, breast) | Cefazolin | 2g IV | 0-60 min before incision |
| Clean-contaminated (GI) | Cefazolin + metronidazole | 2g + 500mg | 0-60 min before |
| Biliary | Cefazolin | 2g IV | 0-60 min before |
| Colorectal | Cefazolin + metronidazole | 2g + 500mg | 0-60 min before |

**Redose:** surgery >3h or blood loss >1500 mL

## DVT Prophylaxis (Caprini Score)
- Low (0-2): early ambulation
- Moderate (3-4): LMWH or UFH
- High (≥5): LMWH + SCDs
- Cancer surgery: extended prophylaxis 28 days

## Post-Op Red Flags
1. Fever >38.5°C (>5 days post-op)
2. Tachycardia unexplained
3. Hypotension
4. Tachypnea / hypoxemia
5. Wound dehiscence
6. Anastomotic leak (tachycardia + pain + fever)
7. DVT signs
8. PE signs
9. Severe pain not responding
10. Mental status change
