# SURG-001 — Sub-Department Catalog

## Sub-Units
1. **SURG-001-OR** — Main operating room
2. **SURG-001-DAYCASE** — Day case surgery
3. **SURG-001-MINOR** — Minor procedure room
4. **SURG-001-EMERG** — Emergency surgery
5. **SURG-001-LAP** — Laparoscopic
6. **SURG-001-OPEN** — Open surgery
7. **SURG-001-AMB** — Ambulatory (clinic)
8. **SURG-001-WARD** — Surgical ward

## Per-Subunit
- **OR:** Major cases, anesthesia required
- **DAYCASE:** In/out same day
- **MINOR:** Local anesthesia, in/out
- **EMERG:** Trauma, acute abdomen
- **LAP:** Laparoscopic procedures
- **OPEN:** Open procedures
- **AMB:** Pre-op clinic, post-op follow-up
- **WARD:** Post-op care

## Cross-Cutting
- Surgical timeout (WHO checklist)
- Site marking
- Antibiotic prophylaxis
- VTE prophylaxis
- Normothermia
- Glycemic control
- Pain management
- Enhanced recovery (ERAS)
