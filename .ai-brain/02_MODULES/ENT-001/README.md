# ENT-001 — Otolaryngology

## Mission
Ear, nose, throat, head & neck: otitis, sinusitis, tonsillitis, hearing loss, vertigo, voice disorders, head/neck cancer, thyroid.

## Top 10 Conditions
1. Acute otitis media
2. Chronic sinusitis
3. Tonsillitis / pharyngitis
4. Allergic rhinitis
5. Hearing loss (conductive, sensorineural)
6. Vertigo / BPPV
7. Head and neck cancer
8. Thyroid nodules
9. Epistaxis (nosebleed)
10. Foreign body (ear, nose, throat)

## Workflow
1. **Clinic** — ENT exam, audiometry
2. **OR** — tonsillectomy, FESS, tympanoplasty, head/neck
3. **Audiology** — hearing tests, vestibular
4. **Speech therapy** — voice, swallowing
5. **Inpatient consults** — ENT emergencies

## Red Flags
- Epiglottitis (drooling, tripod, muffled voice) — DO NOT examine throat
- Foreign body in airway (choking)
- Severe epistaxis (posterior, uncontrolled)
- Sudden sensorineural hearing loss (steroids within 24-48h)
- Peritonsillar abscess (drooling, trismus)
- Neck mass (red flags for cancer)
