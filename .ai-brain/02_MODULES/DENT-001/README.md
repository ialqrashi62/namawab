# DENT-001 — Dentistry & Oral Surgery

## Mission
Oral health: prevention, restoration, extraction, prosthodontics, orthodontics, oral surgery, maxillofacial.

## Top Conditions
- Dental caries
- Periodontal disease
- Tooth impaction (wisdom)
- TMJ disorders
- Oral cancer
- Maxillofacial fractures
- Cleft lip/palate
- Malocclusion

## Workflow
- Comprehensive exam
- Treatment plan
- Preventive (cleaning, fluoride, sealants)
- Restorative (filling, crown, bridge, implant)
- Surgical (extraction, biopsy, orthognathic)
- Endodontic (root canal)
- Prosthodontic (dentures)

## Red Flags
- Ludwig's angina
- Oral cancer (persistent ulcer >2 weeks)
- Maxillofacial fracture (airway compromise)
- Post-extraction bleeding
- Necrotizing fasciitis (odontogenic)
- Anaphylaxis (LA)
