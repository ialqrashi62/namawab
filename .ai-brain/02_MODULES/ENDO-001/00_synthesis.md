3. Imaging
4. Diagnosis
5. Treatment plan
6. Follow-up

## Diabetes
- Type 1: insulin
- Type 2: lifestyle, metformin, GLP-1, SGLT2, insulin
- A1c target < 7%
- Complications: nephropathy, retinopathy, neuropathy, foot

## Thyroid
- Hypothyroid: levothyroxine
- Hyperthyroid: methimazole, PTU, RAI
- Nodule: FNA
