# PAIN-001 — Pain Medicine (Multidisciplinary)

## Mission
Acute, chronic, cancer pain management: pharmacologic, interventional, behavioral, complementary.

## Top Conditions
- Chronic low back pain
- Neuropathic pain (post-herpetic, diabetic)
- Cancer pain
- Headache
- Fibromyalgia
- CRPS (complex regional pain syndrome)
- Failed back surgery syndrome

## Modalities
- Pharmacologic (NSAIDs, gabapentin, opioids)
- Interventional (epidural, facet, nerve blocks, ablation, SCS, intrathecal pump)
- Behavioral (CBT, mindfulness)
- Physical therapy
- Complementary (acupuncture, TENS)

## Red Flags (Opioid Stewardship)
- High-dose opioid (MME >50)
- Co-prescribed benzodiazepine
- Multiple prescribers
- Patient with SUD history
- Early refill requests

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: opioid misuse
- Drug safety: opioid, gabapentin
- PHI: encrypted (controlled substance)
- Auth: Pain specialist
- Compliance: JCI, CDC, MOH
- Tests: opioid risk, intervention
