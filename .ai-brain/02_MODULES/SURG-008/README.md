# SURG-008 — Surgical Endocrinology

## Mission
Endocrine surgery: thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal, pancreatic neuroendocrine.

## Top Conditions
- Thyroid cancer (papillary, follicular, medullary, anaplastic)
- Thyroid nodules (benign vs malignant)
- Hyperparathyroidism
- Adrenal mass (adenoma, pheochromocytoma, cancer)
- Pancreatic NET (insulinoma, gastrinoma)

## Thyroid Surgery
- Total thyroidectomy
- Lobectomy
- Central neck dissection
- Lateral neck dissection

## Red Flags
- Anaplastic thyroid cancer
- Medullary thyroid cancer (MEN 2)
- Pheochromocytoma crisis
- Thyroid storm
- Vocal cord paralysis
- Hypocalcemia (post-thyroidectomy)
- Adrenal crisis (post-adrenalectomy)
