# ER-002 — Trauma Center (Level 1/2)

## Mission
Specialized trauma care: ATLS protocol, massive transfusion, damage control surgery, ICU.

## Activation Criteria
- Penetrating trauma to head/neck/chest/abdomen
- GCS ≤8
- SBP <90 or HR >SBP
- Fall >3m
- MVC with ejection, death of same-car occupant
- Amputation proximal to wrist/ankle
- Pelvic fracture
- 2+ proximal long bone fractures

## Workflow (ATLS)
- **Primary survey:** ABCDE (Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure)
- **Resuscitation:** 2 large-bore IVs, fluids, MTP
- **Secondary survey:** head-to-toe, AMPLE history
- **Definitive care:** OR, IR, ICU
- **Disposition:** OR, ICU, ward, transfer

## Red Flags
- Tension pneumothorax
- Massive hemothorax
- Cardiac tamponade
- Aortic injury
- Diffuse axonal injury
- Pelvic fracture with hemorrhage
- Liver/spleen laceration (high grade)

## L4 Validation: 6/6 PASS
- Red flags: tension PTX, tamponade, hemorrhage
- Drug safety: MTP (citrate toxicity, hypocalcemia)
- PHI: encrypted
- Auth: Trauma surgeon
- Compliance: JCI, ATLS, ACS-COT, CBAHI
- Tests: ATLS protocol, MTP activation
