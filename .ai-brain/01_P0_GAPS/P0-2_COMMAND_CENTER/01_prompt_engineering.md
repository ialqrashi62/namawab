# P0-2_COMMAND_CENTER — Hospital Command Center

## Module ID
`P0-2_COMMAND_CENTER` — Hospital Command Center

## Specialty
Real-time hospital operations + resource management

## Clinical Scope
- **Bed Management** — Real-time availability (ICU/CCU/Gen/Ward)
- **ED Capacity** — Triage queue, surge detection
- **OR Scheduling** — Operating room throughput
- **Patient Flow** — Bottleneck detection
- **Staff Allocation** — Nurses, doctors, technicians
- **Equipment Status** — Ventilators, monitors, etc.
- **Mass Casualty** — MCI protocol activation
- **Surge Planning** — Pandemic/disaster readiness
- **Discharge Coordination** — Reduce LOS
- **Quality Dashboards** — KPIs

## Scoring
- **ED Occupancy Rate** — Surge threshold
- **ICU Bed Turnover** — Efficiency
- **OR Utilization** — Throughput
- **ALOS (Average Length of Stay)** — Quality
- **Patient Wait Time** — ED triage
- **Staff-Patient Ratio** — Safety
- **MCI Levels** — Tiered response

## Compliance
- **MoH** Saudi Hospital Operations Standards
- **CBAHI** Operational Standards
- **NPHIES** Real-time Reporting
- **SCFHS** Staff Licensing
- **SFDA** Drug Availability

## Approval
- Hospital Operations Director
- Chief Medical Officer
- Nursing Director
- ED Director
- Facilities Manager
