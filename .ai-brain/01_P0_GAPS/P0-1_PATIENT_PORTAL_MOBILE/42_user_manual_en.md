# User Manual — Patient Portal (P0-1_PATIENT_PORTAL_MOBILE)

> **Audience**: Patients and their families
> **Version**: 1.0.0

---

## 1. Introduction

The Patient Portal is a mobile app that gives you 24/7 access to your healthcare services. It's compliant with Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and CBAHI standards.

## 2. Registration & Login

### 2.1 Register via Nafath
1. Download app from App Store or Google Play
2. Tap **Sign In**
3. Choose **Nafath** for identity verification
4. Enter your National ID
5. Enter OTP sent to your mobile
6. (Optional) Enable biometric for faster login

### 2.2 PDPL Privacy Consent
After first login, you'll see the consent screen. Read carefully and consent to:
- Data processing
- Sharing with care providers
- (Optional) Research
- (Optional) Marketing

You can **withdraw consent anytime** from Settings.

## 3. Home Screen

You'll see on the home screen:
- **Upcoming Appointments**: date, doctor, time
- **Recent Lab Results**
- **Active Medications**: with refill button
- **Quick Vitals Entry**: BP, glucose, weight

## 4. Book Appointment

1. Tap **Book Appointment**
2. Select **Specialty** (cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics, etc.)
3. Select **Date and Time**
4. Insurance is auto-verified via Wateen
5. Pay copay (if applicable)
6. Tap **Confirm** — you'll receive confirmation

## 5. Telehealth (Video)

1. From appointment, tap **Join**
2. Allow camera and microphone access
3. Wait for doctor
4. After visit, you'll receive summary and prescriptions

### When is telehealth appropriate?
- ✅ Routine follow-up
- ✅ Medication review
- ✅ Lab result discussion
- ✅ Minor complaints

### When to go in-person?
- ❌ Chest pain or breathing difficulty
- ❌ Severe injuries
- ❌ Any emergency (call 911)

## 6. Lab Results

### When do results appear?
- **Normal**: 24 hours after doctor approval
- **Critical**: Within 1 hour (doctor will call you)

### How to understand results?
- Tap any result for plain-language explanation
- If abnormal, consult your doctor

## 7. Refill Medications

1. Tap **Refill** next to medication
2. If refills remaining, request is sent to pharmacy
3. You'll get notification when ready

### ⚠️ Important
**Controlled substances** (e.g., sedatives) cannot be requested through the app. You must visit your doctor in person.

## 8. Track Vitals

### 8.1 Blood Pressure
1. Tap **BP** icon
2. Enter systolic and diastolic
3. If very high (>180), your doctor will be auto-notified

### 8.2 Glucose
- Normal fasting: 70-100 mg/dL
- Normal post-meal: <140 mg/dL

### 8.3 Weight
- Weigh weekly at same conditions

## 9. Pay Bills

1. Tap **Bills**
2. Select invoice
3. Choose payment method (MADA, Apple Pay, bank)
4. For amounts >500 SAR: enter OTP
5. Tap **Pay**

## 10. Caregiver Access (Proxy)

To give family member access:
1. Tap **Settings → Caregivers**
2. Enter spouse/parent/child national ID
3. Both sign PDPL consent
4. Set expiration date

## 11. Privacy & Your Rights

### PDPL Rights:
- ✅ **Access** your data
- ✅ **Correction** if wrong
- ✅ **Erasure** (deletion)
- ✅ **Portability** (FHIR export)
- ✅ **Withdraw consent** anytime

## 12. Emergency

### Call 911 immediately for:
- Severe chest pain
- Breathing difficulty
- Loss of consciousness
- Severe bleeding
- Stroke (drooping face, weak arm, slurred speech)
