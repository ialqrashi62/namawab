# P0-3 BCMA — Barcode Medication Administration

## Module ID
`P0-3_BCMA` — Closed-loop Barcode Medication Administration

## Specialty
Medication safety + bedside scanning

## Clinical Scope
- **Wristband Scan** — Patient identification
- **Medication Scan** — GS1/HIBC barcode
- **5 Rights Verification** — Patient/Drug/Dose/Route/Time
- **Allergy Check** — Real-time alert
- **Interaction Check** — Drug-drug/Drug-food
- **Override Workflow** — Documented reason
- **PRN Tracking** — Pain score, last dose
- **Multi-Dose Verification** — Insulin, Heparin
- **High-Alert Meds** — Double-check (Chemo, Insulin)
- **Disposal Tracking** — Witness required

## Compliance
- **Joint Commission NPSG.01** — 5 Rights
- **CBAHI** — Medication Safety
- **SFDA** — Barcode Standards
- **ASHP** — Best Practices
- **ISMP** — Medication Safety
- **HIMSS EMRAM** — Stage 6/7

## Approval
- Bedside Nurse
- Pharmacist
- Charge Nurse
- Pharmacy Department
- Patient Safety Officer

## Critical Alert
- Allergy mismatch → BLOCK
- Wrong patient → BLOCK
- Wrong dose → BLOCK
- Override → Mandatory reason + 2nd witness
- High-alert medication → 2-Nurse verify
