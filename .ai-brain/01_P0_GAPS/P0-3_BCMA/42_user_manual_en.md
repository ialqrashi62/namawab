# BCMA User Manual

## What is BCMA?
BCMA (Barcode Medication Administration) verifies the **5 Rights** before any drug is given to a patient:
1. Right Patient
2. Right Drug
3. Right Dose
4. Right Route
5. Right Time

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open BCMA App
From main menu tap "BCMA" or scan QR code.

### Step 2: Scan Patient Wristband
- Tap the large camera icon
- Aim camera at patient wrist
- Barcode reads automatically

### Step 3: Scan Drug Code
- After patient name + MRN appears
- Tap "Scan Drug"
- Aim camera at drug barcode

### Step 4: Verify
System auto-checks:
- ✅ 5 Rights
- ⚠️ Allergies
- ⚠️ Drug interactions
- 🚨 HIGH-ALERT drugs (require 2nd nurse witness)

### Step 5: Administer or Refuse
- ✅ Green check: tap "Confirm Administration"
- ❌ Red X: tap "Refuse" with reason

## High-Alert Drugs
- Insulin
- Heparin
- Warfarin
- Chemotherapy
- Potassium chloride

**These require a 2nd nurse witness before administration.**

## FAQ

**Q: What if system refuses a drug?**
A: Tap "Refuse" or request override from prescriber with reason.

**Q: What if internet is down?**
A: App stores scans locally and uploads on reconnect.
