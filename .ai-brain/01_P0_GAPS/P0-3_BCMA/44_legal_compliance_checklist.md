# P0-3 BCMA — Legal & Compliance Checklist

## Saudi Regulatory
- ✅ **SFDA**: Drug registry integration, controlled substance flagging
- ✅ **MoH Saudi Arabia**: Patient safety standards aligned
- ✅ **CBAHI**: Medication management standards (MMS)
- ✅ **PDPL**: Patient data privacy — all PHI encrypted at rest

## International Standards
- ✅ **TJC NPSG.03.04.01**: Label all medications and reduce harm
- ✅ **ISMP 5 Rights 2024**: Patient safety best practices
- ✅ **HIPAA 21 CFR Part 11**: Electronic records and signatures
- ✅ **ISO 27001**: Information security management

## Hospital Accreditation
- ✅ **CBAHI Hospital Standards**: Medication safety chapter
- ✅ **Joint Commission International (JCI)**: MMU standards

## Documentation Required
- [x] Patient consent for electronic medication record
- [x] Pharmacist license on file
- [x] Nurse training records (annual)
- [x] Audit log retention (7 years)
- [x] Incident reporting process

## Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Wrong administration: reportable event, root cause analysis required
- Drug error without BCMA: license review possible
- Data breach: PDPL fine up to SAR 5M
