# 01_clinical_spec.md - Urology Surgery Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Urology Surgery Ecosystem
Surgical management of the urinary tract and male reproductive organs.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Urology:** Cystoscopy, Vasectomy, Circumcision.
- **Endourology:** Ureteroscopy, Laser Lithotripsy (Stone removal).
- **Urologic Oncology:** Prostatectomy, Cystectomy, Nephrectomy.
- **Andrology:** Varicocele repair, Penile implants.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Stone Removal Flow:** Imaging $\rightarrow$ Laser Lithotripsy $\rightarrow$ Stent Placement $\rightarrow$ Stent Removal.
- **KPIs:** Stone-free rate, Post-op urinary leakage, Complication rate.
- **Required Data:** Stone size/location, Ureteral diameter, Post-op creatinine.

## 3 la. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict sterile technique for urinary catheters and stents.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of cystoscopy videos and urodynamic data.
