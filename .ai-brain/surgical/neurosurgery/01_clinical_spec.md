# 01_clinical_spec.md - Neurosurgery & Spine Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Neurosurgery Ecosystem
Precision interventions for the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves.

### Specialized Units:
- **Cerebrovascular:** Aneurysm clipping, AV malformation/stroke surgery.
- **Neuro-oncology:** Brain tumor resection and biopsy.
- **Functional Neurosurgery:** DBS (Deep Brain Stimulation) for Parkinson's/Epilepsy.
- **Spine Surgery:** Discectomy, Spinal fusion, and Scoliosis correction.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Neuromonitoring:** SSEP/MEP monitoring during spinal/brain surgery.
- **KPIs:** Post-op neurological deficit rate, GCS improvement, and infection rate.
- **Required Data:** ICP (Intracranial Pressure), MAP, and Neuromonitoring/EEG/EMG logs.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict adherence to "Wrong-Site" prevention in spinal surgery.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of high-res MRI/CT surgical navigation maps.
