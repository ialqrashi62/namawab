# 01_clinical_spec.md - Ophthalmology Surgery Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Ophthalmology Ecosystem
Precision microsurgery for the eye and orbital structures.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Ophthalmology:** Cataract surgery, Pterygium removal.
- **Vitreoretinal Surgery:** Vitrectomy, Retinal detachment repair.
- **Cornea & External:** Keratoplasty (Cornea transplant), DMEK/DSAEK.
- **Glaucoma Surgery:** Trabeculectomy, Shunt implantation.
- **Oculoplastics:** Eyelid surgery, Orbital decompression.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Cataract Flow:** Pre-op Biometry $\rightarrow$ IOL Selection $\rightarrow$ Phacoemulsification $\rightarrow$ Post-op Drop Regimen.
- **KPIs:** Visual acuity improvement, Endophthalmitis rate, IOL position accuracy.
- **Required Data:** K-readings, Axial length, Intraocular pressure (IOP).

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict verification of the correct eye (Left/Right) and correct lens power.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of high-res retinal scans and OCT images.
