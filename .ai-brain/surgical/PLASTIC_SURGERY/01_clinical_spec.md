# 01_clinical_spec.md - Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Plastic Surgery Ecosystem
Reconstructive and aesthetic surgery for the entire body.

### Specialized Units:
- **Reconstructive Surgery:** Microvascular flaps, Scar revision, Cleft lip/palate.
- **Cosmetic Surgery:** Rhinoplasty, Blepharoplasty, Body contouring.
- **Burns Center:** Acute burn care, Skin grafting, Contracture release.
- **Maxillofacial Surgery:** Jaw reconstruction, Facial trauma repair.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Flap Monitoring:** Hourly capillary refill check $\rightarrow$ Doppler/Ultrasound $\rightarrow$ Flap viability assessment.
- **KPIs:** Flap survival rate, Patient satisfaction (Aesthetic), Wound healing time.
- **Required Data:** Flap dimensions, Perfusion index, Suture type.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict monitoring of graft viability and sterile dressing changes.
- **PDPL:** Extreme encryption of "Before/After" aesthetic photographs.
