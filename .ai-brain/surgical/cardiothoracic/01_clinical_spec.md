# 01_clinical_spec.md - Cardiothoracic & Vascular Surgery Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Cardiothoracic Ecosystem
High-acuity surgical interventions for the heart, lungs, and major blood vessels.

### Specialized Units:
- **Open Heart Surgery:** CABG, Valve replacement, and Heart transplant.
- **Thoracic Surgery:** Lobectomy, Pleurectomy, and Airway surgery.
- **Vascular Surgery:** Endovascular stenting, Aortic aneurysm repair, and Bypass grafts.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Perioperative Care:** Strict hemodynamic monitoring $\rightarrow$ Mechanical ventilation $\rightarrow$ Early mobilization.
- **KPIs:** 30-day mortality, Pump-time (for open heart), and Graft patency rate.
- **Required Data:** Intra-operative MAP, Cardiac Index, and Blood loss.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** High-risk surgical site verification and blood product management.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of intra-operative hemodynamic waveforms.
