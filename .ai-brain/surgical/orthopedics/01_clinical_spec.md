# 01_clinical_spec.md - Orthopedics & Joint Replacement Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Orthopedic Ecosystem
Surgical and non-surgical management of the musculoskeletal system.

### Specialized Units:
- **Joint Replacement (Arthroplasty):** Hip, Knee, and Shoulder replacement.
- **Trauma Orthopedics:** Fracture fixation, Intramedullary nailing.
- **Spinal Orthopedics:** Deformity correction and spinal stabilization.
- **Sports Medicine:** Arthroscopy, ACL/MCL reconstruction, and Meniscus repair.
- **Pediatric Orthopedics:** Congenital hip dysplasia and growth plate surgery.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Joint Replacement Flow:** Pre-op Templating $\rightarrow$ Implantation $\rightarrow$ Post-op Rehab.
- **KPIs:** Implant survival rate, Range of Motion (ROM) improvement, and SSI rate.
- **Required Data:** Implant size, Material, Alignment angle, and ROM degrees.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict implant traceability (Lot/Serial number tracking).
- **PDPL:** Encryption of pre- and post-op X-ray/CT images.
