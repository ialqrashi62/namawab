## 1. AI Architecture: The Auditory-Sinu Brain
RAG pipeline for complex ENT pathology and implant optimization.

### A. VectorMine Implementation
- **Vector Database:** PGVector storing embeddings of audiograms and endoscopic images.
- **Retrieval:** Semantic search to match current sinus anatomy with "Optimal Surgical Path" cases.

## 2. LangChain Orchestration
- **Chain 1: Audiogram Analysis:** `Input (Audiogram Data) -> Vector Search (Similar Hearing Loss) -> LLM (Analysis) -> Output (Cochlear Implant Candidacy)`.
- **Chain 2: Sinus Pathology Predictor:** `Input (CT Scan) -> Predictive Model -> LLM (Summary) -> Output (Surgical Approach Suggestion)`.
