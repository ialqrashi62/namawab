# 01_clinical_spec.md - ENT Surgery Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of ENT Surgery Ecosystem
Surgical management of the ear, nose, throat, and head/neck region.

### Specialized Units:
- **General ENT:** Tonsillectomy, Adenoidectomy.
- **Rhinology & Skull Base:** FESS (Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery), Pituitary tumor removal.
- **Otology & Neurotology:** Cochlear implants, Tympanoplasty, Mastoidectomy.
- **Laryngology:** Vocal cord surgery, Laryngeal stents.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Cochlear Implant Flow:** Audiometry $\rightarrow$ Imaging $\rightarrow$ Implantation $\rightarrow$ Programming $\rightarrow$ Rehab.
- **KPIs:** Hearing threshold improvement, Post-op nasal patency, Infection rate.
- **Required Data:** Audiogram/Tympanometry, Endoscopic/CT findings.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict airway management and aspiration prevention protocols.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of endoscopic videos and audio recordings.
