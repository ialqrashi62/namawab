# 01 Clinical Spec — Surgical Suite

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Full perioperative management across all surgical subspecialties.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - OR booking and scheduling.
  - Pre-operative assessment and consent.
  - WHO surgical safety checklist.
  - Intra-operative charting and implant tracking.
  - PACU handoff and post-operative follow-up.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Surgical consultation → 2. Booking → 3. Pre-op → 4. Time-out → 5. Surgery → 6. PACU → 7. Ward/Rehab → 8. Follow-up.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Safety checklist completion gate.
- Implant serial tracking.
- Prolonged operative time alerts.
- PACU discharge criteria.

## 4. Integration Points
- Anesthesia, PACU, CSSD, inventory, billing, pathology, radiology.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Time-out before incision.
- Consent and anesthesia readiness.
- Implant verification.
- Mandatory PACU handoff.
