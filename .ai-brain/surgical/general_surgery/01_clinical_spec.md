# 01_clinical_spec.md - General Surgery Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of General Surgery Ecosystem
Comprehensive management of surgical interventions, from elective procedures to emergency trauma surgery.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Surgery:** Appendectomy, Cholecystectomy, Hernia repair.
- **Surgical Oncology:** Resection of solid tumors, lymph node dissection.
- **Endocrine Surgery:** Thyroidectomy, Parathyroidectomy, Adrenalectomy.
- **Minimal Invasive & Robotic Surgery:** Laparoscopic and robotic-assisted procedures (Da Vinci system).
- **Bariatric Surgery:** Gastric sleeve, Bypass, and metabolic surgery.
- **Breast Surgery:** Lumpectomy, Mastectomy, and reconstructive surgery.
- **Trauma Surgery:** Damage control surgery, laparotomy for hemorrhage.
- **Colorectal Surgery:** Colectomy, anastomosis, and proctology.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Surgical Safety Checklist:** WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (Sign-in, Time-out, Sign-out).
- **Perioperative Flow:** Pre-op/Anesthesia $\rightarrow$ Intra-op (Surgical Log) $\rightarrow$ PACU $\rightarrow$ Ward.
- **KPIs:** Surgical Site Infection (SSI) rate, 30-day readmission, and operative time.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Surgical Site Marking:** Mandatory verification of the surgical site.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of intra-operative videos and pathology reports.
- **JCI:** Strict adherence to sterilization and instrument counting protocols.
