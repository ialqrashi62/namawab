# 01_clinical_spec.md - Integrative & Complementary Medicine Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Integrative Medicine
Evidence-based complementary therapies integrated with conventional medical care.

### Specialized Units:
- **Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM):** Acupuncture and Medical Cupping.
- **Herbal Medicine:** Standardized botanical extracts and phytotherapy.
- **Mind-Body Therapy:** Medical Yoga, Art Therapy, and Music Therapy.
- **Physical Wellness:** Medical Massage and Pet Therapy.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Integration Flow:** Conventional Diagnosis $\rightarrow$ Complementary Plan $\rightarrow$ Synergy Monitoring.
- **KPIs:** Patient-reported pain reduction, Quality of Life (QoL) improvement, and Synergy/Interference rate.
- **Required Data:** Acupuncture points, Herbal dosage, and Session duration.

## 3. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict hygiene for needles (Acupuncture) and cupping/massage.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of holistic health records.
