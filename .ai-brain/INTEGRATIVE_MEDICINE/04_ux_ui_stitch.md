# 04 UX/UI Stitch — Integrative & Complementary Medicine

## 1. Design Tokens
- **Palette**: calming earth tones, wellness green, alert amber for interactions.
- **Typography**: same as Stitch clinical stations.
- **Layout**: 3-column station.

## 2. Components
- `IntegrativePlanBuilder`: therapies, goals, duration.
- `SessionLog`: date, therapy, practitioner, notes.
- `OutcomeTracker`: patient-reported outcomes and objective measures.
- `InteractionAlert`: drug-herb and drug-supplement flags.
- `ConventionalCareSummary`: active diagnoses and medications from CPOE.

## 3. User Stories
- "As an integrative physician, I want interaction alerts before adding herbal therapy."
- "As a patient, I want to see my integrative plan and progress."

## 4. Wireframe Logic
- Left: patient list + referral queue.
- Center: plan/session/outcome tabs.
- Right: interaction alerts + conventional care summary.

## 5. Accessibility
- RTL/LTR, clear interaction warnings, simple patient view.
