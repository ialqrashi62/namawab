# 01 Clinical Spec — Internal Medicine Suite

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Adult non-surgical medical care across all internal medicine subspecialties.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - General internal medicine consultations.
  - Subspecialty referrals and co-management.
  - Chronic disease registry and longitudinal tracking.
  - Pre-operative medical clearance.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Presentation → 2. Triage/assessment → 3. Workup → 4. Diagnosis → 5. Treatment/monitoring → 6. Follow-up or subspecialty referral.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Subspecialty routing based on problem list and ICD codes.
- Chronic disease gap alerts (missing labs, overdue visits).
- Guideline-based order suggestions.

## 4. Integration Points
- CPOE, LIS, RIS, pharmacy.
- All internal medicine subspecialties.
- Surgery and critical care for co-management.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Allergy and contraindication checks.
- Specialist approval for complex referrals.
- Critical result communication.
