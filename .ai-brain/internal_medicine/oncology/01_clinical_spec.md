# 01_clinical_spec.md - Oncology & Hematology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Oncology Ecosystem
Comprehensive management of malignant neoplasms and hematologic disorders.

### Specialized Units:
- **Medical Oncology:** Chemotherapy, Targeted Therapy, and Immunotherapy.
- **Hematology:** Leukemia, Lymphoma, and Myeloma management.
- **BMT Unit (Bone Marrow Transplant):** Autologous and Allogeneic stem cell transplants.
- **Gynecologic Oncology:** Specialized cancer care for female reproductive systems.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Chemotherapy Cycle:** Dose calculation based on BSA (Body Surface Area) $\rightarrow$ Pre-medication $\rightarrow$ Infusion $\rightarrow$ Toxicity monitoring.
- **BMT Protocol:** Conditioning regimen $\rightarrow$ Stem cell infusion $\rightarrow$ Engraftment monitoring $\rightarrow$ GVHD screening.
- **KPIs:** Progression-Free Survival (PFS), Overall Survival (OS), and Neutropenia rates.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Toxicity Alerts:** Automatic alerts for Grade 3/4 neutropenia.
- **PDPL:** Extreme encryption for genetic markers and oncology reports.
