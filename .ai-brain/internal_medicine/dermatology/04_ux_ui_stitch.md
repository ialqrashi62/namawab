# 04_ux_ui_stitch.md - Dermatology UI/UX Design
**Expert: Product Manager & UX Lead**

## 1. Design Philosophy: "The Visual Derm Hub"
Focus on high-resolution imagery and anatomical mapping.

### A. The Dermatology Station
- **Layout:** 3-column fluid grid.
    - **Left:** Patient skin type, allergy history, and current treatment.
    - **Center:** Dynamic tabs for [Lesion Map | Cosmetic Log | Phototherapy | AI Analysis].
    - **Right:** AI-Brain panel showing "Visual Matches" and suggested pathology.

## 2. Stitch Component Specifications
- **Image Gallery:** `Stitch-Clinical-Gallery` for comparing "Before" and "After" photos.
- **Anatomical Map:** `Stitch-Body-Map` for marking lesion locations on a 3D human model.
- **Data Grid:** `Stitch-Data-Table-Premium` for tracking laser settings.
