# 01_clinical_spec.md - Dermatology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Dermatology Ecosystem
Management of skin, hair, and nail disorders, including cosmetic and surgical dermatology.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Dermatology:** Acne, Psoriasis, Eczema, and general skin health.
- **Cosmetic Dermatology:** Laser therapy, fillers, and aesthetic procedures.
- **Dermatosurgery:** Mohs surgery, excision of lesions, and skin grafts.
- **Dermatologic Oncology:** Melanoma and Basal/Squamous cell carcinoma.
- **Phototherapy Unit:** UV-B and PUVA treatment.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Lesion Tracking:** Mapping of lesions $\rightarrow$ Biopsy $\rightarrow$ Histopathology $\rightarrow$ Treatment.
- **Cosmetic Protocol:** Consultation $\rightarrow$ Patch Test $\rightarrow$ Procedure $\rightarrow$ Post-care.
- **KPIs:** Patient satisfaction (Cosmetic), Recurrence rate (Oncology), and Healing time.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Drug Interaction:** Alerts for systemic retinoids and pregnancy risks.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of high-resolution clinical photographs.
