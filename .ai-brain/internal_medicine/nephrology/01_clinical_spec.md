# 01_clinical_spec.md - Nephrology & Dialysis Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Nephrology Ecosystem
This module manages kidney health, chronic kidney disease (CKD), and renal replacement therapies.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Nephrology:** CKD staging, hypertension management, and electrolyte balance.
- **Renal Transplantation:** Pre-transplant evaluation, post-op immunosuppression, and graft monitoring.
- **Dialysis Unit:**
    - **Hemodialysis (HD):** Vascular access management, dialysis prescriptions, and adequacy (Kt/V).
    - **Peritoneal Dialysis (PD):** Peritonitis monitoring and effluent analysis.
    - **Home Dialysis:** Remote monitoring and patient training.
- **Plasmapheresis:** Therapeutic plasma exchange for autoimmune/hematologic crises.
- **Pediatric Dialysis:** Specialized pediatric renal replacement therapy.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
### A. Hemodialysis Session
- **KPI:** Urea Reduction Ratio (URR) and Kt/V (Dialysis Adequacy).
- **Protocol:** Pre-dialysis weight $\rightarrow$ Ultrafiltration goal $\rightarrow$ Dialysate composition $\rightarrow$ Post-dialysis stability.
- **Required Data:** Blood flow rate (Qb), Dialysate flow (Qd), Arterial/Venous pressures.

### B. Renal Transplant
- **Protocol:** HLA Matching $\rightarrow$ Crossmatch $\rightarrow$ Surgical Implantation $\rightarrow$ Immunosuppression Titration.
- **Required Data:** Creatinine clearance, biopsy results, and drug levels (Tacrolimus/Cyclosporine).

## 3. Clinical Decision Support (CDS)
- **Automatic Scoring:** GFR (Glomerular Filtration Rate) calculation using CKD-EPI and MDRD formulas.
- **Alerts:** Critical hyperkalemia or severe fluid overload alerts.
- **Guideline Alignment:** KDIGO (Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes).

## 4. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict water quality monitoring for dialysis (AAMI standards).
- **PDPL:** Encryption of transplant waiting list and donor-recipient matching data.
