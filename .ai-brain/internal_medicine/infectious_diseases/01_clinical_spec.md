# 01_clinical_spec.md - Infectious Diseases Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Infectious Diseases Ecosystem
Management of complex bacterial, viral, fungal, and parasitic infections, including antimicrobial stewardship.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Infectious Diseases:** Fever of unknown origin, sepsis, and systemic infections.
- **Infection Control:** Hospital-acquired infection (HAI) monitoring and outbreak management.
- **Tropical Medicine:** Malaria, Dengue, and other travel-related pathologies.
- **Antimicrobial Stewardship (ASP):** Optimization of antibiotic use to prevent resistance.
- **Vaccination Center:** Adult and travel immunization schedules.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Sepsis Bundle:** 3-hour and 6-hour bundle compliance (Lactate, Blood Culture, Broad-spectrum antibiotics).
- **ASP Protocol:** De-escalation of antibiotics based on culture results (Sensitivity/Resistance).
- **KPIs:** Reduction in HAI rates, Antibiotic consumption (DDD), and Vaccination coverage.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Resistance Alerts:** Automatic alerts when a prescribed antibiotic is flagged as "Resistant" in the local antibiogram.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of HIV/Hepatitis status and other sensitive infectious data.
