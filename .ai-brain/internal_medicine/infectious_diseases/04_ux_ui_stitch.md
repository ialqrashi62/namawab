# 04_ux_ui_stitch.md - Infectious Diseases UI/UX Design
**Expert: Product Manager & UX Lead**

## 1. Design Philosophy: "The Pathogen Command Center"
Focus on rapid response, culture tracking, and antibiotic stewardship.

### A. The Infectious Disease Station
- **Layout:** 3-column fluid grid.
    - **Left:** Patient fever chart, current antibiotic/dose, and isolation status.
    - **Center:** Dynamic tabs for [Culture Results | ASP Review | Vaccination Log | AI Analysis].
    - **Right:** AI-Brain panel showing "Pathogen Match" and suggested narrow-spectrum alternatives.

## 2. Stitch Component Specifications
- **Alerts:** `Stitch-Alert-Critical` for "Resistant Pathogen" detections.
- **Charts:** `Stitch-Chart-Heatmap` for ward-level infection clusters.
- **Data Grid:** `Stitch-Data-Table-Premium` for sensitivity profiles (S/I/R).
