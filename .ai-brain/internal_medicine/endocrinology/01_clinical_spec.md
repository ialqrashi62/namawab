# 01_clinical_spec.md - Endocrinology & Diabetes Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Endocrinology Ecosystem
Management of hormonal imbalances and metabolic disorders.

### Specialized Units:
- **Diabetology:** Type 1, Type 2, and Gestational Diabetes.
- **Endocrine Glands:** Thyroid, Adrenal, and Pituitary disorders.
- **Metabolic Bone Disease:** Osteoporosis and Calcium metabolism.
- **Obesity Medicine:** Metabolic surgery and weight management.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **Diabetes Management:** HbA1c tracking $\rightarrow$ Insulin titration $\rightarrow$ Diabetic Foot screening.
- **Thyroid Protocol:** TSH/T4/T3 monitoring $\rightarrow$ Dosage adjustment $\rightarrow$ Ultrasound correlation.
- **KPIs:** Time-in-Range (TIR) for glucose, HbA1c reduction.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Hypoglycemia Alerts:** Critical low glucose alerts.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of metabolic/hormonal/genetic data.
