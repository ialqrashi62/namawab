# 01_clinical_spec.md - Gastroenterology & Hepatology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Gastroenterology Ecosystem
This module manages the digestive system, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas, with a heavy focus on advanced endoscopic interventions.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Gastroenterology:** Routine GI health, IBS, and IBD management.
- **Advanced Endoscopy:** 
    - **EUS (Endoscopic Ultrasound):** Staging of GI tumors and pancreatic cysts.
    - **ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography):** Gallstone removal and biliary stenting.
    - **Enteroscopy:** Small bowel imaging and biopsy.
- **Hepatology:** Cirrhosis, Hepatitis, and Liver Transplantation/Follow-up.
- **Pancreato-Biliary:** Pancreatitis management and pancreatic ductal/acinar pathology.
- **GI Motility:** Gastroparesis and esophageal motility studies.
- **Clinical Nutrition:** TPN/Enteral nutrition and metabolic support.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
### A. ERCP Protocol
- **KPI:** Success rate of biliary drainage and post-ERCP pancreatitis (PEP) rate.
- **Protocol:** Pre-op fasting $\rightarrow$ Endoscopic access $\rightarrow$ Sphincterotomy $\rightarrow$ Stenting $\rightarrow$ Post-op monitoring.
- **Required Data:** Sphincter status, stone size, stent diameter, and contrast/dye used.

### B. Hepatology (Cirrhosis)
- **Protocol:** Child-Pugh Scoring $\rightarrow$ MELD Score calculation $\rightarrow$ Varices screening.
- **Required Data:** Bilirubin, Albumin, INR, Creatinine, and Ascites grade.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support (CDS)
- **Automatic Scoring:** Integration of MELD (Model for End-Stage Liver Disease) and Child-Pugh scores.
- **Alerts:** Critical liver enzyme spikes or suspected bleeding during endoscopy.
- **Guideline Alignment:** ASGE (American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy) and EASL (European Association for the Study of the Liver).

## 4. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict sedation monitoring during advanced endoscopy.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of endoscopic imagery and biopsy reports.
