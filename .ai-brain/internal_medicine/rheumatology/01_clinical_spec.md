# 01_clinical_spec.md - Rheumatology & Immunology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Rheumatology Ecosystem
Management of autoimmune and inflammatory musculoskeletal diseases.

### Specialized Units:
- **Rheumatology:** RA, SLE, and Spondyloarthritis.
- **Clinical Immunology:** Primary immunodeficiencies and systemic autoimmunity.
- **Chronic Inflammatory Diseases:** Vasculitis and Sjogren's syndrome.
- **Allergy & Asthma:** Hypersensitivity and respiratory allergies.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
- **RA Management:** DAS28 scoring $\rightarrow$ DMARD titration $\rightarrow$ Joint erosion monitoring.
- **SLE Protocol:** SLEDAI scoring $\rightarrow$ Organ involvement check $\rightarrow$ Steroid tapering.
- **KPIs:** DAS28 reduction, ACR20/50/70 response rates.

## 3. CDS & Compliance
- **Drug Toxicity Alerts:** Monitoring for Methotrexate/Biologic side effects.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of autoimmune markers and genetic data.
