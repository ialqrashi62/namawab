# 01_clinical_spec.md - Pulmonology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Pulmonology Ecosystem
This module manages respiratory health, focusing on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), asthma, interstitial lung diseases, and sleep apnea.

### Specialized Units:
- **General Pulmonology:** Routine spirometry, asthma/COPD management.
- **Allergic Pulmonology:** Hypersensitivity pneumonitis and immune-mediated lung disease.
- **Sleep Medicine:** Polysomnography, CPAP/BiPAP titration, and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA).
- **Respiratory Care:** Mechanical ventilation, non-invasive ventilation (NIV), and oxygen therapy.
- **Bronchoscopy Unit:** Interventional bronchoscopy, BAL (Bronchoalveolar Lavage), and biopsy.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
### A. Sleep Medicine (Polysomnography)
- **KPI:** Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI) and Oxygen Desaturation Index (ODI).
- **Protocol:** Sleep study $\rightarrow$ AHI Calculation $\rightarrow$ CPAP Titration $\rightarrow$ Compliance Monitoring.

### B. Bronchoscopy
- **Protocol:** Pre-op sedation $\rightarrow$ Intubation $\rightarrow$ Specimen Collection $\rightarrow$ Post-op recovery.
- **Required Data:** Segmental location of biopsy, mucus quality, and sedation dosage.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support (CDS)
- **Spirometry Analysis:** Automatic interpretation of FEV1/FVC ratios.
- **Oxygen Therapy Alerts:** Alerts for hypoxic/hypercapnic respiratory failure.
- **Guideline Alignment:** GOLD (Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease) and GINA (Global Initiative for Asthma).

## 4. Compliance
- **JCI:** Strict monitoring of sedation levels during bronchoscopy.
- **PDPL:** Encryption of sleep study raw waveforms.
