# Go-to-Market — Cardiology

> **Owner:** PM
> **Date:** 2026-07-22
> **Scope:** Public-facing cardiology service promotion

---

## Target Audience

### Primary
- **Cardiology patients** (adults 18+ with CVD)
- **Cardiology outpatient referrals** (from PCPs, ER, other depts)
- **Cardiology inpatients** (already admitted)

### Secondary
- **Referring physicians** (need to know capabilities)
- **Insurance partners** (need CPT codes + outcomes)
- **Saudi MoH + CBAHI** (compliance + accreditation)

### Tertiary
- **Medical students + residents** (training program)
- **Research collaborators**
- **Conference speakers** (publications)

---

## Value Proposition

**"Comprehensive, evidence-based cardiac care with AI-assisted decision support — every patient, every shift, every guideline."**

### Key benefits
- **24/7 STEMI service** with door-to-balloon target <90 min
- **AI-assisted CDS** aligned with ESC + AHA + Saudi guidelines
- **Integrated workflow** from clinic → echo → cath → rehab
- **Multidisciplinary** (HF team, EP, anticoag, cardiac rehab)
- **CBAHI/JCI-accredited** care
