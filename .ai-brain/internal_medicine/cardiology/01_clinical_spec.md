# 01_clinical_spec.md - Cardiology Clinical Specifications
**Expert: Chief Medical Officer (CMO)**

## 1. Scope of Cardiology Ecosystem
This module covers the full spectrum of cardiovascular care, divided into specialized units:
- **General Cardiology:** Routine diagnostics, hypertension, and heart failure management.
- **Interventional Cardiology (Cath Lab):** PCI, TAVI, and hemodynamic monitoring.
- **Electrophysiology (EP):** Arrhythmia mapping, ablation, and pacemaker/ICD implantation.
- **Nuclear Cardiology:** SPECT, PET, and myocardial perfusion imaging.
- **Preventive Cardiology:** Lipid management and risk stratification.

## 2. Clinical Protocols & KPIs
### A. Interventional Cardiology (Cath Lab)
- **Door-to-Balloon (D2B) Time:** Target < 90 minutes for STEMI.
- **Protocol:** Standardized approach for coronary angiography $\rightarrow$ PCI $\rightarrow$ Post-op care.
- **Required Data:** Vessel diameter, stenosis %, balloon size, stent type, and contrast volume.

### B. Electrophysiology (EP)
- **Protocol:** Mapping of atrial/ventricular tachycardia $\rightarrow$ Ablation $\rightarrow$ Verification.
- **Required Data:** Activation maps, voltage/timing, and ablation energy parameters.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Requirements
- **Automatic Risk Scoring:** Integration of GRACE and TIMI scores for acute coronary syndromes.
- **Alerts:** Critical potassium/magnesium levels during EP procedures.
- **Guideline Alignment:** ESC (European Society of Cardiology) and ACC/AHA guidelines.

## 4. JCI & Compliance
- **Patient Safety Goals:** Correct site verification for catheterization.
- **Documentation:** Mandatory time-stamping for all critical interventions.
