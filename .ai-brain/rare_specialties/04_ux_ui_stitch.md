# 04 UX/UI Stitch — Rare Specialties & Orphan Diseases

## 1. Design Tokens
- **Palette**: rare disease awareness accents, calm neutrals.
- **Typography**: same as Stitch clinical stations.
- **Layout**: 3-column station.

## 2. Components
- `RareCaseForm`: phenotype, suspected condition, workup.
- `RegistryEnrollmentCard`: registry, consent, enrollment date.
- `MDTScheduler`: multidisciplinary meeting notes and decisions.
- `ProtocolTracker`: specialized treatment and monitoring.

## 3. User Stories
- "As a rare disease specialist, I want to enroll patients in registries and schedule MDTs."
- "As a geneticist, I want phenotype matching suggestions and specialist network links."

## 4. Wireframe Logic
- Left: rare case list + registry status.
- Center: case/MDT/protocol tabs.
- Right: alerts + specialist network + research links.

## 5. Accessibility
- RTL/LTR, clear consent status, touch-friendly forms.
