# 05 Compliance & Security — Rare Specialties & Orphan Diseases

## 1. Compliance
- Saudi MOH and international rare disease registry requirements.
- Research ethics and informed consent.
- Saudi PDPL for patient data.

## 2. Security
- `requireRole('rare_disease_specialist')`, `requireTenantScope`.
- Registry data encrypted and consent-tracked.

## 3. Audit
- Hash-chained audit for case creation, registry enrollment, MDT decisions, and protocol changes.

## 4. Safety Gates
- Specialist approval for protocol activation.
- Documented informed consent for registry/research.
- Drug safety monitoring for orphan therapies.

## 5. Data Retention
- Rare disease records retained per research/policy; audit logs 7+ years.
