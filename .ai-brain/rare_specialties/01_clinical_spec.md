# 01 Clinical Spec — Rare Specialties & Orphan Diseases

## 1. Clinical Domain & Scope
- **Focus**: Rare genetic, metabolic, immunologic, and orphan diseases requiring multidisciplinary care, registries, and specialized protocols.
- **Key Workflows**:
  - Rare disease registry enrollment.
  - Multidisciplinary case conferences.
  - Specialized treatment protocols and compassionate use.
  - Patient/family education and support.

## 2. Patient Journey
1. Suspicion/screening → 2. Specialist referral → 3. Diagnostic workup → 4. Registry enrollment → 5. Treatment → 6. Long-term follow-up.

## 3. Clinical Decision Support
- Phenotype/genotype matching suggestions.
- Specialist network recommendations.
- Treatment protocol alerts.

## 4. Integration Points
- Internal medicine subspecialties, genetics, laboratory, pharmacy.
- Research registries.

## 5. Safety Gates
- Specialist approval for rare disease protocol.
- Informed consent for registry and research.
- Drug safety monitoring for orphan therapies.
