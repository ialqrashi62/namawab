# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in NamaMedical a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, gender, gender identity, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion,
disability, or socioeconomic status — aligned with KSA values of mutual respect,
dignity, and professionalism.

## Our standards
Examples of positive behavior:
- Showing empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for patients and the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or insulting comments.
- Public or private attacks; political or religious targeting.
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of professional discussions.
- Any conduct unprofessional in a healthcare setting.

## Healthcare-specific
- Patient confidentiality is sacrosanct; never discuss real cases publicly.
- Disagreements about clinical decisions stay professional and patient-focused.
- Speak up about safety concerns without fear of retaliation.

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces (code, issues, PRs, chat, events) and to public spaces
when representing the project.

## Reporting
Report violations confidentially to **conduct@nama.local**. Reports are reviewed by
a designated committee of HR + Legal + Senior Engineering. Retaliation is prohibited.

## Enforcement
Possible actions: private warning → public warning → temporary ban → permanent ban
→ termination of employment / contractor relationship → legal action where warranted.
