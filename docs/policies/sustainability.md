# Sustainability / ESG Policy
v1.0 — aligned with Saudi Vision 2030 sustainability pillars

## Environmental
- KSA-region cloud infrastructure with vendor renewables targets.
- Right-sizing + autoscale to reduce idle compute.
- Quarterly review of cluster utilization; decommission unused workloads.
- Green-friendly defaults: dark mode (lower screen energy), efficient image formats.
- Hardware refresh: prefer refurbished where suitable; recycling certificate required.
- Print minimization: digital-first workflows by design.

## Social
- Health equity: bilingual AR/EN; accessibility WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Affordable patient access (no paywalls on essentials).
- Workforce wellness: EAP, fair shift scheduling, burnout monitoring.
- Saudization: meet/exceed Nitaqat.
- Vendor diversity: opportunities for SME suppliers.
- Patient education: free, evidence-based, AR-first.

## Governance
- Executive sponsor for ESG.
- Annual ESG report aligned with KSA disclosures.
- Anti-bribery, COI, whistleblower policies (separate docs).
- Board-level ethics committee oversight.
- Independent audit yearly.

## Targets (illustrative)
- Carbon footprint per active facility ↓ 10% YoY.
- Workforce satisfaction ≥ 75% YoY.
- Saudization above Nitaqat platinum.
- Patient access in remote regions (telemedicine adoption).

## Reporting
- Annual ESG snapshot in Executive board pack.
- Public summary published.
