# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
v1.0 — 2026-05-13 — Applies to all NamaMedical users

## 1. Scope
Applies to clinicians, staff, contractors, vendors, students, and any user with access
to NamaMedical systems or PHI.

## 2. Permitted use
- Use NamaMedical only for authorized clinical, administrative, educational, or research
  duties.
- Access only the **minimum necessary** PHI to perform your duty.
- Use the language of the patient when communicating; respect cultural norms.

## 3. Prohibited use
You will NOT:
- Share credentials; use another user's account; bypass MFA.
- Access records of family/colleagues/celebrities/yourself for non-clinical reasons.
- Export PHI to personal email/cloud/USB.
- Take screenshots/photos of PHI for personal devices.
- Discuss patients in public spaces or on personal social media.
- Install unauthorized software on workstations.
- Use AI co-pilot to bypass safety guardrails or to generate content unrelated to care.
- Tamper with audit logs or attempt to evade monitoring.
- Use systems for personal commercial benefit, harassment, or any unlawful purpose.

## 4. Email / messaging
- Use facility email for work; do not forward PHI to personal accounts.
- Encrypt outbound PHI to external recipients via approved channels only.
- Beware of phishing; report suspicious messages to security@nama.local.

## 5. Mobile devices
- Use only enrolled devices with MDM.
- Lock screens within 2 minutes; biometric or PIN required.
- Lost/stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour.

## 6. Social media
- Do not post PHI, identifiable photos, or facility-internal information.
- Personal opinions are personal — do not represent the facility without authorization.

## 7. AI assistant usage
- AI is **decision-support**; clinician remains accountable.
- Do not paste real PHI into external chatbots; use only the embedded NamaMedical AI.
- Do not "jailbreak" prompts or attempt prompt injection.

## 8. Reporting
- Suspected breach, unauthorized access, lost device, or policy violation:
  security@nama.local + immediate supervisor within **1 hour**.
- Whistleblower protection per facility policy.

## 9. Sanctions
- Violations may result in: warning, mandatory retraining, suspension of access,
  termination of employment, and legal action including criminal liability under PDPL.

## 10. Acknowledgment
All users sign this AUP at onboarding and annually. Electronic signature recorded with
timestamp and user ID.
