# Patient FAQ

## 1. Account & Access

**Q: How do I create a NamaMedical account?**
A: Download the app, tap "Sign up", verify your identity via Yaqeen with national ID + OTP. Your account is auto-linked to your medical record if you've visited before.

**Q: Forgot password.**
A: Tap "Forgot password", enter your ID, an OTP will arrive. If verification fails, contact support at 937 or patient-rights@nama.local.

**Q: Can my family share one account?**
A: No — each patient has their own account by national ID. Guardians can manage minor/dependent accounts via "Linked accounts".

## 2. Appointments

**Q: How do I book?**
A: Open Appointments → choose clinic, doctor, time. Confirmation arrives via SMS + Sehhaty. You can also book via the Mawid app.

**Q: How do I cancel/reschedule?**
A: From appointment details → "Cancel" or "Reschedule". Please do so 24 h ahead so others can use the slot.

**Q: I didn't receive a reminder.**
A: Check that notifications are enabled and your phone is current. Reminders go out 24 h and 2 h before.

## 3. Results & Medical Record

**Q: When do my lab/imaging results appear?**
A: Automatically once finalized — usually within 24 h. Critical values may trigger a phone call before publishing.

**Q: I don't understand a result.**
A: Tap the result for a plain-language explanation (when available). For clinical discussion, book a follow-up.

**Q: Can I get a copy of my record?**
A: Yes — Medical Record → Export. Secure PDF download. For formal transfer to another facility, contact Patient Relations.

## 4. Medications & Prescriptions

**Q: How do I dispense my prescription?**
A: Prescriptions are recorded in Wasfaty. Visit any participating pharmacy; the pharmacist will find your Rx by your national ID.

**Q: My medication ran out and I need a refill.**
A: From Current Medications → "Request refill". Your doctor receives the request and may need a review first.

**Q: A drug-interaction warning appeared.**
A: We screen interactions automatically. If a warning appears, consult your doctor or pharmacist before combining medications.

## 5. Bills & Insurance

**Q: My bill has items I don't recognize.**
A: Review bill details. To dispute, tap "Report billing error" within 30 days.

**Q: Insurance denied my claim.**
A: The reason appears under "Claims" with recommendations. You can request a re-submission with extra documentation.

**Q: What about ZATCA e-invoicing?**
A: Yes — every bill is a compliant e-invoice with a QR code. Keep a copy for insurance.

## 6. AI

**Q: Does AI diagnose me?**
A: No. AI **assists** clinicians only. The final medical decision belongs to your treating physician. You may request human-only review.

**Q: Is my data sent abroad?**
A: No. Your health data is processed inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (data residency). Identifiers are removed before any AI processing.

## 7. Privacy & Rights

**Q: Who has accessed my record?**
A: Access Log shows everyone who viewed your data and when. Contact DPO (dpo@nama.local) for inquiries.

**Q: I want my data deleted.**
A: You have the right to request deletion within clinical/legal limits. Submit via "My Rights" or dpo@nama.local. Response within 30 working days.

**Q: How do I file a complaint?**
A: Via "Complaints" or patient-rights@nama.local. Response within 15 working days; will not affect your care.

## 8. Emergencies

**Q: Can I use the app in emergencies?**
A: No. **Call 997 immediately** or go to the nearest ED. The app is not a substitute for emergency services.
