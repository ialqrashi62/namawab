# Privacy Policy
**NamaMedical Hospital Information System**
Version 1.0 — 2026-05-13 — Region: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

---

## 1. Introduction
We are committed to protecting your personal and health data in compliance with the
**KSA Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)**, regulations issued by the **Saudi Data &
AI Authority (SDAIA)**, and **CBAHI** and **MoH eHealth** standards.

## 2. Data Controller
- Entity: NamaMedical
- Representative: Data Protection Officer (DPO)
- Email: dpo@nama.local
- Address: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

## 3. Categories of Data We Collect
1. **Identification**: name, national/iqama ID, DOB, sex, nationality.
2. **Contact**: phone, email, address.
3. **Sensitive health data**: medical history, diagnoses, medications, lab/imaging
   results, surgical history, vaccinations, vital signs.
4. **Financial & insurance**: insurer, policy number, invoices, payments.
5. **Usage data**: login timestamps, technical logs, IP when required for security.
6. **AI data**: redacted prompts/responses, confidence scores, recommendations.

## 4. Lawful Bases
- Explicit consent (optional services, marketing).
- Performance of treatment contract.
- Legal obligation (MoH registries, CBAHI reporting, ZATCA invoicing, communicable disease reporting).
- Vital interests in emergencies.
- Legitimate interest for quality, audit, and platform security.

## 5. Purposes
- Provide healthcare and maintain your medical record.
- Billing and insurance claims (NPHIES).
- Appointment, result, and follow-up communications.
- Quality, accreditation, and internal audit.
- Approved scientific research under IRB oversight using **de-identified data**.
- Assist clinicians with **advisory-only** AI tools — final decision belongs to the physician.
- Cybersecurity, fraud detection, platform protection.

## 6. Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. Sharing is limited to:
- **Other healthcare providers** within your care path.
- **Insurers** via the **NPHIES** platform.
- **Government bodies** (MoH, SFDA, CBAHI, Civil Defense) as legally required.
- **Service providers** under signed Data Processing Agreements.
- **Court orders** or legal mandates.

## 7. Storage & Retention
- All health data is stored within **the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia** (data residency).
- Retention:
  - Adult medical records: **10 years** after last contact.
  - Minor records: **until age 21 + 5 additional years**.
  - Radiation/pathology records: **30 years**.
  - Blood bank/donor records: **30 years**.
  - IVF/cryopreservation: per renewed consent.
- After expiry: secure destruction or final de-identification per approved policy.

## 8. Your Rights
Per PDPL, you may:
1. Access your data and obtain a copy.
2. Correct inaccurate data.
3. Erase data within clinical/legal limits.
4. Restrict or object to processing.
5. Withdraw consent for optional services (no retroactive effect).
6. Port your data to another provider.
7. Not be subject to fully automated decisions of legal/health significance without
   human review.
8. Lodge a complaint with SDAIA.

To exercise rights: dpo@nama.local — response within **30 working days**.

## 9. Breach Notification
If a breach likely affects your data, we notify **SDAIA within 72 hours** and notify
you directly when impact is high.

## 10. Artificial Intelligence
- We use AI to assist clinicians (ECG interpretation, imaging triage, sepsis alerts…).
- All outputs are **advisory only**; the treating physician makes the final decision.
- We redact personal identifiers before sending data to large language models.
- Every AI invocation is logged (who, when, confidence, output).
- You may request **human review** of any AI recommendation that affected your care.

## 11. Cookies
Technical cookies only for session and security. No tracking/advertising cookies.

## 12. Minors
We do not collect minor data without **parental/guardian consent**. Minor records have
stricter access controls and independent audits.

## 13. Updates
Material changes are notified at least **30 days** before they take effect.
