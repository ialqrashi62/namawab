# Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy
v1.0 — Aligned with KSA Anti-Corruption Law (Nazaha) + ISO 37001

## 1. Position
Zero tolerance for bribery in any form. Anyone offering or accepting bribes will face
termination + reporting to authorities.

## 2. Prohibited
- Cash, "facilitation payments", lavish gifts, hospitality beyond modest courtesy.
- Kickbacks from vendors, suppliers, recruiters, referral sources.
- Bribery of public officials (KSA + foreign).
- Indirect bribery via consultants, intermediaries, or family.
- Conflict-of-interest concealment.

## 3. Acceptable
- Promotional items of nominal value (branded pen, calendar) ≤ 200 SAR.
- Modest meals during legitimate business meetings.
- Charitable donations approved by Compliance + made transparently.
- Standard discounts negotiated through procurement.

## 4. Gifts/hospitality limits
- Single item / occasion: ≤ 500 SAR.
- Annual aggregate from any single source: ≤ 2000 SAR.
- Anything above must be declared and may need return or auction for charity.

## 5. Vendor interactions
- All purchases through procurement with competitive bidding above 50K SAR.
- DPA + anti-bribery clause in all contracts.
- Annual vendor compliance attestation.

## 6. Pharmaceutical & device industry
- No personal gifts from pharma/device companies.
- CME sponsorship disclosed and goes through formal process.
- Speaker fees declared in conflict-of-interest registry.
- Free drug samples handled per pharmacy policy (logged).

## 7. Reporting
- Suspected violations → Whistleblower channels (`whistleblower.md`).
- Compliance team investigates with Legal.

## 8. Sanctions
- Termination, civil action, criminal referral to Nazaha.
- Vendor blacklisting.

## 9. Training
- Mandatory annual module for all staff and new hires.
- Procurement + Executive: enhanced annual training.

## 10. Records
- Conflict-of-interest registry.
- Gifts/hospitality log.
- Vendor due-diligence files.
- Audit annually by internal audit; independent audit every 3 y.
