# Whistleblower Policy
v1.0 — Owner: CEO + Legal — Reviewed annually

## 1. Purpose
Encourage and protect anyone who reports — in good faith — wrongdoing, illegal acts, or
serious risks to patient safety, security, finance, ethics, or compliance.

## 2. Scope
Applies to: employees, contractors, vendors, and patients.

## 3. What to report
- Patient safety threats (near-misses, sentinel events, intimidation around safety).
- Fraud, bribery, conflict-of-interest abuses.
- Privacy/security breaches not properly disclosed.
- Discrimination, harassment.
- Regulatory violations (PDPL, CBAHI, MoH, SFDA, IAEA).
- Falsification of medical records or research data.

## 4. How to report
- **Internal**: ethics@nama.local (Ethics Committee).
- **Anonymous external** (independent vendor): tipline.nama.local
- **Confidential phone**: shared with the independent vendor; logs sealed.

Reports should include facts, dates, individuals involved, supporting documents if any.

## 5. Confidentiality
- Identity of reporters protected; disclosed only on a strict need-to-know.
- Anonymous reports accepted and investigated.

## 6. Non-retaliation
- Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a serious violation, leading to
  disciplinary action up to termination + legal consequences.
- Even if the report turns out unfounded — provided it was made in good faith — the
  reporter is fully protected.

## 7. Investigation
- Acknowledged within 5 working days.
- Independent investigator assigned (depending on subject).
- Findings + actions reported back to reporter (when not anonymous) within 60 days.
- Substantiated cases lead to corrective + disciplinary actions.

## 8. False reports
- Knowingly false reports are themselves disciplinary offenses.

## 9. Records
- Cases recorded in a sealed register; access restricted.
- Anonymized aggregate metrics reported to the Board annually.

## 10. Communication
- Posters in staff areas (AR + EN) with QR to tipline.
- Annual training reminder.
- Onboarding mention for all new hires.
