# Conflict of Interest (COI) Policy
v1.0

## 1. Purpose
Prevent personal interests from influencing decisions made on behalf of NamaMedical or
patients. Required for clinical safety, regulatory trust, and public confidence.

## 2. Examples of COI
- Financial interest in vendors, pharma/device companies, or competing facilities.
- Family relations in supplier or recruitment chains.
- Outside paid roles (consulting, speaking, board seats).
- Research grants from companies whose products you can prescribe/refer.
- Personal benefit from referrals (kickbacks, marketing fees).

## 3. Disclosure
- All clinical staff and management complete an annual COI declaration.
- New COI declared within 14 days.
- Centralized registry maintained by Compliance; audit-ready.

## 4. Management
- Disclosure ≠ approval. Compliance decides on:
  - **No action** (declared, no impact)
  - **Mitigation** (recuse from specific decisions)
  - **Restriction** (cannot prescribe certain products / refer to certain entities)
  - **Termination of conflict** (divest, resign outside role)

## 5. Research COI
- Disclose to IRB during protocol submission.
- Sponsored research subject to additional firewalls (independent monitor, blinded analysis).

## 6. Procurement
- Anyone with COI cannot vote on the related vendor selection.
- Quorum without conflicted member required.

## 7. Patient interactions
- If a clinician has a COI affecting a recommendation, they must disclose to the patient
  and offer a conflict-free alternative or referral.

## 8. Sanctions
- Failure to disclose: disciplinary action up to termination.
- Use of role for personal gain: termination + legal action + Nazaha referral.

## 9. Records
- Annual declaration retention 7 y.
- Mitigation plans archived alongside.
- Reviewed by internal audit yearly; independent every 3 y.
